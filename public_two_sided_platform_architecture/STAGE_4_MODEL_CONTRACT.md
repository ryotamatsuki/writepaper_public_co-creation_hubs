# Stage 4R-TP Model Contract

Stage 4 must use the selected architecture without adding mechanisms.

## Must solve

1. project cutoff ordering and participation regimes;
2. partner participation fixed point;
3. private demand and `p_T^*(x_1,x_2)`;
4. B1, B2, B3, B4, G;
5. public FOCs/SOCs/KKT in the relevant regimes;
6. symmetric SPNE and any natural asymmetric equilibria;
7. regional Nash vs national planner;
8. non-use and rival-public access;
9. counterexamples and open-set robustness.

## Primary hard kill

P2-R: compare B3 and G. A pass requires a qualitative difference—sign/threshold/ranking/multiplicity/welfare-ordering—not a level shift.

## Essentiality hard kills

- beta=0 must materially alter the P2 theorem;
- remove H2 must remove the public-public theorem;
- fix pT must remove the full-game channel;
- coordinated public objective must materially alter the regional result.

## Prohibited rescue

No dynamics, private quality, public prices, extra private fee, bargaining, relocation, matching technology, logit shocks, arbitrary interaction terms, or additional heterogeneity solely to manufacture a reversal.

Stage 4 failure on qualitative P2-R is a NO-GO for this platform architecture.
