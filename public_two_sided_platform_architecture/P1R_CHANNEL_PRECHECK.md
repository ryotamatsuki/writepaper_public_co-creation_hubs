# P1-R Channel Precheck

Regional Nash and a national planner internalize different incidence objects.

Each `x_i` raises partner participation at `H_i`, affects resident and nonresident project sorting, changes private demand, and changes resident partner surplus. The planner additionally internalizes the cross-region project and partner effects plus private profit.

A network multiplier can therefore alter the Nash-planner wedge. However generic network-driven over-/underinvestment reversals are prior-art threatened.

Stage 3 status: **secondary / unresolved**. Stage 4 may retain P1-R only if the threshold explicitly contains regional-incidence terms that disappear under coordinated public optimization and are not already present in B2.
