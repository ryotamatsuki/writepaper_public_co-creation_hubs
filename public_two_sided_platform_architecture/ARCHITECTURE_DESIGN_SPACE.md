# Architecture Design Space

Exactly ten candidate architectures were compared.

The design dimensions were: side anchoring, single-/multi-homing, source of endogenous non-use, public investment incidence, private pricing side, regional welfare incidence, B1–B4 nesting, and the P2-R feedback channel.

The design was deliberately restricted to three strategic controls: `x_1,x_2,p_T`. No dynamic state, private quality, bargaining, relocation, public pricing, or arbitrary interaction term was permitted.

The selected model uses one productive heterogeneity dimension on the project side (`z`) and one real participation-cost dimension on the partner side (`c`). Geography enters only through fixed real access costs and is not the contribution.
