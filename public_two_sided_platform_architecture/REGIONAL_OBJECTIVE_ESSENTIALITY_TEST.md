# Regional Objective Essentiality Test

`G_i` counts surplus of region-i resident projects and partners and bears only its own public investment cost. A coordinated planner sums both regions and private profit.

Regional objectives therefore omit benefits/costs imposed on nonresidents and treat private fee payments as regional outflows.

Replacing `W_1,W_2` with a single coordinated public objective removes the decentralized public-public best-response system. This makes regional incidence structurally relevant, but regional welfare maximization itself is not novel.

Stage 4 must show that regional incidence affects the P2-R sign/threshold, not merely the level of welfare.
