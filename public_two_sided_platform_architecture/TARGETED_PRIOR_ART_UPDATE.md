# Targeted Prior-Art Update

Stage 3 did not repeat Stage 2. It checked only substructures introduced by the shortlisted architectures.

- Belleflamme & Peitz, *Platform competition: Who benefits from multihoming?*, IJIO 64 (2019), DOI 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2018.03.014: one-side multihoming is established and cannot be a contribution.
- Jeitschko & Tremblay, *Platform Competition with Endogenous Homing*, International Economic Review (2020), DOI 10.1111/iere.12457: endogenous single-/multi-homing and exit are established.
- Dou, He & Xu, *One-side value-added service investment and pricing strategies for a two-sided platform*, IJPR 54(13) (2016), DOI 10.1080/00207543.2016.1148275: one-side platform investment is established.
- Kim (2024), DOI 10.1111/manc.12467: public/private mixed two-sided platform competition and investment are established.
- Liu, Reshidi & Rivadeneyra (2026), DOI 10.34989/swp-2026-10: strongest B1 threat; public/private participation and private fee response are established.

No targeted search changed the Stage 2 conclusion that P2-R remains the only serious full-game target. Candidate C's homing and investment primitives are explicitly treated as known components.
