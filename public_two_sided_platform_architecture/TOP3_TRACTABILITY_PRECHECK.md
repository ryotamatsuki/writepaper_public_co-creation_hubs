# TOP3 Tractability Precheck

C has the cleanest low-dimensional system.

For partner costs `c~U[0,1]`, interior partner masses are linear in platform project mass. For projects `z~U[0,1]`, platform utilities are affine in `z`; pairwise cutoffs are therefore closed form conditional on partner masses.

Generic pairwise project cutoff between h and k for resident r:

`z_hk^r = [(kappa_rh+p_h)-(kappa_rk+p_k)] / [alpha(n_h^P-n_k^P)]`

when the denominator is nonzero; outside cutoffs are

`z_h0^r=(kappa_rh+p_h)/(v+alpha n_h^P)`.

The full participation equilibrium remains piecewise because cutoff order can change, but the state dimension is three platform project masses plus three partner masses and one price, with the partner block substitutable analytically.

E requires another partner sorting cutoff. I is similarly tractable but relies on segmented pools. C is therefore preferred.
