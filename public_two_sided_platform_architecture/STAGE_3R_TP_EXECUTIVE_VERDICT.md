# Stage 3R-TP — Executive Verdict

## Verdict

**GO — MINIMAL PUBLIC TWO-SIDED PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE SELECTED**

Ten architectures were screened under the Stage 2R-TP survival contract. Seven were hard-killed in the first screen. TOP3 were C, E, and I. Deep dive rejected E and I relative to C, leaving one preferred baseline.

Selected architecture: **Project Single-Homing × Partner Multi-Homing with Partner-Side Public Facilitation**.

Projects are regionally resident, single-home across `0,H1,H2,HT`, and heterogeneous in productive project value `z`. Partners form a common support side, may multi-home, and face a real per-platform participation cost `c`. Public investment `x_i` raises partner-side participation value on `H_i`; the private platform charges one scalar fee `p_T` to projects only.

The key Stage 4 object is still P2-R. In the selected model the firm-to-partner feedback makes platform partner mass endogenous, while endogenous private pricing creates terms in the rival government's marginal incentive that are absent when `p_T` is fixed. Stage 3 establishes a nonzero channel and literal B1–B4 nesting, not the desired qualitative theorem.

Stage 4R-TP is authorized to solve and hard-kill the full game.
