# Regional Welfare Candidates

W-A (resident projects + resident partners) is the clean baseline.

Selected accounting:

`W_i = resident-project surplus + resident-partner surplus across platforms - gamma x_i^2/2`.

Projects retain region of residence whichever platform they use. Partner population is nationally accessible but can be partitioned by residence; symmetry gives each region its resident share of partner surplus. Private fees paid by resident projects are regional outflows and therefore reduce `W_i`.

National welfare sums `W_1+W_2` and private profit, so `p_T n_T^F` cancels as a transfer, subject to any real private operating cost.

W-B and W-C were retained as Stage 4 counterfactual accounting checks, not selected because they introduce platform-location incidence unnecessary for P2-R.
