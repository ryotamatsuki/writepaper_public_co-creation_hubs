# P2-R Channel Precheck

Within an interior project-choice regime, project cutoffs are rational functions of `n_h^P` and `p_T`. Partner masses satisfy the linear interior equations

`n_i^P=rho+x_i+beta n_i^F`,

`n_T^P=rho_T+beta n_T^F`.

Private profit is

`Pi_T=p_T D_T(p_T;x_1,x_2)`.

An interior private optimum satisfies

`D_T+p_T D_{T,p}=0`,

so by the implicit-function theorem

`p^*_{x_1}=-(D_{T,x_1}+p_T D_{T,px_1})/(2D_{T,p}+p_T D_{T,pp})`.

This derivative is generically nonzero because `x_1` changes `n_1^P`, project sorting, `n_T^F`, and therefore `n_T^P`.

For government 2,

`dW_2~/dx_2 = W_{2,x_2}+W_{2,p}p^*_{x_2}`.

Its cross derivative contains terms absent in B3, including

`W_{2,x_2p} p^*_{x_1}`

and the corresponding endogenous-participation terms. Thus fixing `p_T` removes an entire feedback channel, not merely a coefficient.

Stage 3 result: **channel nonzero in general; qualitative sign/ranking effect unproven and reserved for Stage 4**.
