# Private Pricing Side Test

Three one-price variants were considered.

## Pricing F — selected

`p_T` is charged to project/firm users only. Partner access is free. This gives a direct private demand and matches the policy question of firms choosing an intermediary.

## Pricing P

Charging partners weakens the direct project-choice interpretation and makes the private price response less transparent.

## Pricing FP

A single bundled price affecting both sides lacks a clean incidence interpretation.

## Decision

Select **Pricing F**. Do not introduce separate `p_T^F,p_T^P` in Stage 4.
