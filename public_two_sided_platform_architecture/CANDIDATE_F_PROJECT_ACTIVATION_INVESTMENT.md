# Candidate F — Project Activation Investment

Public `x_i` lowers participation costs or raises onboarding value on the project side, generating `x_i -> n_i^F -> n_i^P`.

The architecture is tractable and supports non-use, but it pushes the public control directly onto the side that determines platform demand. This increases the risk that P2-R becomes ordinary demand-enhancing quality competition with a two-sided multiplier.
