## Candidate mechanism
Pilot breadth n affects effort e and spillover delta; delta changes entrant profits and entry decision; private entry can be socially excessive; government may optimally narrow program scope to prevent socially costly entry even if direct pilot impact H_2>H_1.

## Strict novelty verdict
NOT CERTIFIED. Discrete endogenous entry causing policy non-monotonicity may be a straightforward composition of standard over-entry theory and reduced-form spillovers. Applying existing mechanisms to public hubs does not suffice.

## Required ablations
A. Kill investment channel (fix e or delta) and see if main nonmonotonicity persists.
B. Kill matching-scope channel (fix n), permit exogenous public spillover. Test whether candidate result is identical.
C. Fix private entry or set F_P=0; check whether candidate welfare reversal still exists.
D. Remove quadratic location preferences; see if exact policy switch is just the discrete entry threshold.
E. Replace public establishment with a subsidy or contract that induces the same spillover; identify when direct provision is strictly necessary.
F. Relax one-private-entrant assumption, add multiple potential entrants and endogenous hub locations.
G. Obtain actual Nash bargaining with relationship-specific investment. Determine whether reduced-form r_n changes the economic mechanism.

## Publication gates
- Gate 1: Complete baseline equilibrium derivations, including monopoly outside option and global deviations.
- Gate 2: Prove equilibrium existence / uniqueness or carefully delineate multiplicity and selection.
- Gate 3: Recover 1991 spatial benchmark in the no-experimentation limit.
- Gate 4: Demonstrate a proposition that fails in direct predecessors and survives stripping public-hub terminology.
- Gate 5: Show that direct public provision is not weakly dominated by subsidy, procurement or digital facilitation under the same resource costs.
- Gate 6: Independent replication, sensitivity, source provenance and referee-style rejection attempt.
Do not write a publishable-main-text novelty claim until Gates 1–4 pass.
