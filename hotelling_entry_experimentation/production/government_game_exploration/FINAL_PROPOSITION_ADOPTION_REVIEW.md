## Recommended exact lemma (no new economic assumptions)

**Lemma S1 (Strict private-entry dominance and institutional reduction).** Assume uniform skill/premium taste `theta_i~U[0,1]`, `Delta_i>0`, local basic utility `u_i(s_j)>0`, and nonnegative match-return coefficients `a_i^m,b_i^m>=0`. Assume fee/participation continuation equilibria exist for any first-stage profile. If
```
F_{P_i} < N_i*Delta_i/4
```
then the private provider's stage-one entry is strictly profitable whatever the other three stage-one actors choose, even allowing the opposite region's founder participation to respond endogenously. If the inequality holds in both regions, **every** stage-one mixed or pure SPNE has both private providers active with probability one; only `(P,P)`, `(B,P)`, `(P,B)`, `(B,B)` can be supported in first-stage equilibrium.

*Proof.* With own free basic government service active, the private specialist's premium upgrade demand is `1-f_i/Delta_i` for `0<=f_i<=Delta_i`; therefore the global optimal gross fee receipt is exactly `N_i*Delta_i/4`. Without own free government basic service, the specialist can always choose `f_i=u_i(s_j)+Delta_i/2`. Every type `theta_i>=1/2` then obtains nonnegative utility, since matching returns are nonnegative regardless of the other region's equilibrium reaction. Hence gross receipt at the private fee Nash is at least `N_i*(u_i(s_j)+Delta_i/2)/2 > N_i*Delta_i/4`. In both cases the net entry profit is positive under the stated condition. □

**Corollary S1.** If the fixed costs of both private providers obey S1, the equilibrium first-stage institution game can be exactly reduced to two simultaneous binary public-availability decisions, **without exogenously assuming private entry**. This says nothing about the uniqueness of the resulting two-government equilibrium, and P/P fee uniqueness still needs to be separately proved where used.

Original reference numbers are `N_A=3, Delta_A=1, F_PA=.65<.75`, `N_B=1, Delta_B=1, F_PB=.24<.25`. In current PR #61, adding the lemma makes the baseline 16-profile proof more transparent; **do not delete** the existing checks (they certify more, including planner comparisons, and guard against mistakes).

Source of model proof: PR #62 `government_game_certificate.py`, PR #65 `entry_cost_phase_diagram.py`. Literature priority guard: Zennyo (2020) already derives low fixed-cost premium adoption dominance under a different, ad-financed two-private-platform market model. A standard low-fixed-cost dominance principle is **not** a new theoretical contribution.
