\section{A calibrated equilibrium illustration}\label{sec:numerical}
We report a documented baseline \emph{existence illustration}, not an unrestricted comparative-statics theorem. The model's Cournot and probability admissibility conditions and the Stage~4/4A fee-continuation conditions are held fixed. The baseline has $(n_A,n_B)=(5,2)$, $(N_A,N_B)=(3,1)$, $\Delta_A=\Delta_B=1$, $\rho=0.4$, $\mu=0.25$, $(\omega_A,\omega_B)=(0.8,0.2)$, $(F_{G_A},F_{G_B})=(0.09,0.0405)$, $(F_{P_A},F_{P_B})=(0.65,0.24)$, $p_0=0.05$, $\zeta=0.005$, $k=0.0004$, and $\chi=0.9$. Basic direct-service values are generated by $(v_A,v_B)=(0.15,0.10)$ and $(w_A,w_B)=(0.22,0.45)$. The exact Cournot demand and marginal-cost normalisation is archived in the frozen Stage~33 source and the Stage~9 regression script.

The comparison isolates \emph{composition}, rather than attributing a different number of firms or different product-market technology to the counterfactual. In the same calibration, the fully subscribed $(B,B)$ welfare value is $2.407438368575148$ under both success specifications at $\lambda=0.5$, while the selected private-only membership composition and region-$B$ government's incremental incentives differ.

For the skill-complementary specification at $\lambda=0.5$, a region-$B$ private-only provider facing full co-provision in $A$ charges approximately $f_B=0.6410074280605$, with $m_B=0.639945174957$ and $q_B=0.435180261481$. Adding $G_B$ generates a local welfare increment approximately $0.00003167010245$. In the homogenised comparison the same conditional public addition yields a local change approximately $-0.00045677076684$. The government fixed cost is positioned between the respective conditional welfare thresholds. These are small, calibration-specific margins: they demand faithful numerical reproduction and cannot justify universal claims.

In the skill-complementary baseline, the local region-$B$ public-addition margin crosses zero at $\lambda^\star\simeq0.3511840318401$ on $[0,0.5]$. At this exact threshold the local government's binary best response may tie, hence numerical uniqueness claims must be restricted to strict-deviation parameter values away from the boundary. There are other admissible parameter adjustments with multiple first-stage equilibria. The sixteen-profile enumeration and the independent regression script are mandatory complements to this table.

\section{Robustness and limitations}\label{sec:robustness}
The frozen negative portability checks matter for interpretation. Replacing independent uniform ability types with a $\operatorname{Beta}(2,2)$ ability distribution, or replacing differentiated Cournot with differentiated Bertrand product-market competition (and the corresponding change in demand substitution), does \emph{not} reproduce the same complete four-entrant ownership switch at the fixed baseline costs. These are documented adverse numerical checks, not general impossibility theorems for either alternative environment.

The identifying economic assumption of the sorting channel is that founders' valuations for private services and their productive capabilities are positively linked. Their equality in the baseline is strong. Independent skill and premium taste may weaken or eliminate the channel; this paper does not prove a sign under unrestricted correlation. Further, the model leaves subsidy and procurement substitutes outside the decision set, does not endogenise a physical-site technology or taxes, and treats each matched venture as having its own product niche. Therefore its policy implication concerns \emph{when a publicly financed service may be jointly supplied with a fee-based option}, not a blanket recommendation to construct government-owned buildings.
