Double-blind peer review; all manuscript and **code/repository reviewer-facing links need anonymisation**. Data/code materials required to support replication. ## D. Journal of Public Economic Theory — stretch, not first
- Current aims https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14679779/homepage/productinformation.html
- Current exact author instructions https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14679779/homepage/forauthors.html
- Closest article **Lee (2026), published July 26**, *Knowledge Spillover Network and Optimal R&D Policy in Oligopoly*, https://doi.org/10.1111/jpet.70128 (original article accessible online; official publisher).
- Very strong match for local spillovers, governmental interventions, Cournot and welfare signs. However, Lee's paper derives a general optimal R&D subsidy/tax threshold, implements symmetric second best and checks complete bipartite, core-periphery, scale-free and spatial networks. PR69's new proposition is a conditional P-to-B comparison with full partner coverage, one ability distribution and weak matching asymptotics, plus a frozen finite-`gamma` rectangle; it **does not** solve second-best instrument design, or certify its sign across alternative distributions. This is a specific **editorial problem-completeness and generality mismatch**. Classification **STRETCH/HIGH-VERY HIGH risk**, not first for this exact manuscript.
- **Costs explicit:** JPET author page says **no submission fees**, and optional OA APC only, with subscription publication available; hard zero-cost path VERIFIED. Free format, Math appendix, code/data statement, AI disclosure. Current page requires structured abstract plus Research Exchange submission and single-anonymized reviews. Not yet journal-formatted.
