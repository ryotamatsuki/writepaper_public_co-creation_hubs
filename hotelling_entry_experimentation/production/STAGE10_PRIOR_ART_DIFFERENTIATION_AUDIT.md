# Stage 10 — Closest prior art versus this model: explicit claim-by-claim differentiation

**Date:** 2026-10-28 JST. **Scope:** `STAGE33-2026-10-28-THEORY-FREEZE-v1`, NOT the unrelated public two-sided-platform model.
**Stage:** manuscript exposition and source audit. **Novelty clearance:** bounded model-specific GO only; not universal priority certification.

## One-paragraph contrast for a referee

We do **not** claim to discover free public versus paid private competition, sorting on willingness to pay, collaborative knowledge heterogeneity, R&D with downstream Cournot, local public-good spillovers, or entry inefficiency. The joint object is **four independent binary public/private provision choices in two regions** where private posted fees determine both firm participation `m_i` and an unnormalised technical ability moment `q_i`; cross-region pairs then create differentiated products, affecting founders' commercial profits and two region-specific incumbent/consumer-surplus accounts. We derive restricted **conditional** fee, local and national public-addition thresholds and enumerate a baseline 16-profile numerical witness of entry-ownership change. This conjunction distinguishes the model from the *examined* predecessors taken individually; it is **not** evidence no more general predecessor theorem absorbs it.

## Three defensible manuscript claims

1. **Mechanism synthesis:** Pricing selects both volume `m` and founder-ability first moments `q`, which affect cross-region joint commercialisation probabilities, not just match counts.
2. **Conditional analytic incidence:** The foreign part of the local-vs-national public coentry threshold is `C*ell_i*(S_j^0+lambda*K_j*ell_i/4)`. Sign unrestricted.
3. **Bounded existence illustration:** Different decentralised provider configurations under selected admissible standard-vs-homogenised skill success, holding full-population mean innovation fixed. Does not generalise to all distributions/competition modes.

## Remaining independent referee gate

The strongest possible absorption targets are **Hua--Chen--Zhang**, **Kim**, **Peitz--Sato**, **Hagiu**, **Berliant--Fujita**, **Cremer--Marchand--Pestieau**, and **Long--Wong**. Stage 11 should read their precise propositions and extensions (where accessible), construct the nearest nested mapping or explain why absent, and attempt stripping *each* proposed innovation mechanism. Important ablations: (a) hold `q_i/m_i` fixed; (b) remove cross-regional `M_j`; (c) remove incumbent business stealing; (d) make fees exogenous; (e) remove private entry; (f) change service-valuation/ability correlation; (g) replace Cournot with Bertrand and uniform with Beta; (h) compare an equivalent private subsidy/contract if the public-ownership policy claim is revisited. No new model is added at Stage 10.
