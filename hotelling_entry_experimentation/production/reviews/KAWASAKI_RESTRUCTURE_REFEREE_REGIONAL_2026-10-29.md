# Independent regional-economics referee report

Date: 2026-10-29 JST. Review object: revised active `manuscript/main.tex` and its section inputs, the Online Supplement and frozen 37-entry ledger, inspected directly. Perspective: regional economics. This report was prepared independently of the manuscript authoring path; it is an adversarial reading, not a new calculation, a new model or a fresh certification of every code result. No empirical regional calibration was supplied.

## Assessment

The revised manuscript now answers a defensible regional question: how the regional appropriation of venture returns and private exclusion from basic services affect a conditional public-service cost ceiling, and how that local comparison differs from the same comparison evaluated nationally. It also gives three separately certified examples of complete institutional configurations. It does not answer a general question of which urban, rural, large, small or incumbent-dominated economies should provide public innovation services.

The presentation makes that distinction explicit. The strongest economic object is the interaction of `M_i`, `omega_i` and `u_i/Delta_i` in Proposition 1; the regional interpretation does not make the standard selection and externality components new. The region labels are fiscal and product-market incidence boundaries, not a spatial-economy model.

## Findings and concerns

### 1. Regional primitives and actual comparative statics — MAJOR BUT FIXABLE in positioning

`M_i` is a derived consumer-plus-incumbent market effect from the maintained Cournot primitives. `omega_i` is the fraction of aggregate successful-venture profit allocated to that region's founder. It is not a measured fraction of all local economic activity, a regional innovation-productivity index, or an endogenous bargaining outcome. The profit shares sum to one; changes in one region's share necessarily change the other region's share in a whole-economy exercise.

The paper proves a conditional lambda response for interior private-only selection, an intermediate founder-share band for the public-value slope with negative commercialisation incidence, and a decreasing critical share as basic-service value relative to specialist value rises. It does not establish a global monotonicity result for the public-cost ceiling in `omega_i`, `u_i`, `N_i`, `N_j`, `n_i`, demand slopes or product substitutability. In particular, the leading weak-matching population product is a scale term holding other objects fixed; populations also affect private returns and cutoffs at finite intensity. It cannot support a universal rule favouring public services in larger or smaller regions.

The new “What regional characteristics identify” paragraph correctly makes these limitations visible. The broad question in the Introduction is followed by an explicit scope restriction, which prevents a false promise. The remaining issue is substantive completeness, not a missing adjective: the full regional-market-structure classification requested by the broad motivation is absent and must remain absent under the present freeze.

### 2. Fiscal incidence and national welfare — adequately bounded

Lemma 3's national/local gap is the other jurisdiction's welfare increment for the same conditional P-to-B change. Positive spillovers create a range in which local rejection and national acceptance coexist; negative spillovers permit the opposite conditional ordering. This is a known fiscal-externality principle. The manuscript correctly avoids claiming that zero wedge suffices for equality of all four actors' decentralised choices and the sixteen-configuration national maximum.

The equilibrium examples demonstrate public underprovision and agreement relative to that restricted benchmark. They do not establish public overprovision, all-parameter policy inefficiency or a first best. Regional fiscal coordination, transfers, optimal subsidies and procurement are not strategic instruments here. A diagnosis-only paper is possible, but the editor must decide whether this particular diagnosis adds enough beyond familiar externality accounting.

### 3. Geography and structural interpretation — PASS within the stated limits

No inspected active passage identifies region A as an empirically established city economy or region B as a rural economy. The research/design versus manufacturing/implementation interpretation is explicitly a founder-type illustration. The two regions have the same independent uniform ability distribution, and no transport distance, commuting, interregional trade cost, spatial spillover decay, industry-specific skill distribution or skill correlation is estimated or modelled. The new title “Private Innovation Intermediation, Regional Incidence, and Public Access” is consistent with this scope.

The paper also distinguishes public service availability from construction of a public building. Actual Catapult/EDIH/extension examples motivate service coexistence; they are not tests of the paper's mechanism and cannot validate its parameter values. Maintaining these qualifications is essential.

### 4. Economic magnitude and portability — MAJOR substantive limitation

The full-game configuration change is tied to a narrow public-cost band. The magnitude is printed and explained as a small continuous movement crossing an indicator threshold. The manuscript retains the Beta(2,2) failure and adverse competition-form record. The broader conditional rectangle is not a four-party equilibrium region, and the current text says so.

Consequently the model supplies a possible allocation mechanism and a conditional diagnostic, not an estimated important determinant of observed regional public-service choices. A claim of material regional policy importance would require evidence or theory beyond the frozen record. No such addition is recommended or undertaken in this editorial exercise.

### 5. Measurement implications — PASS as hypotheses, not empirical findings

The three empirical implications distinguish participant mass, total represented ability, mean ability, provider survival and market-incidence location. Their counterfactual and endogeneity requirements are stated. Measuring local founder registrations alone does not identify the location of product-market gains, or `M_i` and `omega_i`. The model's intraregional-transfer treatment of fees also requires the local ownership convention maintained in the model; it should not be transplanted automatically to an external service provider in an applied evaluation.

The alignment of ability with premium-service valuation is especially strong. An observed fee-induced change in users' willingness to pay is not evidence of a technological-composition change unless that alignment is supported. The manuscript appropriately retains this limitation.

### 6. Remaining presentation repairs — MINOR

At inspection, `04_propositions.tex` contains an empty subsection heading “Regional primitives and technical complementarity” immediately followed by “Central proposition: adverse local innovation and valuable access.” Merge the heading hierarchy without altering the theorem.

At inspection, `04_diagnostic.tex` says separate off-path Nash and entry-deviation certificates are “reported below,” although the complete-game discussion has moved to the preceding welfare section. Replace the directional phrase with a section/Online Supplement reference.

These are editing repairs and do not call for new mathematics.

## Verdict and routing

**Editorial reconstruction: GO**, after the two minor presentation repairs. The revised regional interpretation respects the existing model and its limitations.

**JE submission-readiness gate: NO-GO on the evidence reviewed.** This is not a mathematical rejection and does not mean the proposition is known: theorem-level novelty and editorial economic value remain unclosed. Reorganisation cannot close those research judgments. Route to evidence-scoped Stage 6 novelty assessment and Stage 11 editorial-value synthesis; keep the theory frozen. If journal positioning is reopened, its contribution and portability limits must be taken as inputs, not strengthened to preserve a desired target.
