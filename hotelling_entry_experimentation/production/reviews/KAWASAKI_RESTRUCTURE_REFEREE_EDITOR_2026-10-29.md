## Major concerns and attempted falsifiers

### 1. Known-model-in-disguise attack — MAJOR BUT FIXABLE only by evidence, not rhetoric

Remove the institutional labels. A monopolist prices against upper-tail uniform demand; selected skill is a deterministic function of the participation mass. Conditional welfare is direct surplus plus bilateral nonlinear participation returns and fixed costs. National welfare adds the foreign increment. This reduction absorbs neither every possible claim nor necessarily the whole game, but it defeats claims to a new screening mechanism, a new independent quality state, or a new externality-wedge theorem.

Proposition 1 is the remaining candidate. To clear it, identify the strongest applicable predecessor statement and attempt a substitution recovering the same reoptimized price response, adverse local incidence and public-cost derivative. Damiano–Li, Hagiu and platform/public-option papers are serious threats. Lack of a literal four-institution analogue does not defeat absorption. Conversely, familiar components alone do not prove full absorption. The present honest UNRESOLVED status is correct. A further source audit is a substantive scholarship requirement, not a request for new algebra or a changed model.

### 2. Economic scale — MAJOR editorial reservation

The cost decomposition shows that direct-service surplus is the dominant component. The complementarity-related threshold increase is small, and the discrete configuration comparison changes because a fixed cost lies near indifference. That is a legitimate existence illustration, but it cannot carry a large empirical-policy claim. The narrow three-case interval and failed Beta alternative are visible, as required. The two-dimensional conditional slope certificate is stronger than a point calculation; it does not establish a large welfare gain or a broad full-game switch.

A skeptical editor may still regard the result as a mathematically careful but economically modest synthesis of screening, direct-service access and business stealing. This objection remains after reconstruction. Resolve it through a persuasive interpretation of the existing diagnostic or acknowledge that JE is a stretch; do not search for more favorable parameters.

### 3. Proposition 1's strict baseline claim — MAJOR mathematical scope blocker

The frozen proposition prints strict negativity of the baseline regional incidence under hypotheses that do not explicitly require a positive baseline success intercept. Its Supplement proof explicitly requires that positivity. The newly added paragraph correctly exposes the degenerate zero-baseline case rather than hiding it. The core cutoff-response slope and the finite-matching certificate need not fail because of this issue.

Nevertheless, a caveat placed after a theorem cannot make its literal broader statement true. A referee is entitled to insist on one unambiguous theorem domain. The frozen-edit constraint prevents silently repairing the statement or changing its proof in this task. Record this as an **inherited statement-fidelity defect**, retain the caveat, and withhold submission clearance. Any later authorized correction must receive a separate mathematical scope review; it is not new theory and must not be confused with this editorial reconstruction. Compiling unchanged mathematics does not cure it.

### 4. Research-question completeness — bounded, with one wording risk

The revised question can be answered only as conditional analytical boundaries plus certified examples. It cannot deliver an all-parameter classification of market size, demand, incumbent competition or decentralized provision. The Introduction and regional-primitives paragraph now say so. The national threshold is a comparison of the same two configurations; its equality with the local threshold is not sufficient for full-game planner/decentralized agreement. The welfare section explicitly prevents that inference.

“Regional market characteristics determine the equilibrium” would be too broad if reintroduced in a cover letter or PR. Prefer “identify conditional provision boundaries and illustrate their decentralized consequences.” The restrictions on geography, ability distributions, service productivity, instruments and physical facilities are appropriate.

## Hostile reconstruction of the central economic bridge

From the manuscript alone, hold partner coverage fixed. Complementarity raises the slope of founders' private willingness to pay. The monopoly cutoff rises. Restoring coverage consequently saves a larger direct basic-service loss, while the regional commercialization term can remain negative. Differentiating the displayed cutoff and substituting into the displayed welfare ceiling generates the two signed components in the derivative identity. The weak-matching coefficient separates the adverse market term from the founder-share term; the stated band makes the coefficient positive while keeping local commercialization negative. This is a reconstructible economic argument, rather than a black-box certificate claim.

The reconstruction also reveals precisely why the result is conditional: it treats partner moments as fixed and does not solve every off-path coupled fee game over the parameter interval. The draft correctly separates the latter equilibrium requirement. The baseline-incidence strictness issue above remains a statement-domain problem, not an omitted intuitive bridge.
