## Reduction attempt (not merely comparing titles)

Represent each region's welfare by
```
W_i(s) = W_i^0 + Z_i(s) - F_Gi * g_i - F_Pi * p_i
       + A_i * m_A(s)*m_B(s) + B_i * q_A(s)*q_B(s)
A_i = p_b M_i + omega_i R_0
B_i = lambda*(M_i + omega_i Pi).
```
Conditional on the four-to-two sufficient statistics `(m_A,m_B,q_A,q_B)`, this is an **ordinary game with two interregional public-good externality indices**. The national-minus-local public addition threshold `C ell_i(S_j^0+lambda K_j ell_i/4)` is exactly the foreign external surplus, and the universal P3 novelty claim fails.

The price-screening component also reduces to a standard single monopolist choosing `f` against linearly truncated uniform demand, with the full-coverage price kink. This is not a priority claim for P2. The only possible novelty is the particular endogenous mapping **tariff -> $(m,q)$ -> complementary JV success -> two regional Cournot incidence -> independent public/private entry**. Its general economic force remains under review because the four-actor switch witness is fragile.

## New adverse reviewer conclusion

**Cannot certify a new class of theoretical result.** Four actor count and mixed ownership do not by themselves qualify. An editor could dismiss the model as a re-labeling or combination of externalities, screening and business stealing. The appropriate revised Introduction explicitly says this. The submitted article remains HOLD for novel theorem strength and global off-path fee equilibria, despite mathematically consistent conditional algebra and reproducible numerical examples.
