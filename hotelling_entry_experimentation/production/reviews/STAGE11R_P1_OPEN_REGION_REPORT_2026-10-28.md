## 2. The binding actors and full-deviation proof

**Lower boundary:** region B **government** at heterogeneous lambda=0, comparing public co-provision (B,B) against continued private only in B (B,P). At F_GB=T_0 this government is indifferent between entering and not; for F_GB>T_0, its public entry is unprofitable and (B,P) is the unique pure four-actor entry Nash in this scenario over the range.

**Upper boundary:** region B **government** at heterogeneous lambda=0.5 comparing (B,B) against (B,P). At F_GB=T_H this government is indifferent between keeping and dropping the free basic service; below T_H its co-provision is strictly optimal and (B,B) is the unique pure four-actor entry Nash over the band.

**Nonbinding constraint:** in the pooled lambda=0.5 case its B government's corresponding threshold T_P is strictly below T_0, so B's private-only provision remains strictly locally preferred throughout (T_0,T_H).

The exact-rational checker \`production/reviews/stage11r_p1_open_region_certificate.py\` imports only the previously isolated baseline price/cost primitives and rational arithmetic in \`stage11r_pp_global_certificate.py\`. It constructs all 16 profiles and 4 unilateral binary entry flips per profile. For the **entire exact open interval (T_0,T_H)**, it partitions its closure [T_0,T_H] into eight adjacent rational subintervals and:
- proves all **non-pivotal** selected Nash inequalities with strict rational lower bounds, in all three scenarios on each closed subinterval;
- proves **all fifteen other configurations** fail at least one player's unilateral deviation test on every subinterval; the two boundary-tie counterconfigurations are excluded by the exact **affine** B-government cost expression on the open interval, rather than by spuriously demanding a strict inequality at the endpoints;
- proves the restricted two-region welfare at (B,B) exceeds every other profile on the full closure, scenario by scenario; planner choice remains unique;
- retains the globally unique P/P fee continuations from preceding P0a for the same three scenarios, so the off-path fee game is not accepted on finite-grid grounds alone.

**Important quantifiers:** This full-band statement varies only F_GB, not the skill distribution, private service values, venture profit shares or product competition. No first-best claim. At either boundary, the pivotal inequality becomes an equality, so no uniqueness is claimed at the two endpoints. The eight-piece subdivision is a proof technique, not a discretely sampled sensitivity regression: interval inclusion bounds apply to every real-valued cost within each subinterval, with exact formulas treating the pivotal boundary equalities separately.

## 3. A genuinely multidimensional open neighborhood

To rule out the assertion that the witness survives only at one combination of unrelated parameters, the same checker proves **an explicit three-dimensional open rectangle** simultaneously in the 3 frozen technology cases:

    0.089999 < F_GA < 0.090001
    0.040499 < F_GB < 0.040501
    0.199999 < omega_B < 0.200001; omega_A=1-omega_B.

All other Stage8 primitives, including respective lambda=0/0.5 settings and the uniform distribution/pooled technology distinction, remain fixed. Here omega is the **division of joint-venture returns among founders**, not a change in government ownership.

The proof uses rational **inclusion interval arithmetic** across the entire closed rectangular box; conservative treatment of omega_A=1-omega_B may overestimate variation but cannot introduce a false positive strict sign. It recomputes fee-game coefficients a_i,b_i over omega intervals, rechecks all baseline P/P best-response bounds and Banach contraction estimates (<0.01), and establishes a Poincare-Miranda four-face inclusion certificate around the actual continuous P/P equilibrium using cutoff boxes of radius 1e-7. Thus every parameter vector in the rectangle has an admissible globally unique P/P pure-fee continuation. The interval checker then evaluates all 64 actor/profile deviation inequalities and all 15 planner comparisons per scenario, proves the expected NE strict and every other profile has a strict profitable deviation, and proves planner (B,B) strictly dominates each alternative throughout the rectangle.

These widths are **small** (micro-neighborhood, not economically large robustness). They certify openness in dimensions beyond F_GB, not portability to different ability distributions, costs outside the box, alternative forms of technological complementarities or differentiated Bertrand competition.
