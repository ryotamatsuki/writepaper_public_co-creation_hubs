## 3. Economic importance: a precise diagnostic, not a large calibrated effect

The article now states the policy distinction explicitly. A government's public-cost ceiling is an allocation difference. The return per successful venture is only one component. With negative regional commercialization incidence, fixing the private cutoff gives one policy signal; reoptimizing the fee changes the basic-service access restored by the public alternative and can reverse the derivative. The existing share band identifies when the latter effect wins, and its service-value dependence explains how the boundary changes.

The lower founder-share boundary sustains enough privately appropriable screening; the upper boundary keeps regional commercialization negative. At zero appropriation the screening response disappears. These existing distinctions establish what is being learned beyond a generic foreign-benefit identity.

The numerical section now separates the positive **level** of public value (mainly direct service) from its much smaller technology-induced **change**. The narrow configuration switch is an existence illustration near indifference, not a large empirical welfare estimate. No favorable recalibration, new simulation or new robustness claim was used to make the effect look larger. The existing Beta and Bertrand negative records, finite sensitivity failures, conditional rectangle versus SPNE distinction, and sixteen-configuration planner limitation remain visible.

This addresses the meaning of the result, not an objective minimum economic magnitude required by JE. Whether this conditional diagnostic is sufficiently important for that journal remains an editorial judgment; no rhetorical rewrite can guarantee it.

## Current contribution, in one paragraph

In an innovation-intermediation market with privately selected founders, the net regional return to successful ventures is not sufficient to sign the technology response of a government's willingness to finance basic access. The existing primitive profit-share band identifies when technical complementarity raises that willingness despite adverse local commercialization. The mechanism works through the private fee response and recovered direct-service value, while the national comparison adds foreign welfare. Its scope is conditional and model-specific, supported separately by finite-intensity continuous certification and narrow full-game examples. Known screening, mixed provision and externality accounting are not claimed as new theory.
