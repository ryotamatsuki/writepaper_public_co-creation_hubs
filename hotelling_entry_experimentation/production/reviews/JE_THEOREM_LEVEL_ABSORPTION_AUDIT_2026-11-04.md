## Epistemic and editorial decision

**A universal guarantee that no existing or future general screening theorem implies a result is impossible from a bounded literature search.** A verified mapped counterexample in a predecessor would defeat the novel-priority claim; failure of direct substitution in a finite collection of inspected works cannot certify the theorem's absence from all literature. Do not mark this universal gate PASS, do not claim a new mathematical theory of private pricing, and do not equate a correct chain rule with publication-level originality.

The defensible applied-economics claim is narrower: within the documented predecessor theorems, the published conclusions about fee screening, sorting, optimal contract menus, tier structure, congestion and public/private fee response do not directly sign the local regional public-basic-access fixed-cost derivative under both endogenous private repricing and an independently evaluated Cournot business-stealing incidence. To make that separation observable, the current revision adds Proposition 3: the PRIVATE pricing and founder-selection data, including venture profits, can be exactly identical in two permissible markets with OPPOSITE public-value derivative signs.

## Exact target of a proposed direct-absorption map

The private-only provider selects a single fee f maximising fee receipts, inducing founder cutoff ell and selected skill moment q. Conditional on partner-region full participation, the separate local government compares a fixed government basic-plus-private premium option B with private-only P. The resulting gross fixed-cost ceiling T_i(lambda) incorporates real direct-service benefits and the independent downstream regional incidence coefficients K_i=M_i+omega_i*Pi and S_i^0. The model varies technical complementarity lambda, retaining a private provider that ignores M_i.

Proposition 1 supplies a uniform-type profit-share band. Corollary 1 demonstrates that a mechanically fixed private fee gives the *opposite* local ceiling slope at the same primitive band. Proposition 2 generalises the conditional band to a regular ability distribution under increasing hazard. Proposition 3 constructs admissible Cournot markets with identical private-side behaviour and opposite conditional policy-value signs.

Any assertion of absorption must specify which theorem, its hypotheses and the substitution or short logical reduction. It is NOT sufficient to note that public and private services, fee-induced sorting, and negative spillovers have all previously appeared. Conversely, distinct institutions or failure of a literal renaming is NOT proof that a general theorem could not imply the result.

## Constructive Proposition 3: what screening-only inference cannot establish

Fix all original economic parameters except the two downstream Cournot net-demand intercepts in region B. Maintain n_B=2, chi_B=9/10, b_B=d_B=1, and the successful new good's output y_B=20/73 in both markets. Choose incumbent x_B either 55/219 (original market) or 2/5. Define A_B=3*x_B+(9/10)*(20/73), D_B=2*(20/73)+(9/5)*x_B. In both cases the Cournot FOCs are solved exactly with positive outputs. The original A_B=D_B=1, while the second has A_B=528/365 and D_B=2314/1825.

Because the new good output and other market remain fixed, total venture operating profit Pi and hence p_b, R_0, U, eta, all matching benefits and private-only fees/cutoffs are IDENTICAL at any given lambda. However local consumers and incumbents have different M_B:
    M_B(x_B)=-4*(219*x_B-14)/5329.
Both K_B<0 and S_B^0<0. Exact Fraction bounds on the FULL closed lambda interval [0,1/2], conditional on region A being fully served, establish:
    x_B=55/219: dT_B/dlambda > 1/10000 >0,
    x_B=2/5:    dT_B/dlambda <-1/100000 <0.
This is a proof that **private screening, participation and venture earnings are not sufficient statistics for regional public-access cost-ceiling comparative statics**. It does NOT mean a general social-welfare theorem that already includes the government incidence coefficient cannot be re-solved to yield the same result. It does not certify the full entry SPNE in the second world.
