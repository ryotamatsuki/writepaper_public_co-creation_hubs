## Claim-to-model mechanism mapping

| Theoretical mechanism | Best observed fact | Evidence gap |
|---|---|---|
| Public baseline vs private paid service; potential displacement | A1+A2, B3, E1 | no observed tariff best-response function or effect on paid competitors' net profits |
| Interregional heterogeneity, knowledge search and coverage | B1+B2, D2, C1 | no evidence that two regions jointly maximize `theta_A theta_B` |
| Commercialization and new-product development | C1, D2, D4 | no identified venture-success probability or innovation spillover magnitudes |
| Public complementarity with private specialist | D3 and C1 | programme referral is not proof a privately supplied matching hub optimizes monopoly fees |
| Local versus national welfare and regional crowding-out | A1+A2, UK report evaluation concerns | not a measured sign of `W_i` versus `W_A+W_B`; reported conflict is policy disagreement |
| Free, low-price and mixed funding modalities | B3, D1, E1, F1 | actual institutions often combine G/P financing, so Stage33 is a stylized decomposition |

## Use discipline

Do not use a descriptive programme source to “validate” the model's structural `theta=service-taste` correlation, output Cournot game, Nash equilibria or planner's 16-profile optimum. Do not use commercial funding as proof of a for-profit objective. EU main FAQ is not identical to the supplemental **DRAFT** 2025 FAQ. Different financial denominators must not be combined into an invented net public price or return.
