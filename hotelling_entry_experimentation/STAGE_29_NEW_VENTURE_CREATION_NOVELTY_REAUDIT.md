## 3. CRITICAL ABSORPTION/REDUCIBILITY NO-GO lemma
Keep Stage28 pure binary s_i∈{0,G,P,B}, h_i=1{s_i!=0}. Add a NEW VENTURE with Bernoulli success p(s_A,s_B)=p0+η_A h_A+η_B h_B+λ h_A h_B within probability bounds, EXOGENOUS added project real surplus V_i per town conditional on success; fixed venture value and no feedback on participation/prices/profits as functions of venture probability.
Then ∆W_i = V_i p0 + η_A V_i h_A+η_B V_i h_B+λ V_i h_A h_B. After ignoring constant p0V_i, this is exactly reparameterization of local benefit and cross-site interaction already in local indivisible public-goods games (Cremer 1997), even if outcome labeled a "new business". If identical q whenever there is at least ONE hub in a city, there is NO impact of G vs P on q at fixed h, and consequently q addition alone cannot produce new ownership-composition ranking between G-only and P-only in a given fixed h_A,h_B.
Proof: expand p algebraically, q levels depend solely on h_A,h_B; V_i is constant. Difference in payoff between G and P for same h_i is zero from the innovation term. The extra expected value adds constants to fixed-location welfare scores. This is a strict no-go against fake novelty from relabeling exogenous cost reduction as "business creation." Local gov can still alter binary h marginal incentives; business creation alone cannot establish a *new structural mechanism*.

## 4. NONTRIVIAL new-to-Stage28 venture formation and location-of-profits threshold; NOT literature-certified novelty
Fix region B downstream product market with n≥2 identical B-owned incumbent firms, inverse demand P(Q)=a-bQ, cost c<a, A=a-c>0. Without target venture, n incumbents produce Cournot. A cross-A/B linked founder team can produce one NEW venture (equally cost-efficient new product incumbent substitute) that sells into B as one (n+1)th Cournot competitor at unit cost c. Firm profits divided: A residents receive external share ω∈[0,1], B residents receive 1−ω. Ignore separate venture development resource costs for closed formula, then account if needed. B government counts its consumers and incumbent B profits, plus resident share (1−ω) of new venture profit. Joint two-region planner counts ALL producer profits (including ω foreign share). Venture success may happen without hubs with baseline probability q0∈(0,1), and interconnected hubs raise it by q1−q0=λ>0, with q1≤1 (ensures zero-hub baseline entrepreneurship is not excluded). Thus this is a conditional incremental incentive for adding local public facility when other site active.
Without venture: per-firm output q_n=A/[b(n+1)].
With venture: q_(n+1)=A/[b(n+2)].
Gross global welfare generated by entrant (ignoring real development fixed cost) is:
   ΔW_global=(A²/(2b))[(n+1)^(-2)−(n+2)^(-2)]
            =π_venture * (2n+3)/(2(n+1)^2),
   π_venture=A²/[b(n+2)^2].
B local real welfare gain:
   ΔW_B=ΔW_global−ω π_venture
       =π_venture*[ (2n+3)/(2(n+1)^2) −ω ].
Hence if
   ω>(2n+3)/(2(n+1)^2)
then new venture creation raises GLOBAL surplus but LOWERS LOCAL region B's surplus—even before additional local facility fixed cost. At n=2, threshold 7/18≈.38889; at n=5, 13/72≈.18056. This threshold DECLINES with local incumbent firm count n (continuous derivative negative).
Proof direct Cournot quantities, CS_i, incumbent π, entrant π, and resident profit accounting. Known international trade economics FOREIGN ENTRY analog, e.g. Ahn & Shin 2012, must not call publication novel merely from this observation.

If A currently has a site and B lacks one, and a new B public G raises venture probability by λ, both site entry choices and ownership feasibility still must be solved. Government B's EXTRA marginal payoff from venture=λΔW_B−F_GB while global marginal=λΔW_global−F_GB (aside from any other baseline hub benefits). Thus with ω above threshold, a public B hub can be rejected by local B EVEN WHEN global planner wants it, as long as other hub-utility benefits don't reverse the sign.
Exact rational illustration n=2, A²/b=1, ω=1/2, q0=1/10, λ=1/2 (so q1=3/5), real B public fixed cost F=1/200:
 π_venture=1/16,
 ΔW_global=7/288,
 ΔW_B=−1/144,
 global creation gain net local site cost λΔW_global−F = 103/14400 >0,
 B government net creation gain λΔW_B−F = −61/7200<0.
