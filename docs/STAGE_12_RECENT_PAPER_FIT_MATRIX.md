## Interpretation

The recent evidence rules out the claim that the paper needs empirics simply because the application is regional. It also shows why the final ladder differs by audience: JPET values the generic public-strategic mechanism; Journal of Economics values the platform-theory mechanism but brings closer priors; RRS values the regional strategic application with a lower theorem bar; JRS accepts pure theory but typically demands stronger spatial structure and analytical depth.
