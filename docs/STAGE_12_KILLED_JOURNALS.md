# Stage 12 Killed Journal Register

Initial universe: 13 journals across regional/urban, IO, public economics, general theory and innovation/regional-science families. Kill means “do not place on the serious submission ladder for this frozen paper”; it does not imply low journal quality.

| Journal | Status | Exact reason |
|---|---|---|
| Regional Science and Urban Economics | KILL | Scope is regional/urban, but the frozen model has no metric geography/location structure and the current contribution would risk a “where is the spatial economics?” desk/referee response. Making it fit would require Class T spatial theory. |
| International Journal of Industrial Organization | KILL | Platform/IO mechanisms are in scope, but this is precisely the referee pool in which López–Vives-type follower-price logic and crowded component priors most sharply weaken combination novelty. Current proof ceiling is also thin for the likely novelty bar. |
| Review of Industrial Organization | KILL | Pure theory is published, but the IO audience magnifies closest-component overlap while the paper's public-decentralization payoff is secondary to its readership. JPET dominates on contribution fit. |
| Networks and Spatial Economics | KILL | “Network” in this paper is cross-side participation, not an explicit graph/transport/spatial-network object. Current publication practice emphasizes literal networks/spatial systems. Fitting would require a new network/spatial structure. |
| The Annals of Regional Science | KILL | Current practice is strongly spatial and often empirical; the frozen label-stripped mechanism lacks the spatial primitive needed to make the regional label sufficient. |
| Economics of Innovation and New Technology | KILL | Innovation hubs are the application, not the source of an innovation-economics mechanism such as adoption, technological change or R&D production. Recent practice is substantially empirical. Reframing would distort the actual contribution. |
| Economic Modelling | **HARD KILL — COST** | Current Elsevier economics submission-fee regime applies a positive submission fee in the relevant journal submission route, violating the user's binding `submission fee = 0` constraint. |
| Journal of Economic Interaction and Coordination | KILL | Current identity is centered on complex interaction, agent-based/computational and network/coordination methods. The paper is a small sequential game with numerical certification, not a complexity/agent-based contribution. |
| REGION | NOT KILLED; RESERVE ONLY | Passes scope and fee gates, but the checked recent record supplied fewer close standalone theory analogues than RRS. It is a legitimate reserve alternate, not one of the four ordered ladder journals. |

## Prestigious candidates not quietly dropped

JRS was **not killed**: it remains the Stretch option because a real send-out case exists. Its issue is not prestige but fit: explicit geographic structure and stronger analytical characterization are more central to its natural referee tradition.

## No-distortion conclusion

None of the killed journals can be rescued by adding a spatial primitive, empirical section, global theorem, new robustness model, planner theorem, or innovation-management hypothesis. Those would be Class T changes and are prohibited by the Stage-8/11 freeze.
