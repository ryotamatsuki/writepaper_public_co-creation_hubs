# Stage 11 Editor Meta-Report

## Pre-fix decision
**BORDERLINE.** I would hesitate at desk review because the paper could be read as a computational combination of two known channels, with a benchmark chosen to make the contrast sharp.

## Post-fix decision
**SEND OUT.** The revised manuscript now makes the actual bargain with the reader explicit: it offers a narrow matched-price qualitative reversal, not a new price-response mechanism or global theorem.

## 1. Strongest rejection reason
Combination novelty. A referee can reasonably argue that both component forces are known. The paper survives only if the qualitative change in the public-public game is treated as the contribution.

## 2. Second strongest
Proof ceiling. A computationally certified local existence result is weaker than the global analytic characterization some theory outlets will expect.

## 3. Fatal?
Neither is fatal at the current claim strength. No verified predecessor gives the whole P2-R4 object by direct relabeling/restriction, and the computation is explicitly presented as a local existence certificate.

## 4. Can the manuscript answer without theory change?
Yes. Stage-11 fixes clarified B3, separated analytic from computational content, removed global implications, and exposed limitations.

## 5. Main result sufficiently distinct?
Yes, narrowly. The distinction is not the sign of the follower-price contribution; it is the fixed-price-to-endogenous-price change in the strategic relation of two other decentralized public decision makers.

## 6. Numerical certification acceptable?
Acceptable for journal positioning, not automatically for every theory journal. Stage 12 must treat proof style as a journal-fit constraint.

## 7. Welfare additive?
Yes, but secondary. It prevents a strategic-sign/welfare-sign conflation and identifies a real cross-regional coordination wedge after transfer cancellation.

## 8. Institutional framing credible?
Yes as a reduced-form analogue class. It should not be sold as exact validation of the timing, homing, objective, or price-response equation.

## 9. Is journal-positioning work merited?
Yes. The contribution is sufficiently coherent to match against field-journal standards, but likely not broad enough for an outlet demanding a global analytic theorem or a larger package of independent results.
