## Paper that is being positioned

After stripping institutional labels, the paper studies **two decentralized public investors linked through cross-side participation that face a shared commercial alternative which observes public investment and endogenously reprices**. The sole headline contribution is the locally robust qualitative reversal

`BR_i^{B3′} > 0 > BR_i^{G′}`,

where B3 is a matched-price diagnostic and G restores optimal private repricing.

Official proof ceiling: **NUMERICALLY SUPPORTED ONLY**, more precisely computationally certified regular interior existence plus local continuity/open-set persistence. No global sign theorem is claimed.

Strongest Stage-11 risk: **combination novelty**. Second strongest: **proof ceiling**. López & Vives (2019) remains the closest generic follower-price-response threat and absorbs a component, not the whole nested public-public reversal.

## Why JPET is Primary

JPET is the best balance of generic mechanism fit, public-economics audience value, current pure-theory practice, proof-style tolerance, referee-pool risk and cost. Its current scope expressly includes local public goods, externalities, public/social decision making, market failure and regulation, and welcomes game theory, simulation and comparative-static methods. Recent 2024–26 work shows active publication of formal federal/regional public-policy, network/R&D-policy and strategic-interaction theory.

The likely editor can understand the paper without adding a spatial model, empirical section, global theorem or planner result. The paper can be submitted essentially as the Stage-11 manuscript after positioning/format integration only.
