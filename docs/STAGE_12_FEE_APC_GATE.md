## Official-source rule

- Wiley candidate fee status comes from each journal's current Author Guidelines/OA page.
- Springer Journal of Economics publication fee status comes from its current “How to publish with us” page, which explicitly states subscription publication has no APC. Springer Nature's current support page says subscription journals generally have no charge and any page/colour charges are identified on individual journal pages.
- RRS explicitly states no subscription, submission, or publication fees.
- No waiver-dependent journal is treated as passing a mandatory-fee gate.

## Primary hard-gate conclusion

**JPET passes cleanly.** No submission fee is charged; OA is optional; the standard subscription route does not require an APC. Optional OA pricing is not needed to satisfy the user's zero-cost publication constraint and should be rechecked only if OA is later voluntarily selected.
