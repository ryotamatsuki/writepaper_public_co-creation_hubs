## Primary: attack-by-attack assessment

### Combination novelty

**MAJOR BUT DEFENSIBLE / GOOD JOURNAL FIT.** JPET regularly evaluates results where familiar public-economic forces interact in a new strategic environment. The manuscript must not say the network channel or private repricing is independently new. The contribution sentence should stay at the qualitative public-public sign reversal.

### López & Vives (2019)

**MANAGEABLE, MEDIUM RISK.** A JPET referee may know sequential-investment/follower-price logic, but that is not the entire target object. Stage 13 should make the concession before claiming contribution: López–Vives absorbs the generic negative response channel; this paper's contribution is the nested fixed-price/endogenous-price reversal in a pair of decentralized public best responses.

### B3 matched-price benchmark

**MANAGEABLE, LOW-MEDIUM RISK.** A public-theory audience is accustomed to benchmark decomposition. The matched-price choice must be explained as controlling the fee level at the certified comparison while switching off price policy; it is not regulation and not an all-fixed-fee theorem. Do not add alternative B3 exercises.

### Numerical-not-theory

**MATERIAL BUT NOT FATAL.** JPET's current scope explicitly includes simulation and comparative-static methods and imposes no fixed technique-complexity floor. However, recent papers also demonstrate rigorous analytical characterization. The certificate must therefore be presented as a precise existence/local-continuity result, not as a substitute for a global theorem.

### No empirics

**LOW RISK.** Pure theory is the journal's core product. Institutional evidence should remain motivation/interpretation only.

### Incomplete planner / weak policy conclusion

**LOW-MEDIUM RISK if controlled.** The paper should say only that a positive local coordination wedge exists; it is not an optimal-policy paper.

## Final desk judgement at Primary

A plausible JPET editor has enough reason to send the current paper to referees without asking it to become a different paper. That is the decisive Stage-12 standard.
