## Verified identities

Let

\[
P_A=\frac{N_A(N_A-1)}{2},\qquad Q_{AB}=N_AN_B,
\]

and

\[
\Lambda_A=(1+\beta)-\beta\delta(m_A^1+m_A^0).
\]

The reconstructed no-hub and hub-alone welfare expressions imply exactly

\[
\Delta_H
=v_LP_A(m_A^1-m_A^0)\Lambda_A-F.
\]

The decomposition used in the short-run section is also algebraically exact:

\[
\Delta_H=\Pi_H+D_A,
\]

where

\[
\Pi_H=v_LP_A(m_A^1-m_A^0)-F
\]

and

\[
D_A=\beta v_LP_A(m_A^1-m_A^0)
[1-\delta(m_A^1+m_A^0)].
\]

Conditional on a hub, the pipeline contribution is

\[
G(b)=\beta\eta v_XQ_{AB}b-\frac{\kappa}{2}b^2.
\]

The unique stationary point and SOC are

\[
b^*=\frac{\beta\eta v_XN_AN_B}{\kappa},
\qquad
\frac{\partial^2G}{\partial b^2}=-\kappa<0.
\]

Substitution yields

\[
R_{AB}
=\frac{(\beta\eta v_XN_AN_B)^2}{2\kappa}.
\]

Thus

\[
F^*_{H+b}-F^*_H=R_{AB}>0
\]

under the legacy positive-parameter restrictions.

## Economic-structure checks

These are not algebraic errors, but they materially affect interpretation.

1. `q(b)=q0+eta*b*h` makes the policy effect of `b` identically zero when `h=0`. Pipeline-only provision is excluded by definition.
2. Under positive primitives, the marginal pipeline benefit at `b=0` is `beta*eta*vX*Q_AB > 0`. Consequently `b*>0` is built into the benchmark rather than generated by a trade-off over whether external connectivity should exist.
3. `R_AB` is additively separable from `Delta_H`. Pipeline spending does not reduce `delta`, change the local state, or otherwise repair the legacy redundancy technology. It simply adds another positive surplus term.
4. Region B enters through `N_B` and `Q_AB`; it has no objective, budget, strategy, participation condition, or welfare conflict. The baseline is not a strategic two-region equilibrium.
5. The hub-thickness comparative static follows directly from the combinatorial pair count `P_A=N_A(N_A-1)/2` when `Lambda_A>0`.
6. The cross-regional-size comparative statics follow directly from `Q_AB=N_A N_B` and linear gross benefit in linkage intensity.
