## Most important conclusion
No Network or Club paper needs to reproduce the exact `0/H/P/HP` labels to kill those families. Structural equivalence is sufficient. Search is different only because the retained candidate uses **two asymmetric policy margins**—within-local-market matching productivity versus access to a separate external matching market—rather than two identical search technologies. Whether that residue is actually distinct remains the single blocker.
