# Anti-Confirmation Results

## Search
The audit deliberately sought evidence that would kill the initially favored Search family.

### Counterevidence / threats found
1. **Generic search intermediation is old:** Gehrig (1993) formalizes intermediated versus direct costly search.
2. **Innovation-specific partner search is already explicit:** Lopez-Vega et al. (2016), Meulman et al. (2018), Howells & Thomas (2022).
3. **Domestic versus international search markets are already formalized:** Spray (2021), including market-specific positive/negative externalities.
4. **Public search-market design is current frontier theory:** Mekonnen (2026) jointly studies public segmentation, directed allocation, thick-market effects and congestion.
5. **Subsidized external matching is not merely hypothetical:** export-promotion studies and Békés-Molnar-Steinwender (2026) connect subsidized trade fairs to search/matching frictions and congestion.
6. **Search-market segmentation has many general-theory analogues:** targeted search and matching-market segmentation literatures make local/external relabeling risky.

### What survived anti-confirmation
Only the asymmetric intervention pair:
- improve matching productivity in incumbent market A;
- reduce the access/segmentation barrier to market B.

No novelty is claimed. The exact-prior-art blocker remains open.

## Network
Anti-confirmation found stronger prior art than the initial pro-Network intuition:
- Bathelt et al. map almost directly to local H/global P language;
- Morrison formalizes global pipeline value conditional on local cluster state;
- Operti & Kumar explicitly distinguish internal versus external regional brokerage and show non-monotone configurations.

**Result: KILL strengthened.**

## Club
Anti-confirmation confirms that club theory strongly explains H but undermines the full H/P family:
- congestion/optimal membership is foundational, not novel;
- innovation agglomeration/congestion is established;
- randomized coworking evidence directly supports proximity spillovers;
- no comparably natural club-theoretic P technology was found.

**Result: KILL strengthened.**

## Anti-confirmation conclusion
The audit does not select Search because its literature is thin; it is not. Search is retained conditionally because it alone leaves one narrow structural residue after aggressive stripping. If the next targeted search finds that residue in prior art, all three families terminate.
