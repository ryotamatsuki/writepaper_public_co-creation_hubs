## Preferred residual
If the exact-prior-art blocker is cleared, the minimal candidate should be a **two-market two-sided matching-function model** rather than a pure labor-style directed-search model.

The core state is side-specific market thickness/tightness in the local and external markets. The policy margins must remain asymmetric:
- H: local matching-productivity improvement;
- P: external-market access/segmentation reduction.

Directed allocation may be introduced only if it is essential to produce interaction between those two policy margins and survives the exact-prior-art audit. It is not authorized merely because it is mathematically richer.

## Why not generic labor matching
The model must map to innovation/co-creation partner formation rather than worker-vacancy matching by label only. A next-stage GO would require a defensible mapping of the two sides (e.g. problem/solution, seeker/provider, firm/research-capability) and policy observables. If the same theorem is obtained with workers/vacancies and the H/P distinction is irrelevant, the application fails the label-stripping test.
