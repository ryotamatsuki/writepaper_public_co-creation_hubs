# Next-Stage Contract — Search Exact-Prior-Art Resolution

## Status

**CONDITIONAL GO — NO FORMAL MODEL YET.**

Search/Matching is the sole surviving family, but it has not earned a minimal-model authorization.

## Frozen candidate technology
Only the following asymmetric pair may be tested:

- `H`: increase matching productivity inside an incumbent local two-sided partner-search market.
- `P`: reduce the segmentation/access barrier to a distinct extra-regional partner-search market.

The external market may have its own thickness/tightness once accessible. Searchers may reallocate across accessible markets if directed allocation is essential.

## One authorized blocker
Resolve whether existing formal theory already contains the joint policy problem:

`public actor chooses between improving matching productivity in incumbent submarket A and reducing access/segmentation to submarket B, with endogenous search allocation/tightness across the two markets.`

## Mandatory backward/forward anchors
- Gehrig (1993) search intermediation;
- Spray (2021) domestic/international search externalities;
- Mekonnen (2026) public market segmentation and directed search;
- Yang (2020) targeted search/market segmentation;
- export-promotion/search-friction theory;
- Békés, Molnar & Steinwender (2026) subsidized trade fairs and search/matching frictions;
- recent middlemen/search-equilibrium survey.

## Kill rule
If a paper already contains the asymmetric intervention pair or a structurally equivalent planner problem after label stripping, record:

`NO-GO — SEARCH ABSORBED`

and terminate this technology branch.

The absence of exact `hub/pipeline` terminology is irrelevant.

## Success rule
If the blocker is cleared with publisher/working-paper-level evidence, authorize:

`GO TO TECHNOLOGY MINIMAL MODEL — SEARCH`

The minimal model may then test only:

`Can the two-market search technology naturally generate socially desirable 0/H/P/HP regions?`

It may not yet model decentralized misselection.

## Formal-model prohibitions if authorized
Even after the blocker clears, do not add:
- Network/structural-hole payoffs;
- separate Club utility or congestion terms;
- information asymmetry;
- heterogeneous political objectives;
- portfolio scope/delegation;
- dynamics/irreversibility;
- arbitrary H/P complementarity;
- fixed costs solely to manufacture four regimes.

Matching-function thickness/congestion is allowed because it is internal to Search.

## Required minimality test
The next model must show that H and P differ because one changes **within-market matching productivity** and the other changes **market access/segmentation**, not merely because two independent binary subsidies have different names.
