## Cross-family conclusion
Search wins on **completeness**, not on low prior-art risk. It is the only family where the same core matching technology can naturally contain market thickness and congestion while also supporting a distinct external matching environment. Network has the most direct H/P analogy but is therefore the most absorbed. Club gives the cleanest H technology but cannot generate P without hybridization.

## Hybridization rule
- Search thickness/congestion generated by the matching function: **allowed internal mechanism**.
- Search plus structural-hole payoff: **forbidden hybridization**.
- Search plus separate club utility/crowding term: **forbidden unless matching technology itself cannot represent the effect and a new evidence pivot authorizes it**.
- Club plus external-variety term solely to manufacture P: **forbidden**.
- Network plus ad hoc search cost to manufacture thickness: **forbidden**.
