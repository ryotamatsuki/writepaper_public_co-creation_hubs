# Stage 3R Referee Kill Test

## Attack 1 — “This is just a standard policy-mix problem.”
**FATAL.** Innovation-policy literature already treats interacting instruments, multiple actors, governance, policy boundaries and regional context. H/P labels alone do not create a distinct mechanism.

## Attack 2 — “This is just delegation with a restricted action set.”
**FATAL for M-A/M-F.** If bounded scope causes error solely because the unit is not allowed to select another architecture, the result is a restricted-action tautology. If authority changes information/incentives, an unauthorized delegation primitive has been added.

## Attack 3 — “This is just coordination failure between agencies.”
**FATAL for M-B/M-E/F architecture.** Without a specified new interaction, omission/duplication across bounded units is generic fragmentation/coordination economics.

## Attack 4 — “Two state variables simply manufacture four regimes.”
**MAJOR moving to FATAL for the proposed theory route.** L/X are empirically defensible state dimensions but Stage 2R does not establish directional H/P associations. Merely using two states to label four policy regimes would be a parameterization exercise.

## Attack 5 — “The decentralized distortion is imposed by the organizational structure rather than derived.”
**FATAL.** This is the strongest attack. Under P1–P3, the only direct route from bounded scope to a different choice is an imposed feasible-set/information/objective restriction. No endogenous wedge has been identified.

## Attack 6 — “H and P are just relabelled multitasking.”
**MAJOR/FATAL.** Besanko–Régibeau–Rockett and the broader multitask/organizational-form literature already study how task allocation and cross-task interactions affect organizational form. To distinguish the model, H/P would need a regional-state-specific interaction, but that interaction is not among the frozen primitives.

## Attack 7 — “The same result would arise in any two-task public organization.”
**FATAL.** Feature stripping confirms that every candidate intuition survives replacement of H/P by tasks A/B unless a new regional-economic interaction is introduced.

## Attack 8 — “Regional evidence motivates the labels but is irrelevant to the theorem.”
**FATAL.** P1/P2 currently enter only as state dimensions. No candidate makes multidimensional regional state indispensable to the distortion rather than to the naming of regimes.

# Overall referee verdict
No top candidate has all fatal attacks resolved. Stage 4R authorization would therefore violate the workflow requirement that a minimal-model candidate have a distinct strategic mechanism before formalization.
