## Search conclusion
No source exactly contains the project labels `0/H/P/HP`. That absence is not sufficient for novelty. The underlying structural elements—policy instrument mixes, task/portfolio boundaries, multi-agency versus integrated decision structures, and architecture-induced selection errors—are mature. The Stage 3R candidate would therefore need an additional theorem-generating interaction beyond P1/P2/P3. None survived without adding a new primitive.
