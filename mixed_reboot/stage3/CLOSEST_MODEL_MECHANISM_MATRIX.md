## Bottom line
No closest paper exactly models a regional `0/H/P/HP` menu. But exact label matching is not the novelty test. Once labels are removed, policy-mix theory already covers interacting instruments and actors, and organization theory already covers task partition, authority, centralized/decentralized architectures and selection errors. A new contribution would require an interaction specific enough to survive these families. P1–P3 alone do not supply that interaction.
