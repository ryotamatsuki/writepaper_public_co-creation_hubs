# Stage 1R Anti-Confirmation Results

## A1 — NEXs Tokyo: P-only survives only at program level

NEXs remains a clean P-dominant **program** under strict functional coding. However, Tokyo also operates TIB and other local intermediation. The prefecture-year union is therefore HP.

**Implication:** Stage 0R's observation that P does not technologically require H survives, but it cannot be represented as a P-only Tokyo region-year.

## A2 — E:N BASE: facility H does not equal Ehime system H

E:N BASE itself was provisionally H-heavy, while Ehime already had Aile Lab/community functions and an external-technology/company import function through the digital co-creation architecture. At system level the focal-facility label understates P.

**Implication:** facility coding and region-year system coding answer different questions.

## A3 — Modular P expansion is hidden by region-year binary coding

OIH GLOBAL BRIDGE (2026) and the Fukuoka-CIC linkage intensify external connectivity but leave their regional unions at HP before and after.

**Implication:** the proposed binary system regime erases a central object of interest: a principal adding or funding a P module.

## A4 — Candidate zero regions do not stay zero

Akita, Aomori, Iwate, Mie and Yamanashi all reveal material local support/intermediation once a systematic inventory is performed; several also reveal external matching or outside-startup programs.

**Implication:** absence of a famous hub is not a credible zero. System-level `0_S` is especially difficult because universities, public research organizations, chambers, banks and specialist programs provide intermediation almost everywhere.

## A5 — Kagawa exposes a P-boundary problem

Setouchi-i-Base has a documented broad-area/outside-attraction mission and matching activity, but the primary evidence does not cleanly establish whether outside matching is a dedicated recurrent P function under the strict rule.

**Implication:** geographic mission language cannot substitute for observed service architecture.

## A6 — Local and external linkage proxies can be mechanically dependent

The NISTEP same-prefecture versus outside-prefecture university-industry collaboration shares are valuable, standardized regional indicators, but they partition a common total.

**Implication:** using both as independent local-fragmentation and external-connectivity primitives would manufacture multidimensionality rather than measure it.

## Overall anti-confirmation result

The Stage 0R motivation survives, but the Stage 0R proposed **prefecture-year architecture unit does not**. The data push the project toward a nested design: regional conditions at prefecture-year level, but architecture choices at a single decision-maker/portfolio/commissioning-episode level.
