No credible zero was obtained, and the key regional primitives local fragmentation and external connectivity were not independently validated.

Canonical combined verdict: `NO-GO — MEASUREMENT FAILURE`.

Stage 2R-A/B were not run, as required by the protocol.

The only authorized next route is a nested measurement redesign: prefecture-year regional state plus a single-principal/governing-coalition portfolio/commissioning-episode architecture unit.
