## Main conclusion

The most severe circularity is not conventional econometric endogeneity. It is definitional: `region-year H/P union` aggregates choices made by multiple principals/operators and then labels their union as one architecture choice. That object does not correspond to a single decentralized decision and therefore cannot later be compared cleanly with a single planner choice.
