## 3. Unit under test

The tested unit was `prefecture × year`, with year-end architecture status. All material publicly supported H/P modules in the prefecture were unioned before assigning the regime. Private, university, chamber/bank and national/digital substitutes were separately inventoried for the zero test.

Diagnostic window: **2024–2026**.

Pilot coverage: **15 prefectures × 3 years = 45 region-years**.

## 4. Strict architecture coding

H requires recurrent local search/coordination functions. P requires an explicit recurrent extra-regional brokerage/network function. Generic openness, a guest speaker, or a partner list is insufficient.

The diagnostic panel yields:

- `HP = 28`
- `H = 14`
- `P = 0`
- `0 = 0`
- `UNCERTAIN = 3`

Among all 42 adjudicated observations, **H=1 in 42/42**.

This is the central failure.

## 5. Why the high H rate is not a substantive stylized fact

At the prefecture-year system level, the union operation aggregates distinct modules operated by different principals, agencies, universities, public support organizations and contractors. A region only needs one qualifying local-support module anywhere in the system for H to equal one. In practice, such a module is ubiquitous in the pilot.

The resulting binary H indicator therefore measures the existence of *any* local intermediation in a broad regional system, not a single architecture choice.

## 6. P-only kill test

At the program level, NEXs Tokyo remains a clean P-dominant observation and therefore still demonstrates that P does **not technologically require H**.

But at the prefecture-year system level, Tokyo also contains TIB and other local-intermediation modules. Tokyo therefore codes HP.

No P-only prefecture-year survived the 45-observation diagnostic panel. Additional targeted searches in peripheral prefectures repeatedly found local consultation/community/matching structures alongside any extra-regional program.

Thus the Stage 0R P-only observation survives as a **program-level institutional fact**, but not as a prefecture-year regime.

## 7. Zero-regime kill test

Five candidate-zero systems were explicitly searched: Akita, Aomori, Iwate, Mie and Yamanashi.

All were rejected as zero because a systematic search found material publicly supported local intermediation; several also contain extra-regional matching/import functions.

`0_S = no material system-level intermediation` is even less defensible because universities, public research organizations, chambers, banks and specialist intermediaries supply intermediation in regions without a famous hub.

A weaker `0_P = no dedicated broad public architecture` is conceptually possible, but the pilot did not produce a clean observation under a stable inclusion rule. Creating one would require excluding existing support institutions selectively, which would reintroduce researcher discretion.

## 8. Double coding

Two AI-assisted passes using the same written functional thresholds produced exact architecture agreement on **42/45 = 93.3%** observations. The three disagreements are Kagawa P-boundary cases.

This apparently high agreement is not reassuring: H has no variation among adjudicated cases. A reliability statistic cannot rescue an outcome whose support is saturated. Cohen's kappa for H is not substantively meaningful with no negative H observations.

## 9. Facility/program versus regional-system contradiction

Several Stage 0R cases demonstrate why the two units answer different questions:

- NEXs: program-level `P`; Tokyo system `HP`.
- E:N BASE: focal facility initially coded H-heavy, while Ehime's wider system already contains external-resource coordination; system `HP`.
- SCOP TOYAMA: focal facility H, while later regional ecosystem programs add P.

The Stage 0R functional evidence was not wrong. The aggregation rule changed the object being measured.

## 10. Transition test

The wrong-unit problem is also longitudinal.

OIH GLOBAL BRIDGE (2026) is a meaningful addition/intensification of P. Yet Osaka is HP before and after at the prefecture-year union level. The Fukuoka-CIC linkage behaves similarly. E:N BASE changes the focal institutional portfolio while the Ehime system remains HP.

Thus the binary prefecture-year regime erases precisely the modular architecture changes the future theory aims to explain.

## 11. Regional-state measurement

Standard regional controls are feasible:

- establishment/employment density;
- industrial structure;
- research capacity;
- accessibility.

The theoretically important variables are weaker.

### Local fragmentation

Candidate 1: NISTEP's same-prefecture university-industry collaboration share is available and comparable, but it measures a specific university-industry linkage channel, not general network fragmentation.

Candidate 2: within-prefecture co-patent/project-network density is conceptually preferable but was **not validated** as a reproducible prefecture-year public-data extraction in Stage 1R.

Therefore local fragmentation is **NOT VALIDATED**.

### External connectivity

Candidate 1: NISTEP's outside-prefecture university-industry collaboration share is available, but within the same total it is mechanically complementary to the same-prefecture share. Using both as separate primitives would manufacture multidimensionality.

Candidate 2: outside-prefecture co-patent/project-network connectivity was **not validated** as a reproducible panel variable.

Therefore independent baseline external connectivity is **PARTIAL / NOT YET VALIDATED**.

## 12. Funding and governance

Stage 1R confirms that governance/control structure is observable in form. The pilot contains:

- long-horizon PFI/concession;
- ordinary outsourcing;
- designated management;
- multi-stakeholder committee governance;
- public-private operation;
- modular program contracting.

Decision rights can be partially mapped for mission, operations, eligibility, partner recruitment, portfolio control, evaluation and renewal.

Exact annual funding shares and H/P earmarks are substantially less complete. This is a manageable data issue, but there is no reason to expand it while the outcome unit itself fails.

## 13. Participant geography

Structured participant geography remains usable for a small subset (especially NEXs and TIB) and entity-enrichable for E:N BASE, but it is not harmonized across conventional hubs. Missingness is related to program/platform form.

This is a limitation but not the decisive failure.

## 14. Endogeneity and circularity

The deepest problem is definitional rather than econometric.

`prefecture-year H/P union` aggregates actions taken by several principals/operators into one label. That aggregate has no single decision maker. It therefore cannot later be cleanly compared with a single decentralized architecture choice `R^D`.

Funding earmarks and governance may also be jointly determined with program architecture, but those issues can be handled only after the choice unit is valid.

## 16. What survives Stage 1R

The following facts remain valuable:

1. H and P are institutionally separable **functions**.
2. P-only can exist at the **program** level (NEXs).
3. P is often added through a distinct module/network relation.
4. governance and control rights vary materially across otherwise similar public intermediation organizations.
5. regional state must remain multidimensional; actor count alone is insufficient.

What does **not** survive is the assumption that these functions should be unioned to a single prefecture-year architecture choice.

## 17. Required measurement redesign

Do not return to theory and do not proceed to Stage 2R yet.

The next empirical unit should be nested:

### Regional state unit

`prefecture-year`

Used only for exogenous/predetermined regional conditions `z`.

### Architecture choice unit

`public intermediation portfolio / commissioning episode / program-year`

A valid choice unit must have an identifiable principal or governing coalition that can actually choose H, P, HP or neither within a comparable policy portfolio.

This preserves NEXs P-only, permits modular H/P changes to be observed, and gives governance/funding variables a decision maker to attach to.

### Zero under the redesigned unit

Zero should be defined relative to a **comparable decision opportunity/portfolio**, not as absence of all intermediation in an entire regional economy.

This redesign remains a measurement problem. It does not authorize a payoff function or theory model.
