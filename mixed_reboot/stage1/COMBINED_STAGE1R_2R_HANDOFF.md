# Combined Stage 1R–2R Handoff

## Canonical result

`NO-GO — MEASUREMENT FAILURE`

Stage 1R failed before Stage 2R-A/B. The failure is not that H/P functions are unobservable. It is that the **prefecture-year union is not a valid single architecture-choice unit**.

## Diagnostic facts

- 15 prefectures × 2024–2026 = 45 observations.
- 42 adjudicated observations: HP 28, H 14, P 0, 0 0.
- H=1 in every adjudicated observation.
- NEXs is P-dominant at program level, but Tokyo is HP at region-year level.
- candidate modular P additions such as OIH GLOBAL BRIDGE are hidden as HP→HP.
- no clean zero survived targeted candidate-zero inventory.
- local fragmentation and baseline external connectivity were not independently validated as separate pre-treatment variables.

## Required redesign

Use two nested units:

1. `prefecture-year` for regional state variables;
2. `single-principal / governing-coalition intermediation portfolio or commissioning episode-year` for H/P architecture choice.

Attach funding, governance, control rights and contract horizon to the second unit. Define zero relative to a comparable portfolio/decision opportunity.

Re-run Stage 1R on this redesigned measurement architecture before any Japanese stylized-fact analysis, international portability test, mechanism search or formal model.
