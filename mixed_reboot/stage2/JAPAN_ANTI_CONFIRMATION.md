# Japan Anti-Confirmation Results

The purpose is to prevent Stage 2R from turning observed examples into deterministic rules.

- **Public/local sponsor does not imply H:** Tokyo NEXs, Saga Connect+, Aomori Blue Ocean, Akita outside-startup support and Okayama global-network procurement are P-only.
- **Public/local sponsor does not imply P either:** many prefectural/municipal H-only units remain.
- **Same sponsor can choose multiple architectures:** Tokyo, Okayama and Saga each contain at least three regimes; Osaka, Fukuoka, Akita and Hiroshima contain at least two.
- **HP does not require one governance form:** HP appears under concession/PFI, ordinary public/program governance, public-private operation, designated management and coalition governance.
- **P modularity is not universal:** many H-only programs remain without an attached P module, including E:N BASE, Camps, KOIN, XOSS POINT and others.
- **Zero is boundary-dependent:** Z03 and Z04 are weaker zeros; conclusions using zero must be reported using the strong-zero subset (Z01, Z02, Z05).
- **Regional-state direction is not established:** Stage 2R finds no defensible evidence that weak external linkage mechanically causes P adoption or that weak local linkage mechanically causes H adoption.

## Implication
The surviving evidence rules out deterministic stories based on sponsor identity, administrative label or one-dimensional regional thinness. It does not identify a causal determinant of architecture choice.
