# International Anti-Confirmation

Stage 2R-B deliberately searched for cases that break simple mappings from governance or regional-policy identity to architecture.

- **Enterprise Singapore GIA — P:** a government agency can commission a bounded external-connectivity portfolio without packaging the Japanese H definition into the same choice unit.
- **BIND Basque — P:** a strongly place-based regional-government initiative explicitly imports startups worldwide to match with regional venture clients. Public/regional sponsorship does not imply H-heavy design.
- **Seoul Startup Hub — HP:** a city-linked foundation combines incubation/local ecosystem functions with global expansion and open innovation.
- **Catapult Network — HP:** a national public innovation-agency portfolio combines technical/local ecosystem collaboration with national/international connections.
- **Bayern Innovativ — HP:** a state-commissioned intermediary combines Bavarian networks with a dedicated Europe & International function.
- **Maria 01 — HP:** an integrated local/global startup ecosystem operates through a community company in a City-supported campus rather than direct government operation.
- **MaRS — HP:** an integrated innovation hub with public support is organised as a nonprofit; its architecture does not follow a simple public-control rule.
- **LaunchVic — HP:** a state startup agency uses a mix of ecosystem-building, programme grants and scale-up support, showing that grant/program funding need not imply a zero or P-only architecture.

## Kill implications
1. `public ownership -> H`, `delegation -> P`, `long horizon -> HP`, and similar one-variable governance rules are rejected as mechanism candidates.
2. H/P separability and dedicated P modules are portable.
3. The potentially useful institutional margin is **the scope of the bounded portfolio decision**, not the Japanese legal form used to implement it.
4. International evidence does not establish causal effects of portfolio scope; it only validates the economic decision margin and its boundary conditions.
