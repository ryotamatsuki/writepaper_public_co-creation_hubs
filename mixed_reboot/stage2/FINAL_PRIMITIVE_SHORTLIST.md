## Primitives killed / demoted
- Residual operator control/delegation: **KILL as standalone** — standard authority/delegation theory and no stable architecture mapping in Japanese/international evidence.
- Task-specific finance/earmark: **SECONDARY, not part of the frozen Stage 3R core** — portable but Japanese quantitative coverage is sparse and the idea is exposed to standard multitask/public-finance theories.
- Sponsor identity / public ownership / contract label: **KILL** — anti-confirmation directly rejects deterministic mappings.

## Stage 3R frozen input
The core evidence-constrained search space is therefore a **two-dimensional regional linkage state (P1,P2)** and **one institutional decision-scope primitive (P3)**. Stage 3R may generate at most six mechanisms from these primitives. It may not add lobbying, generic spillovers, multitask incentives, common agency, platform pricing, dynamics or new network-formation primitives to rescue a weak candidate.
