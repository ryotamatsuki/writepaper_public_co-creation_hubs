## Structural conclusion
The following claims are **not available** as contributions:
- regions need different local/external policy mixes;
- interregional connections depend on place-specific capabilities;
- innovation policies are portfolios of interacting instruments;
- governance affects policy mixes;
- delegation/control rights matter;
- multiple funders can distort multitask provision.

What remains open enough for Stage 3R is narrower: whether an empirically observed **two-dimensional local/external regional state**, combined with the **scope of a bounded public portfolio decision**, can generate a discrete `0/H/P/HP` architecture-misselection result that is not already a standard policy-mix, delegation, multitask, common-agency or spillover theorem.

This is a search permission, not a novelty certification.
