## International portability verdict
The surviving margins are not artifacts of Japanese administrative law. The strongest confirming case is **Enterprise Singapore's Global Innovation Alliance**, a government-agency P-specific portfolio explicitly built around overseas partner and market access. The strongest counterexamples to governance determinism are **MaRS** and **Maria 01**, where integrated HP-like support exists under nonprofit/community operating forms rather than direct government operation.
