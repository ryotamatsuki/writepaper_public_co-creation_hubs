# Novelty Kill Results

## Kill test 1 — Spray already contains the same policy pair
**PARTIAL / not decisive.** Spray contains domestic and international search with different externalities and access/trade-cost changes, but not the exact H/P planner menu.

## Kill test 2 — Mekonnen contains structurally equivalent intervention pair
**MAJOR.** Segmentation is chosen by a planner and induces endogenous allocation/tightness. It fully absorbs the P-side mechanism and the proposed reallocation channel, but does not separately parameterize H as local matching-productivity investment.

## Kill test 3 — export-promotion theory already compares domestic improvement vs foreign access
**NO exact formal pair located.** Trade-fair/export-promotion work strongly supports foreign-access intermediation but is not the fatal formal prior art.

## Kill test 4 — matching-platform theory contains productivity vs market-opening choice
**FATAL / YES in general form.** Immorlica et al. optimize pair-specific meeting rates in a two-sided heterogeneous matching market. Intensifying a positive rate and switching a zero rate to positive are respectively the candidate intensive and extensive margins. The candidate discrete policy set is nested in their general control space.

## Kill test 5 — local/external labels are unnecessary
**FATAL.** Local and external can be represented as arbitrary type subsets. No theorem-essential geography is present in the candidate.

## Kill test 6 — public actor is cosmetic
**YES at this stage.** A welfare-maximizing platform/designer performs the same economic role. Public-employment-service models further show that public intermediation itself is standard in search theory.

## Kill test 7 — H/P remain independent investments
Not necessarily: general search design can create congestion/cannibalization interactions. But that hurts rather than helps novelty because those interactions are already present in the closest platform literature.

## Kill tests 8–10 — additional rescue primitives
No information asymmetry, network payoff, club utility or ad hoc H×P complementarity is needed to explain why the current branch fails. Adding them would violate the stage contract.

## Kill test 11 — fixed costs manufacture four regimes
Not evaluated formally because formal model construction is prohibited. Any future attempt to generate four regimes solely through two fixed-cost cutoffs would be weak and does not cure prior-art absorption.

## Kill test 12 — exact labels differ but stripped problem is known
**FATAL / YES.**

# Final verdict

**NO-GO — SEARCH ABSORBED.**

The decisive finding is not that an identical `Ehime H/P` paper exists. It is that the general economic design problem already exists and is more general than the proposed discrete architecture.
