# Teh–Wang–Watanabe (2024) Accessibility Deep Dive

Reference: Christopher Teh, Chengsi Wang & Makoto Watanabe, *Strategic limitation of market accessibility: Search platform design and welfare*, Journal of Economic Theory 216 (2024), 105798. DOI 10.1016/j.jet.2024.105798.

## Core object
The paper studies an intermediated directed-search market in which the platform chooses a meeting technology. Under its maintained technology class, the meeting-technology choice maps one-to-one into a **market accessibility level**: the number/share of buyers who can observe or obtain meeting opportunities with a seller.

## Timing and behavior
The platform sets its meeting technology/accessibility. Participants make joining and trading/search decisions. Buyers direct search conditional on the information/meeting opportunities allocated by the platform.

## Social planner
The paper explicitly asks what market accessibility maximizes expected total surplus under directed-search frictions. Full accessibility is not generally efficient; imperfect accessibility can improve efficiency by controlling search congestion and allocation.

## Relevance to candidate P
Stage 0R-S defined P as reducing a segmentation/access barrier to an external matching pool. Teh et al. show that market accessibility is itself a search-platform design variable with a formal welfare benchmark. Therefore P cannot claim novelty merely because public intermediation opens more counterpart access.

## Relation to H
The paper does not separately define an H-style investment that improves matching productivity in a designated incumbent local submarket. This is why it is classified as `STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE`, not the sole exact prior art.

However, its key equivalence—meeting technology and accessibility as two representations of platform search design—supports the broader absorption result from Immorlica et al.: intensive matching design and extensive access design belong to the same mature platform-design neighborhood.

## Classification
**STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE.**
