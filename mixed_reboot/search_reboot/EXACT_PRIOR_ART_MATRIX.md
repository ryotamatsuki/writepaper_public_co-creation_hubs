## Decision
The matrix does not depend on finding a paper with the literal four labels `0/H/P/HP`. Immorlica et al. provide a higher-dimensional control space in which both intensive meeting-rate changes and extensive zero/nonzero visibility of type pairs are jointly designed for welfare. The proposed discrete architecture is nested in that general design space.

**Stage classification: `NO-GO — SEARCH ABSORBED`.**
