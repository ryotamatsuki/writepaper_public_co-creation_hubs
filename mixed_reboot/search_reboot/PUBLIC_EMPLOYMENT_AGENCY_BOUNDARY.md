# Public Employment Agency Search Boundary

## Purpose
Test whether replacing a private matching platform with a government/public intermediary creates a distinct search-theory contribution.

## Pissarides (1979)
`Job matchings with state employment agencies and random search` explicitly combines a state employment agency and decentralized/random search. This is an early formal precedent for a public matching institution coexisting with an alternative search technology.

## Fougère, Pradel & Roger (2009)
A structural search model gives workers an exogenous job-contact arrival rate from the public employment service while workers endogenously choose search intensity. Increasing public contacts can crowd out private effort, making the total effect ambiguous ex ante.

## Plesca (2010)
The public labor exchange is modeled as a directed-search channel and all other search methods as another channel in a dynamic general-equilibrium matching model. General-equilibrium effects differ from partial-equilibrium program effects because both sides respond.

## Jahn & Wagner (2008)
A matching model has two search methods, PES and random search. Organizational reforms raise PES search effectiveness but may crowd out active search and reduce overall employment/social welfare even as PES placement improves.

## Merkl & Sauerbier (2024)
A macro search-and-matching model contains public and private search channels. The analysis separates direct public intermediation/matching effectiveness from activation/participation effects.

## Implication for the regional-hub project
These models do not reproduce the exact local/external partner market. They nevertheless establish that:
- public matching efficiency is a standard parameter;
- public and alternative search channels can coexist;
- changing public effectiveness reallocates search activity and can create crowd-out/nonlinear effects;
- public status by itself does not make the search technology novel.

Thus `public intermediation` cannot distinguish the candidate from the general matching-platform prior art without an additional public-sector primitive. Such a primitive is not authorized on this branch.

**Boundary verdict: PUBLIC LABEL DOES NOT RESCUE SEARCH THEORY.**
