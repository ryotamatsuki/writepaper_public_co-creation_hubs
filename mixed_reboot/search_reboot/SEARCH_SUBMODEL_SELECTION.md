# Search Submodel Selection

## Status

**NO SEARCH SUBMODEL SELECTED.**

Stage 0R-T ranked a two-market two-sided matching-function model above directed search and random search. Stage 0R-S withdraws that conditional selection because the proposed policy control space is absorbed by more general matching-platform design.

## S-A — Two-market matching function
Would map H to local matching productivity and P to external-market accessibility. It is tractable and policy-measurable, but once local/external actors are treated as type subsets, H/P are restrictions on pair-specific meeting opportunities already optimized by Immorlica et al.

**KILL.**

## S-B — Directed search across two markets
Would make search reallocation and tightness interaction endogenous. However this moves even closer to:
- Mekonnen's endogenous submarket choice under designed segmentation;
- Teh-Wang-Watanabe's directed-search accessibility design;
- Immorlica et al.'s optimal directed-search meeting-rate design.

**KILL — even higher prior-art exposure.**

## S-C — Random search with external access
Simpler, but the H/P interaction becomes weaker and tends toward two independent policy margins. Public-employment-service/random-search models also provide close analogues.

**KILL — no distinct mechanism.**

## Decision
The next operation must not be `Technology Minimal Model — Search`. There is no authorized formal model on this branch.

If the application is pursued, use existing search/matching theory as a benchmark framework rather than presenting a new model as the contribution.
