# Stage 0R Anti-Confirmation Cases

## 1. NEXs Tokyo — P without required H

NEXs Tokyo explicitly links Tokyo startups to regions nationwide and non-Tokyo startups into Tokyo, with overseas expansion support and geography-coded membership. Its core value proposition is cross-region brokerage rather than building a local matching hub first.

**Kills:** the legacy technological premise that P becomes productive only when H exists.

## 2. Ishikawa Innovation Base — smaller regional economy with deliberate HP

IIB combines local startup exchange/consultation with explicit import of Tokyo resources and metropolitan collaboration.

**Kills:** any simple claim that smaller/thinner regions should mechanically choose H while P is a thick-region luxury. It does not establish that HP is optimal.

## 3. Setouchi-i-Base — prefectural HP with outside-attraction mission

Kagawa explicitly describes the facility as an open-innovation base intended to draw people and firms from outside the prefecture while also providing local activity, consultation and business matching.

**Kills:** architecture cannot be inferred from local actor mass alone.

## 4. MomoSta — multi-stakeholder public/local governance does not imply P

Okayama City's hub is operated by a committee including local banks, a shinkin bank, chamber and city. Yet the core official facility description is H-heavy, and no dedicated extra-regional brokerage function was identified in the screened source.

**Warning:** multi-funder or broad governance should not be assumed to expand geographic scope.

## 5. E:N BASE — openness is not the same as external connectivity

The facility has free membership and 561 listed co-creation partner organizations as of July 15, 2026. The public list is rich in names and industries but does not directly code partner geography, and the core operating description does not yet separate P as a dedicated service.

**Warning:** an open or large membership base cannot be coded as P without geographic service evidence.

## 6. OIH — P can be modularly intensified after a mature hub exists

OIH has operated since 2013 with global elements. In 2026 Osaka City introduced the dedicated OIH GLOBAL BRIDGE overseas expansion program.

**Implication:** external connectivity can be a separately scalable policy module. Co-location with H does not prove technological complementarity.

## 7. TIB / NEXs — participant geography can be observable in digitalized public platforms

TIB's official startup database distinguishes Tokyo-region and outside-Tokyo companies; NEXs provides region filters spanning all Japanese macro-regions and overseas.

**Warning:** lack of participant-geography data in conventional hubs is a measurement-design problem, not evidence that geography is irrelevant.

## 8. `0` regime is not obtained by selecting facilities that exist

The screened case universe is intentionally positive on some intermediation function. Therefore a facility-level sample mechanically has few/no `0` observations.

**Correction:** Stage 1R must define a region-year system and inventory university, chamber/bank, private accelerator, TTO, digital platform and other alternatives before coding `0`.
