## 1. Executive verdict

**GO TO STAGE 2R — JAPANESE STYLIZED FACTS AND INTERNATIONAL PORTABILITY.**

The redesign fixes the specific Stage 1R failure. It does not authorize theory, welfare claims or causal interpretation.

The two binding measurement boundaries are:
1. `0` means no H/P **inside a bounded documented decision opportunity**, not absence of intermediation from the regional economy.
2. the validated local/external regional-state measures are **science–industry linkage proxies**, not general measures of the full innovation network.

## 2. Prior Stage 1R failure
The rejected outcome was the union of all H/P activity in a prefecture-year. It generated H in 42/42 adjudicated observations, eliminated P-only and zero regimes, erased modular additions, and combined decisions made by different principals. It therefore could not map to a single decentralized choice `R^D`.

## 3. New nested measurement architecture
- Regional state: `prefecture × year`, used only for `z_rt`.
- Architecture choice: `principal × portfolio/commissioning episode`, used for observed institutional choice `R_j`.
- Link: each decision unit maps to its region and decision year; regional conditions are attached using the nearest defensible pre-decision observation.

Independent programs in the same region-year are never unioned.

## 4. Regional-state unit
Prefecture-year remains useful for actor density, industrial structure, research capacity, accessibility and channel-specific local/external science–industry links. It is no longer an architecture outcome.

## 5. Architecture choice unit
A unit must have: identifiable principal, documented decision opportunity, common portfolio boundary, attachable governance/funding, temporal identity and no aggregation across independent principals.

The resulting decision-unit universe contains **40 candidate units: 35 valid and 5 explicit exclusions**.

## 6. Principal definition
The principal is the public actor or governing coalition that can authorize the bounded program/commission/portfolio. Examples include a prefecture, city, three-party governing coalition, operating committee or public partnership.

Principal identification is **35/35 = 100%** for valid units.

## 7. Portfolio boundary definition
H and P are coded together only when they are contained in the same documented program, contract, concession or governed portfolio. Synthetic region-level combinations are excluded.

Boundary coding is sufficient for all 35 retained units; two observations (Setouchi-i-Base and K-NIC) required adjudication of the **P evidence threshold**, not the identity of the principal.

## 8. Decision opportunity
The observed choice is tied to a launch, procurement, concession, annual support program, MoU/network expansion, subsidy or other dated instrument decision. For every retained unit the project can state who chose/authorized what and when.

## 9. Zero definition
The new zero is an instrument/portfolio-choice zero: an identifiable principal has a documented innovation/startup-policy opportunity but the bounded unit contains neither the two-function H bundle nor explicit P brokerage.

Five zeros survive. The strongest is Saga Startup Promote 2024 because the same principal and annual menu simultaneously contain Startup Connect (H) and Startup Connect+ (P), while Promote provides mentoring/media outreach without H/P under the strict thresholds.

The zeros must never be interpreted as regions with no intermediary services.

## 10. Sample construction
The sample deliberately includes metropolitan, regional and smaller-prefecture decisions, reuse of Stage 0R cases, modular programs and zero candidates. Five invalid constructions are retained as explicit exclusions, including the failed prefecture-year union and a synthetic NEXs+TIB portfolio.

## 11. H/P coding
H requires at least two recurrent local search/coordination functions. P requires at least one recurrent explicit extra-regional brokerage/network function. Generic openness is insufficient. Architecture is mechanically derived from H/P binaries.

## 12. Double-coding reliability

- H agreement: **100%**, kappa 1.000;

These are diagnostic coding statistics, not human inter-rater reliability claims.

H is present in 23/35 (65.7%), not 100%. The saturation failure is removed.

NEXs Tokyo remains P-only under the new unit. Additional P-only units include OIH GLOBAL BRIDGE, Saga Connect+, Aomori Blue Ocean, Fukuoka–CIC, Akita outside-startup demonstration support and Okayama's global-network commission.

The legacy claim that P technologically requires H remains rejected.

## 15. H-only revalidation
At least 15 H-only units survive, including E:N BASE, MomoSta, Camps, KOIN, XOSS POINT, CO-DEJIMA, Lagoon KOZA, SCOP TOYAMA, Shinshu Startup Station, YOXO BOX and others.

## 16. HP revalidation
Eight HP units survive at bounded portfolio level, including TIB, STATION Ai, OIH, FGN, IIB, Setouchi-i-Base, K-NIC and Mie's integrated commissioned package. HP therefore no longer arises from region-wide aggregation.

HP remains a co-provision label only; complementarity is not inferred.

## 17. Zero validation
Five bounded zeros are retained. Stage 2R must run sensitivity analyses excluding weaker zeros (especially coalition/PR-style Z03/Z04) and separately report the strongest within-principal/menu zeros. No claim about a `zero region` is authorized.

## 18. Governance mapping
Governance attaches naturally to the decision unit. Observed forms include:
- long-horizon PFI/concession;
- ordinary public outsourcing;
- designated management;
- multi-stakeholder operating committee;
- three-agency coalition;
- direct public program administration;
- function-specific annual procurement;
- inter-institutional MoU/network partnership.

This is real institutional variation, but not a causal result.

## 19. Funding mapping
Funding form is broadly observable; exact monetary flows are much less complete. Function-specific H/P earmarks are clearest in dedicated procurements/programs. Missing amounts are not imputed. Funding amount is therefore secondary for Stage 2R unless source coverage improves.

## 20. Regional-state merge
All 35 valid decision units map to a prefecture and decision year. Standard regional controls have high source feasibility. Historical pre-decision extraction is required for older launch episodes and must be flagged if no genuinely pre-choice value can be produced.

## 21. Local-fragmentation validation
The project does **not** claim that a general regional-network fragmentation index has been validated.

A narrower measure survives: same-prefecture university–industry collaboration **absolute activity normalized by a predetermined research base**. Its inverse/low level may be used as a `science–industry local-linkage fragmentation proxy`.

Verdict: **VALIDATED WITH CHANNEL BOUNDARY**.

Co-patent/project-network density remains a desired robustness measure, not a validated baseline variable.

## 22. External-connectivity validation
Other-prefecture university–industry collaboration **absolute activity normalized by the same research base** is separately observable.

Unlike the failed Stage 1R shares, local and external activity measures do not mechanically sum to one: both may be high or low.

Verdict: **VALIDATED WITH CHANNEL BOUNDARY**.

This remains a science–industry channel measure rather than full regional external connectivity.

## 23. Transition recoding
At least two high-value transitions survive:
1. Osaka City / OIH — GLOBAL BRIDGE 2026 adds/intensifies a P module.
2. Saga Prefecture — Startup Connect+ 2024 adds a P module alongside the existing Connect architecture.

Fukuoka–CIC 2025 is an additional medium-boundary external-network expansion.

The new unit observes the actual modular decision rather than coding all cases HP before and after.

## 24. Anti-confirmation
The strongest finding is within-principal architecture variation:
- Tokyo: NEXs=P, TIB=HP, bounded subsidy decisions=0.
- Okayama City: MomoSta/local exchange=H, global-network procurement=P, incorporation grant=0.
- Saga: Connect=H, Connect+=P, Promote=0.

Thus `public sponsor type -> architecture` cannot be used as a mechanical explanation.

## 25. Endogeneity
The redesign fixes definitional aggregation, not econometric endogeneity. Governance form, contract horizon and funding restrictions may be jointly selected with architecture. Stage 2R may use these as descriptive institutional contrasts only.

Participant geography remains post-choice/secondary and must not be used as a pre-treatment primitive.

## 27. Theory-measurement correspondence
The measurement object now corresponds to a real decentralized action: an identifiable principal/coalition selects an intermediation service composition inside a bounded public policy portfolio. Future theory must use the **same choice boundary**. It may not silently return to the region-wide union.

The regional motivation survives because each decision is conditioned on regional state `z_rt`. Whether a social planner would choose H/P/HP/0 for the same intervention opportunity remains a later theoretical question; Stage 1R-M does not answer it.
