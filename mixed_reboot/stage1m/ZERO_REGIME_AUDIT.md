# Zero Regime Audit

## Definition
A valid `0` is a bounded innovation/startup-policy decision opportunity in which an identifiable principal selected an alternative support instrument but did not commission the H or P functions defined in the coding manual. It does **not** mean that the surrounding region has no intermediation.

| ID | Principal / opportunity | Principal identified? | H/P feasible in policy menu? | Neither chosen in bounded unit? | Missing-source concern ruled out? | Comparable? | Verdict |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Z01 | Tokyo Metropolitan Government — 2026 deep-tech commercialization expense subsidy | YES | YES — same principal operates/commissions TIB and NEXs | YES | YES | YES, instrument-choice comparison | CREDIBLE 0 |
| Z02 | Okayama City — startup incorporation grant | YES | YES — same startup-support department separately commissions local exchange and global-network functions | YES | YES | YES | CREDIBLE 0 |
| Z03 | Tokyo Metropolitan Government + Tokyo SME Support Center — 2026 startup expense subsidy | YES | YES — broader Tokyo startup policy contains H/P programs | YES | YES | YES, with coalition caveat | CREDIBLE 0 |
| Z04 | Hiroshima Prefecture — 2026 innovation-policy PR commission | YES | YES — same innovation-policy system also operates Camps/local intermediation | YES | YES | MEDIUM — communication procurement differs from service provision | CREDIBLE WITH BOUNDARY |
| Z05 | Saga Prefecture — Startup Promote 2024 | YES | STRONG YES — same annual menu contains Connect (H) and Connect+ (P) | YES under strict H/P thresholds | YES | STRONG | CREDIBLE 0 |

## Strongest zero
`Z05` is the strongest design: the same prefectural principal offers multiple named support instruments in the same annual startup policy menu. `Startup Connect` commissions business matching (H), `Startup Connect+` commissions overseas counterpart/investor introduction (P), while `Startup Promote` provides mentoring/media outreach but neither the two-function H bundle nor external brokerage required for P. This makes zero an observed instrument/portfolio choice rather than absence by search failure.

## Boundary condition
`0` is authorized only as **no H/P in the bounded decision unit**. It must never be interpreted as `no intermediation in the regional economy`. Stage 2R must retain this boundary and should run robustness excluding weaker Z03/Z04 zeros.

## Rejected zero logic
- no famous hub: rejected;
- no webpage found: rejected;
- prefecture-year absence: rejected;
- independent principal provides H/P elsewhere: irrelevant unless wrongly aggregated into this unit.
