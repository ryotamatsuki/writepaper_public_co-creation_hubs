# Anti-Confirmation Results

Stage 1R-M searched for observations that would defeat simple mappings from region or public sponsor type to architecture.

1. **Same public principal, different architectures — Tokyo.** Tokyo Metropolitan Government commissions/operates NEXs (`P`), TIB (`HP`) and financial-support-only decision units (`0`). Public sponsorship therefore does not mechanically imply H or HP.
2. **Same public principal, different architectures — Okayama City.** MomoSta / local exchange functions are `H`, the 2026 global-network procurement is `P`, and the incorporation grant is `0` under the bounded decision-unit definition.
3. **Same annual menu, different architecture — Saga.** Connect is `H`; Connect+ is `P`; Promote is `0` under strict H/P thresholds. This is the strongest within-principal anti-confirmation set.
4. **P-only does not require a weak local ecosystem.** NEXs is P-dominant in Tokyo, so P-only cannot be identified with peripheral/thin regions.
5. **HP does not require long-horizon concession.** HP appears under Aichi's long concession but also under other governance forms (city/agency operation, commissioned packages, designated management).
6. **H can exist under multi-stakeholder governance.** MomoSta remains H despite banks/chamber/city representation; multiple principals do not mechanically generate P.
7. **P can be commissioned under strong public control.** Aomori Blue Ocean and Saga Connect+ are explicit prefectural programs.

## Implication
The redesigned measurement does not pre-select the later mechanism. Architecture varies within sponsor type and even within the same principal's policy menu. Governance/control variables remain candidate explanatory margins, not labels that determine the outcome by construction.
