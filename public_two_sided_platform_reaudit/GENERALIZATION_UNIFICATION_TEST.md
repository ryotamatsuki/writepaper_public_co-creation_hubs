# Generalization / Unification Test

## Candidate unification

The route could meaningfully unite:

1. public–private two-sided platform competition (B1), and
2. decentralized regional public competition (B2),

with endogenous national-private pricing in G.

## Why this is not yet a contribution

Each ingredient has a deep prior literature. Three-platform models and multiple-public-firm models also exist. Simply placing them in one notation would fail workflow v1.1.

## Minimum value test

A valid unification must satisfy all three:

1. B1–B4 are literal restrictions of one minimal model.
2. The best-response/feedback network changes when all strategic margins are active.
3. At least one theorem in G is unavailable in every benchmark separately and is not an immediate corollary of canonical platform or mixed-oligopoly theory.

P2 currently provides the only sufficiently sharp candidate for condition 3.

## Stage 2 assessment

**POTENTIAL GENERALIZATION VALUE, NOT YET A GO-LEVEL UNIFICATION.**

Hence the canonical verdict remains `CONDITIONAL GO`, not `GO — GENERALIZATION / UNIFICATION CANDIDATE`.
