# P2 — Private Price Response Changes Interregional Public Competition

## Candidate target

Compare fixed-private-terms B3 with the full game G.

A strong result would be a sign reversal, threshold creation, ranking reversal or equilibrium-regime change in the public-public reaction system:

`sign BR_i^{B3}' != sign BR_i^G'`,

or an economically equivalent qualitative distinction.

## Prior-art audit

- Kim (2024) establishes one-public/one-private two-sided price and investment interaction.
- Liu et al. (2026) establishes private fee responses to one welfare-maximizing public platform and shows that public policy affects the rival's network.
- Dadpay & Heywood (2006) establishes strategic interaction between two public firms maximizing different national interests.
- Three/N-platform private network models establish that adding platforms changes price/network competition.

No located paper combines these objects into the specific strategic derivative:

`x_1 -> private network -> p_T* -> rival regional network -> dW_2/dx_2 -> BR_2`.

Liu et al. explicitly limits its model to one public and one private platform and identifies multiple public/private platforms as future research.

## Essentiality requirement

P2 survives only if:

1. fixing `p_T` removes the qualitative result;
2. removing `H_2` removes the public-public result;
3. switching off cross-side participation removes or materially changes it;
4. regional rather than national public objectives matter for the sign/threshold.
