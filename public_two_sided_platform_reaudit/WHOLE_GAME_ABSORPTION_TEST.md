# Whole-Game Absorption Test

## Candidate economically relevant game

`G_1:x_1`, `G_2:x_2`, `H_T:p_T`, followed by endogenous participation on both sides of `H_1,H_2,H_T`, with non-use and regional welfare incidence.

## Condition A — single-model reproduction

**FAIL TO ABSORB.** No located single model reproduces the whole game through direct relabeling, normalization or parameter restriction.

- Liu et al. (2026) reproduces the one-public/one-private two-sided layer including participation, fragmentation and private price response, but contains only two platforms and a national total-welfare public objective.
- Kim (2024) reproduces a one-public/one-private two-sided mixed-duopoly layer with investment but baseline full coverage and no second regional public objective.
- Ding–Ko–Shen (2022) and Tremblay–Adachi–Sato (2023) establish three/N-platform two-sided competition, but platforms are not two decentralized regional welfare maximizers plus a national private platform.
- Dadpay–Heywood (2006) establishes strategic interaction among public firms serving different national objectives, but has no two-sided participation or private platform pricing layer.
- Inoue–Kamijo–Tomaru (2009) establishes a regional public objective against a private competitor, but only one public firm and one-sided spatial demand.

## Condition B — all headline results immediate corollaries

**PARTIAL ABSORPTION.** Several proposed headlines are already known or too close:

- P3 generic market creation versus rival-network fragmentation: absorbed by Liu et al.
- P4 critical mass: absorbed by canonical two-sided/public-platform theory.
- P5 generic fragmentation from adding platforms: strongly threatened by N-platform theory.
- P1 generic over/underinvestment reversal under network effects: strongly threatened by platform investment literature.

P2 is not located as an immediate corollary because its object is the effect of a national private platform's endogenous price response on the **strategic relation between two separate regional welfare maximizers** through endogenous participation on both sides.

## Verdict

**WHOLE GAME NOT ABSORBED; MANY CANDIDATE RESULTS ABSORBED.**

This warrants only a conditional Stage 2 GO, not a novelty claim.
