# Player–Objective–Strategy Map

| Actor | Scope | Objective | Candidate control | Endogenous outcomes relevant to objective |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `G_1 / H_1` | Region 1 | `W_1` | `x_1` | resident-firm participation/surplus, local partner participation/surplus, cross-region users, fiscal/resource cost |
| `G_2 / H_2` | Region 2 | `W_2` | `x_2` | symmetric counterpart |
| `H_T` | National/metropolitan | `Pi_T` | `p_T` | private-platform participation on both sides and fee revenue |
| Firm/project side | heterogeneous agents | private surplus | join `0, H_1, H_2, H_T` subject to homing rule | `n_h^F` |
| Partner/support side | heterogeneous agents | private surplus | join platforms subject to homing rule | `n_h^P` |

The public platforms are not assumed to maximize participant count or platform market share. A Stage 3 architecture must derive public incentives from regional real surplus and resource costs.
