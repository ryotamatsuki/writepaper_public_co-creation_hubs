# Frozen Research Question

## English

> When does a locally financed public co-creation hub generate positive additional value for local firms and regional welfare when firms can also access competing public hubs in other regions and a metropolitan private intermediary?

## Japanese

> 地方企業が他地域の公共拠点や東京等の民間拠点を利用できるにもかかわらず、地方政府が自地域に官民共創拠点を整備・強化することが有効となるのは、どのような条件か。

The theoretical label “matching” is not part of the frozen question. The candidate platform interpretation must earn its place by generating a distinct strategic/welfare problem.
