# Stage 2R-TP — Executive Verdict

Date: 2026-09-03

## Canonical verdict

**CONDITIONAL GO — WHOLE-GAME ARCHITECTURE SURVIVES, THEOREM UNPROVEN**

The public-two-sided-platform pivot survives the Stage 2 whole-game absorption test, but only narrowly.

No single located prior model reproduces the economically relevant full architecture: two decentralized regional governments, each controlling a welfare-maximizing public two-sided platform; one nationwide profit-maximizing private platform; endogenous participation on both sides; regional rather than national public objectives; and a strategic chain in which public investments affect platform participation, the private platform adjusts its price, and that response feeds back into the rival regional government's investment incentive.

This is not a finding that the ingredients are new. They are not. Canonical two-sided participation and network effects are established by Rochet–Tirole, Caillaud–Jullien, Armstrong, Hagiu and Weyl. One-public/one-private two-sided mixed competition is directly studied by Kim (2024) and, much more closely, Liu, Reshidi & Rivadeneyra (2026). Three-platform and N-platform two-sided competition are also established, including welfare losses from network fragmentation. Multiple public firms with jurisdiction-specific objectives are established in mixed oligopoly.

The strongest structural prior is Liu, Reshidi & Rivadeneyra (2026). Their model already contains a welfare-maximizing public platform, a profit-maximizing private platform, endogenous participation, private fee responses, and the welfare trade-off between market expansion and erosion of the rival network. Therefore the generic creation-versus-fragmentation claim is not a surviving contribution. Their analysis, however, deliberately contains only one public and one private platform and explicitly identifies multiple platforms, including coexistence of multiple public platforms and private ones, as future work.

## Proposition audit

- **P1 decentralization wedge reversal:** `UNCLEAR — STAGE 3 REQUIRED`. Generic network-driven over/underinvestment reversals are known. Only a threshold caused specifically by decentralized regional public objectives remains potentially viable.
- **P2 private-price response changes interregional public competition:** `SURVIVES WHOLE-GAME TEST`. This is the strongest surviving target. No located prior model combines the two regional public objectives with endogenous two-sided participation and the national private platform's strategic fee response.
- **P3 market creation versus network fragmentation:** `ALREADY KNOWN` in its generic form; kill as headline. Liu et al. already derive the participation/diversion/network-fragmentation trade-off in a one-public/one-private model.
- **P4 critical-mass coordination:** `KILL`. Critical mass and public-platform growth are established platform mechanisms.
- **P5 adding a platform can reduce participation/welfare through fragmentation:** `LIKELY COROLLARY`; do not use as a generic headline. Multi-platform theory already shows that added competition can break network effects and reduce welfare.

## Essentiality diagnosis

The **second regional public platform is potentially essential only for the surviving decentralized-feedback theorem**, not for generic two-sided participation, public entry, fragmentation, or critical mass.

The **national private platform is essential for P2**, because the candidate novelty is the endogenous private price channel. It is not necessary for a pure two-government decentralization wedge.

The **regional welfare objective is economically important but not itself novel**. Dadpay & Heywood (2006) already show that public firms pursuing separate country interests can create welfare-reducing strategic interaction. Stage 3 must therefore make regional incidence interact with two-sided participation and private pricing rather than rely on the label “regional.”

## Routing

**Stage 3R-TP — Minimal Public Two-Sided Platform Architecture Selection is authorized.**

Stage 3 must not preserve all candidate propositions. It should center the architecture search on P2 and, secondarily, a sharpened regional version of P1. P3–P5 remain benchmark/interpretive objects unless a genuinely full-game-only theorem is discovered.
