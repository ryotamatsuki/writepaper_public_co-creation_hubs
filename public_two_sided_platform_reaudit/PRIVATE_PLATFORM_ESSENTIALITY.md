# National Private Platform Essentiality

The private platform is not essential to every possible regional-public theorem.

For a pure decentralization wedge P1, `H_1+H_2` may already be sufficient. If so, that result belongs to B2 and cannot be sold as a three-player full-game theorem.

The private platform **is essential to P2** because the candidate mechanism is:

`x_i -> private network participation -> p_T* -> rival public participation/investment`.

The relevant Stage 4 comparison must therefore be B3 versus G. If endogenizing `p_T` only changes levels and leaves the public reaction system qualitatively unchanged, the private platform is strategically redundant for the headline and the route should be killed.

Stage 2 status: **ESSENTIAL FOR P2; UNPROVEN FULL-GAME VALUE**.
