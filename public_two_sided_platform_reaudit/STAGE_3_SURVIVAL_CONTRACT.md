# Stage 3R-TP Survival Contract

Stage 3R-TP is authorized with a narrow mandate.

## Must select

1. exact two sides;
2. geographic anchoring of each side;
3. which agents may use rival regional platforms;
4. single-homing/multihoming structure;
5. minimum productive heterogeneity supporting non-use;
6. exact cross-side network effect;
7. exact interpretation of public control `x_i`;
8. which side(s) the private platform charges;
9. regional welfare incidence;
10. timing;
11. literal B1–B4 restrictions;
12. a minimal closed-form participation system.

## Priority proposition targets

### Primary: P2-R

Does endogenous national-private pricing qualitatively change the strategic relationship between the two regional public investments relative to fixed-price B3?

Target forms include sign reversal, threshold creation, equilibrium multiplicity change or a ranking reversal—not a mere level shift.

### Secondary: P1-R

Does decentralized regional incidence create a network-strength threshold between over- and underinvestment that is absent in B1 and cannot be recovered as a standard B2 public-good/network result?

## Do not use as headline targets

- generic creation vs fragmentation (P3);
- generic critical mass (P4);
- generic “more platforms can lower welfare” (P5).

They may be accounting or benchmark results.

## Kill architecture immediately if

- the second public platform is a mechanical copy;
- regional welfare differs only by labels;
- private price response does not enter the rival government's marginal incentive;
- the two-sided side can be fixed without changing the candidate theorem;
- the model needs dynamics, matching, relocation, bargaining or arbitrary interaction terms to produce P2-R.

## Next routing

If Stage 3 finds a single minimal architecture satisfying this contract, route to `Stage 4R-TP — Full Game × Nested Benchmarks Minimal Formal Model Hard Kill`.
