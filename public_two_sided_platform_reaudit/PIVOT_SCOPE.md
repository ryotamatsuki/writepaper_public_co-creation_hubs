# Pivot Scope

This is a model-family pivot, not a new research question.

The matching route is preserved as negative evidence in PR #25 and PR #26. The present branch does not modify those artifacts and does not assert that matching is the relevant primitive.

The new candidate family treats a co-creation hub as a **two-sided participation platform** connecting a firm/project side and a partner/support side. Matching-specificity is no longer a contribution requirement.

What remains frozen:

- two regions and two regional governments;
- two regional public hubs/platforms;
- one metropolitan/national private intermediary/platform;
- regionally immobile resident firms/projects;
- rational platform use or non-use;
- regional welfare objectives for public decision makers;
- profit objective for the private platform;
- the policy question of when locally financed provision adds value despite outside platforms.

What is deliberately unfrozen for Stage 3:

- exact identities of the two sides;
- homing assumptions;
- participation-cost distributions;
- exact cross-side externality specification;
- exact public instrument `x_i`;
- exact private fee side(s);
- exact regional incidence accounting;
- timing details beyond the candidate public-investment/private-price/participation sequence.

Dynamics, installed base, reputation, learning and ecosystem accumulation are excluded from this Stage 2 audit.
