# P3 — Market Creation versus Network Fragmentation Audit

Candidate decomposition:

- `0 -> H_i`: extensive creation;
- `H_T -> H_i`: private displacement;
- `H_j -> H_i`: interregional displacement;
- cross-side participation amplifies each margin.

## Prior-art result

Liu, Reshidi & Rivadeneyra (2026) already studies the central economic trade-off in a one-public/one-private two-sided market. Public competition can attract agents who otherwise do not participate while also diverting agents from the private platform, weakening the private network and harming participants who remain there. Their optimal public action internalizes this network-fragmentation cost.

Multi-platform theory independently establishes network-fragmentation welfare costs from splitting users across more platforms.

## What might remain

A **regional** fragmentation term can still matter: `H_1` expansion can erode `H_2` and `H_T` simultaneously, while `G_1` internalizes only some of those losses. That may enter P1/P2 as a wedge component.

But “creation versus fragmentation” itself is not a new theorem.
