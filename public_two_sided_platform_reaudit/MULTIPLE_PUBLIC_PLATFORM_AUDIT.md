# Multiple-Platform and Multiple-Public-Player Audit

## Multiple private/two-sided platforms are already standard extensions

Ding, Ko & Shen (2022), “Partial compatibility in two-sided markets: Equilibrium and welfare analysis,” Economic Modelling 116, 105989, DOI `10.1016/j.econmod.2022.105989`, explicitly models **three platforms**, single-homing users and multihoming content providers. Its distinctive contribution is endogenous partial compatibility, not the mere existence of a third platform.

Tremblay, Adachi & Sato (2023), “Cournot platform competition with mixed-homing,” IJIO 91, 103002, DOI `10.1016/j.ijindorg.2023.103002`, allows symmetric competition among `N` platforms and flexible homing. It shows that welfare can decrease as the number of platforms increases because breaking a network into smaller platforms can outweigh price benefits.

Therefore:

- “three platforms” is not novel;
- “more platforms can fragment network effects” is not novel;
- generic P5 cannot serve as a headline.

## Multiple public firms are also established outside two-sided markets

Han (2008), “An International Mixed Oligopoly with Multiple Public Firms,” Studies in Regional Science 38(1), 191–197, DOI `10.2457/srs.38.191`, explicitly analyzes multiple public firms together with private firms in Cournot competition.

Dadpay & Heywood (2006), DOI `10.1111/j.1467-8454.2006.00292.x`, is more relevant to decentralized objectives: two countries each have public and private firms and each public firm acts in its own country's interest. Their key conclusion is that strategic interaction of the two public firms can reduce welfare.

Thus the second public player is not novel by count or ownership label.

## Search result

No located formal paper combines all of:

1. two regional welfare-maximizing public **two-sided** platforms;
2. a single national profit-maximizing private two-sided platform;
3. endogenous participation on both sides;
4. a public investment/private price sequence;
5. regional-versus-national incidence.

This non-location is not proof of novelty. It only leaves the architecture alive for Stage 3/4 theorem testing.
