# Mixed-Oligopoly Prior Audit

Re-opened: Inoue–Kamijo–Tomaru (2009), Dadpay–Heywood (2006), Buehler–Wey (2014), and related mixed-R&D work.

These establish local/regional public objectives, strategic interactions among public firms, and strategic complement/substitute investment relations between public and private firms. They do not contain a two-sided shared private-network channel plus a third private fee that flips the sign of the public-public investment reaction.
