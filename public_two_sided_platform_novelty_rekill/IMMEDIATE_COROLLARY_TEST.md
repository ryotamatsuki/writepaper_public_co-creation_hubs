# Immediate-Corollary Test

## Verdict

**NO IMMEDIATE COROLLARY LOCATED. No Distance-0/1 mapping.**

## Strongest attempted kills

### Attempt 1 — López & Vives (2019)

Claim: because their two-stage Bertrand investment FOC contains a negative endogenous price-response strategic term, P2-R4 follows immediately.

Failure: their theorem establishes the sign of a *strategic follower-response contribution*, not the sign of the cross derivative between two first-stage decision makers under a fixed-price benchmark versus an optimizing **distinct third** price setter. Reaching P2-R4 requires constructing a new cross-effect and moving price ownership to another player. This is not relabeling.

### Attempt 2 — Zogheib & Bourreau (2021)

Claim: private firm + local public firms + price/investment already contains the target game.

Failure on accessible evidence: no theorem was located comparing local-public investment best-response slopes with private pricing frozen versus endogenous, and the model lacks the two-sided participation mechanism that supplies the positive B3 term. Full theorem PDF access was blocked, so the conclusion is “not demonstrated,” not “does not exist.”

### Attempt 3 — Hahn & Kim (2026)

Claim: two platform investments followed by seller pricing mechanically gives the result.

Failure: the paper explicitly shows price-mediated attenuation, but not a complements-to-substitutes investment-BR sign switch between fixed and endogenous seller pricing. Its investors are private and the follower is a seller, not a shared private platform.

### Attempt 4 — Liu/Kim + second public platform

Claim: duplicate the public platform and apply the one-public private-fee comparative static twice.

Failure: the one-public theorem has no rival-government marginal investment incentive and no `W_{i,x_i x_j}` object. The new cross derivative must be solved jointly with the shared private network and fee response.

## Corollary distance

Closest credible distance: **2–3**. Accordingly, P2-R3 is downgraded as known/close economics, while P2-R4 is not killed as an immediate corollary.
