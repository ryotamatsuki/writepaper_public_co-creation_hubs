# Forward / Backward Citation Audit

The strongest theorem-level and architecture threats were used as citation-chain anchors rather than relying only on title searches.

## Strategic-investment chain

- Fudenberg & Tirole (1984) -> Leahy & Neary and later strategic-R&D literature -> López & Vives (2019).
- López & Vives explicitly carries the strategic-commitment logic into a general R&D-spillover setting and a differentiated-Bertrand extension. This chain confirms that a negative endogenous-product-market response in a first-stage investment FOC is established economics.
- Network-R&D neighbors Sääskilahti (2006), Shrivastav (2021), and Knauff (2021/22) confirm that investment complementarity/substitutability generated by network compatibility/externalities is also established.

## Two-sided/platform chain

- Armstrong, Armstrong–Wright, Hagiu, Weyl -> Belleflamme–Peitz / Jeitschko–Tremblay -> Adachi–Sato–Tremblay / Tremblay–Adachi–Sato.
- This chain establishes pricing, homing, compatibility, and N-platform competition as mature components.
- Farhi & Hagiu (2008) links strategic investment to later two-sided pricing.
- Recent sequence/response papers Sánchez-Cartas (2026) and Bontems–Hamilton–Lepore (2025/2026) show that sequencing itself can materially alter two-sided pricing outcomes.
- Current investment papers Hahn–Kim (2026), He et al. (2026), and Katsamakas–Sánchez-Cartas (2026) strengthen the platform-investment frontier but do not state P2-R4.

## Public/mixed/local-government chain

- Mixed-oligopoly sources Inoue–Kamijo–Tomaru, Dadpay–Heywood, and Buehler–Wey establish public/private strategic interaction and constituency-specific public objectives.
- Zogheib–Bourreau (2021) is the key forward architecture escalation: local public firms plus a private firm compete in network-industry prices/investment.
- Mun & Nakagawa (2010) and Mun (2019) supply the regional-infrastructure pricing/investment branch, including two regional welfare-maximizing governments and a charge-setting operator.
- Liu–Reshidi–Rivadeneyra (2026) supplies the current public/private two-sided payment-platform branch.

## Citation-chain conclusion

The citation neighborhoods make every **component** of the proposed mechanism look less novel, which is the desired hostile outcome. They do not reveal a paper in which the fixed-third-price public-public cross effect is positive and the same cross effect becomes negative when a third profit-maximizing price is endogenized. No forward/backward-chain item inspected produces a Distance-0/1 mapping to P2-R4.
