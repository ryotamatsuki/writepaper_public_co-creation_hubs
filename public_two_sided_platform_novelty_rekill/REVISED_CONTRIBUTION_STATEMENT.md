# Revised Contribution Statement

We study two decentralized welfare-maximizing public platforms competing with a national profit-maximizing private platform in a two-sided participation environment. The contribution is a narrow strategic sign reversal: public investments can be complements when the national private fee is fixed yet substitutes when the private platform optimally adjusts that fee. The reversal occurs because the private-price response can dominate the positive cross-side-network channel that otherwise links regional public investments.

We do not claim novelty for the platform ingredients individually.
