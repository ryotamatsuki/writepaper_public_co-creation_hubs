# B4 Prior Re-Audit

B4 sets `beta=0`. The shared project-to-partner feedback vanishes; fixed-price public-public complementarity collapses in the Stage-4 regime, while endogenous private pricing can still re-couple public decisions.

Ordinary one-sided sequential-game logic makes such re-coupling non-novel. Therefore the contribution cannot be “private pricing links governments”; it must remain the stronger two-sided complement-to-substitute reversal.
