## Why multi-paper reconstruction is not enough to kill

A referee can reconstruct every ingredient from prior work:

- network-generated investment complementarity: Shrivastav/Knauff/Sääskilahti;
- negative follower-price strategic effects: López–Vives/Fudenberg–Tirole/Farhi–Hagiu;
- public/private objectives and local public firms: Zogheib–Bourreau/Inoue/Dadpay–Heywood;
- two welfare-maximizing regions plus a charge operator: Mun;
- public/private two-sided fee response: Liu/Kim.

But Stage-6 absorption requires one prior theorem, or a Distance-0/1 corollary, for the **same mathematical comparison**. None was located.

The whole-game conclusion is therefore not “the combination of ingredients is new.” It is narrower: the inspected literature does not already give the theorem that the endogenous third-price term overturns a positive cross-side-network public-public cross derivative.
