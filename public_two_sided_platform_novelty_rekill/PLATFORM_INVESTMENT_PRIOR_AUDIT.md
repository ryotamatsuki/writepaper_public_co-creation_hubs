# Platform Investment Prior Audit

Dou–He–Xu (2016) studies one-side VAS investment and prices for a two-sided platform. Sui et al. (2023) studies bilateral VAS investment and pricing under competing platforms and multihoming. Farhi–Hagiu (2008) studies strategic cost-reducing investment before two-sided platform pricing.

Therefore cross-side investment-to-price effects are known. None of the inspected papers produces the exact B3-to-G public-public BR sign reversal.
