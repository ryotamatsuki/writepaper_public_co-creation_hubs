# B3 Prior Re-Audit

B3 retains two public investments and a shared private two-sided network but fixes the private fee.

Network/spillover theory already makes investment complementarity unsurprising. Shrivastav (2021) and related R&D-spillover results are close conceptually. B3 complementarity is therefore not claimed as independent novelty.

Its role is the positive benchmark term that P2-R4 overturns.
