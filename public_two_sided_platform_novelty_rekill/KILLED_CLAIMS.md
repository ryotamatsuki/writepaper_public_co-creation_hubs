# Killed Claims

The following remain dead as novelty claims:

- two-sidedness itself;
- public/private platform competition;
- partner multihoming;
- platform investment affecting prices;
- private fee response to public competition;
- network-generated investment complementarity by itself;
- critical mass;
- creation versus fragmentation;
- more platforms can lower welfare;
- P1-R Nash/planner wedge as a headline;
- mere private-price re-coupling of two public decisions.

Stage 7 may not revive them.
