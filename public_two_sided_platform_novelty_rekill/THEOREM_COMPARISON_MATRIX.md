## Referee rejection-paragraph test

> “The result follows immediately from López and Vives (2019): their endogenous price response already makes the strategic effect of investment negative, so replacing private firms by public platforms yields the present complements-to-substitutes result.”

**Test: rejected as unsupported.** López–Vives proves the existence/sign of a strategic follower-response term, not that the *cross derivative between two first-stage investments* is positive with the relevant downstream price fixed and becomes negative once a **distinct third player's** price is optimized. Mapping their game requires changing player ownership of the price decision, objectives, participation system, and the benchmark cross-effect. That is more than relabeling.

A parallel rejection based on Zogheib–Bourreau also fails on current evidence: its architecture is closer on public/private objectives, but no accessible proposition supplies `BR_i^{fixed′}>0>BR_i^{endogenous′}` and the model does not use the cross-side participation channel generating the positive benchmark term.

Therefore P2-R3 is known economics; P2-R4 remains the narrower candidate theorem.
