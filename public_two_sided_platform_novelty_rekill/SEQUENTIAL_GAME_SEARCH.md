# Sequential-Game Search

Adjacent searches covered investment/quality/R&D before downstream pricing, compatibility before pricing, public investment and private response, and follower-response reversals.

Fudenberg–Tirole (1984) and BGK (1985) provide the general direct/strategic-effect logic. Farhi–Hagiu (2008) adapts it to two-sided pricing. Sánchez-Cartas (2026) shows sequential two-sided pricing can reverse another qualitative ranking.

These are serious structural threats, but moving from them to P2-R4 requires adding a second first-stage investor, a third price-setting actor, decentralized welfare objectives, and deriving a new public-public cross effect. Corollary distance: about 3, not 0–1.
