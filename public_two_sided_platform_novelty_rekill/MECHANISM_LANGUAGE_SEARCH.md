# Mechanism-Language Search

Mechanism searches covered indirect/cross-side network effects, service/quality investment, private fee responses, user-base contraction, investment before pricing, and follower-response effects.

Known pieces are abundant. Farhi–Hagiu shows downstream two-sided pricing can make a prior cost-reducing investment strategically soft. Liu et al. shows a private platform changes fees under public-platform competition. Neither supplies the P2-R4 cross-government sign reversal.
