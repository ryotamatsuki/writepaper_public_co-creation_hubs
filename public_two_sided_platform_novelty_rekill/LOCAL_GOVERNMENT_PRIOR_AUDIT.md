# Local-Government Prior Audit

Interregional mixed-oligopoly theory makes regional welfare objectives known. Inoue–Kamijo–Tomaru (2009) has a local public firm competing with a private firm and supplying outside-region consumers. Dadpay–Heywood (2006) shows competing public firms acting for separate countries can reduce welfare.

Hence regional objectives alone are not novel. No inspected local-government model supplied P2-R4.
