# Local-Government Prior Audit

Regional/public objectives are not novel. The relevant question is whether a local-government theorem already contains the same third-price feedback and sign reversal.

## Mun (2019), *Joint provision of transportation infrastructure*

This is the closest located local-government architecture. Two regional governments choose financial contributions that maximize their own regional welfare. A jointly established infrastructure operator is responsible for the infrastructure and user charges; the model closes the governments' contribution responses together with the charge/pricing condition.

Similarity to G:

- two decentralized welfare objectives: **yes**;
- two first-stage public decisions: **yes/close** (regional contributions/investment);
- third charge-setting object: **yes/close**;
- shared infrastructure demand: **yes**.

Critical differences:

- the operator is jointly established and its charge follows the model's pricing rule rather than being a distinct profit-maximizing national platform;
- no two-sided/cross-side participation feedback;
- no benchmark holding the third charge fixed and demonstrating positive public-public investment complementarity;
- no located theorem that switching on the operator's charge response reverses the sign of the governments' investment best responses.

Classification: **STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE, Distance 3**, not immediate absorption.

## Mun & Nakagawa (2010)

Cross-border transport infrastructure pricing and investment is analyzed under alternative government/private-involvement regimes. The paper makes strategic responses in regional pricing/investment an established neighboring literature. It does not provide P2-R4 on the inspected record.

## Inoue–Kamijo–Tomaru; Dadpay–Heywood

These papers establish that local/regional public firms can optimize constituency-specific welfare while interacting strategically with private firms or with other public firms. They remove “regional objective” and “multiple public players” from the novelty set.

## Local-government verdict

No inspected local-government theorem yields the exact `fixed third price: complements` -> `optimizing third price: substitutes` comparison. The local-government family therefore supplies strong component/architecture overlap, not whole-game absorption.
