# Two-Sidedness Mechanicality Test

The general idea that a follower response can reverse strategic effects is not unique to two-sided markets. Hence two-sidedness is not claimed as a universal new logic.

However, in the frozen model `beta=0` destroys the **positive fixed-fee B3 complementarity** that is one side of P2-R4. Endogenous pricing can still re-couple governments, but the strong complement-to-substitute comparison is lost.

Verdict: two-sidedness is essential to the stated P2-R4 theorem, but not an independent novelty claim.
