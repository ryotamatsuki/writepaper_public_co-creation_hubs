# P2-R3 Prior-Art Status

**STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE — SUPPORTING MECHANISM ONLY; NOT INDEPENDENT NOVELTY.**

The second hostile pass makes this classification stronger than the initial audit. López & Vives (2019) explicitly formulate a two-stage investment/product-market game in which an endogenous second-stage price response enters the first-stage investment FOC; in their Bertrand extension the strategic term can be negative. Fudenberg–Tirole and Farhi–Hagiu provide the broader strategic-investment and two-sided-pricing lineage, while Hahn & Kim (2026) shows a current platform analogue in which downstream seller price adjustment attenuates platform-investment effects.

Therefore the economic claim “an endogenous later price response can contribute negatively to an earlier investment incentive” is already known.

What remains unabsorbed is **P2-R4**: that this known negative price-response contribution can dominate a separately positive cross-side-network public-public cross effect, so that the same two first-stage public decisions are complements with the third price fixed and substitutes when that third price is optimized.
