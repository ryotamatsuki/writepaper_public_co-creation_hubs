# Structural Distance Test

Distance scale follows the Stage-6 contract: 0 exact theorem; 1 direct relabel/restriction; 2 one transparent restriction/extension; 3 requires an additional strategic player/objective and new cross-effects; 4+ component similarity.

| Prior | What maps directly | What must be added/changed to reach P2-R4 | Distance |
|---|---|---|---:|
| López & Vives (2019) | two first-stage investments; endogenous second-stage price response; explicit negative strategic term | transfer the price decision to a distinct third player; replace private objectives with two separate welfare objectives; generate a positive fixed-price cross-side-network cross-effect; compare fixed vs optimizing third price | **2–3** |
| Hahn & Kim (2026) | two platform investments; third-party downstream price response; price-mediated attenuation of rival-investment effects | public objectives; one shared profit-maximizing private platform rather than seller pricing two products; two-sided participation; prove fixed-price `+` to endogenous-price `-` BR reversal | **2–3** |
| Zogheib & Bourreau (2021) | private/public objectives; local public firms; prices and infrastructure investment | two-sided participation; exact target timing/ownership of price; B3 fixed-price benchmark; theorem-level BR sign comparison | **2–3** |
| Mun (2019) | two regional welfare decisions + a charge-setting operator | profit-maximizing outside third actor; two-sided private-network feedback; fixed-charge positive benchmark; sign-reversal theorem | **3** |
| Farhi & Hagiu (2008) | two-sided pricing after investment; strategic softening | second first-stage investor; two public objectives; distinct third price setter; fixed-price positive public-public cross effect | **3** |
| Liu et al. (2026) | public/private two-sided competition; private fee response | second public investor and its cross derivative; fixed-price positive benchmark; dominance/sign reversal | **3** |
| Sánchez-Cartas (2026) | two-sided follower-response-induced qualitative reversal | first-stage investments; public objectives; distinct third price; benchmark cross-partial | **3** |
| Shrivastav (2021) | network-driven complement/substitute investment relation | distinct follower price and fixed/endogenous comparison; public objectives | **3–4** |

## Distance verdict

No Distance-0 or Distance-1 predecessor was located. The closest threats are **Distance 2–3**, not because the application label differs, but because the target derivative is absent: the prior must be extended to create a public-public first-stage cross derivative and then split it into fixed-third-price and endogenous-third-price components.

This is sufficient to reject an **IMMEDIATE COROLLARY — KILL** verdict, but not to claim broad priority. P2-R4 survives narrowly.
