# P2-R Canonical Proposition

There exists a nonempty regular parameter region in which two decentralized regional public-platform investments are strategic complements when the national private platform's project fee is fixed, but strategic substitutes when that private platform optimally adjusts its fee after observing the public investments.

Mechanism: fixed-price two-sided private-network contraction creates a positive cross-government channel; endogenous private fee cutting adds a negative feedback that can dominate it.

Formal object: `BR_i^{B3 prime}>0>BR_i^{G prime}`.
