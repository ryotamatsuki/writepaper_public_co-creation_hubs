# Game-Architecture Search

Target architecture: two decentralized first-stage public investors plus one profit-maximizing private price setter and endogenous two-sided participation.

Mixed-oligopoly papers establish regional/public objectives and multiple public firms. Platform papers establish public/private or N-platform competition. The audit did not locate one model containing all economically relevant strategic margins or the P2-R4 result.

Absence of an exact combination is not itself the novelty argument; the surviving object is the full-game cross-partial reversal.
