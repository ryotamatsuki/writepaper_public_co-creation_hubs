# Network-Investment Prior Audit

Shrivastav (2021) shows process-R&D investments in network-goods duopoly can be substitutes or complements depending on effective compatibility relative to product substitutability. Related R&D/network models similarly show network/spillover strength can generate complementarity.

This strongly absorbs P2-R1/P2-R5 as generic ideas. It does not establish that endogenizing a third actor's price overturns a fixed-price complementarity between two decentralized public investments.
