# Public–Private Platform Prior Audit

## Liu, Reshidi & Rivadeneyra (2026)
Strongest B1 prior: one welfare-maximizing public and one profit-maximizing private payment platform, endogenous participation, single-homing consumers, multihoming merchants, network fragmentation and private fee responses. Their model has only two platforms and studies platform fees, not two decentralized public investments followed by a third actor's price choice.

## Kim (2024)
One public / one private two-sided mixed duopoly with investment and pricing. Again there is no second public BR object.

Verdict: strong component/B1 overlap, no P2-R4 absorption.
