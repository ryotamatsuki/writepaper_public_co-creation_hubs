# Evidence Limitations

- “Not located” is not “does not exist.”
- Some paywalled papers were available only through publisher abstracts, indexed descriptions, or author-uploaded copies; they are treated as lower-evidence structural comparisons.
- Farhi–Hagiu, Liu et al. and Sánchez-Cartas were inspected in accessible detailed/full text and anchor the main distance judgment.
- Search coverage is broad across platform, network-investment and mixed-oligopoly terminology but cannot exhaust every unpublished manuscript.
- Stage 6 therefore supports a narrow novelty-survival claim, not a universal priority claim.
