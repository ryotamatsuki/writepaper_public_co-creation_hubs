# Evidence Limitations

1. **Nonexistence is not proved.** “No theorem located” is a search result, not a universal priority claim.
2. **Zogheib & Bourreau (2021) full theorem access was incomplete.** RePEc/EconomiX primary records and the thesis structure/result summary were accessible, but the HAL full-document endpoint was blocked by anti-bot protection during this run. The paper is therefore treated as the strongest structural threat, not as an exact kill.
3. **Some publisher material remains paywalled.** Kim (2024), parts of the platform-investment literature, and some mixed-oligopoly articles were compared from primary publisher abstracts/model descriptions rather than full proofs. They are not used to assert exact absorption.
4. **Hahn & Kim (2026) is a working paper.** Full text dated 26 February 2026 was inspected; its publication status may change. The version/date is recorded explicitly.
5. **He et al. (2026) is a current/online publication.** Model/results were checked from publisher material; the complete subscriber text was not relied on for an exact theorem claim.
6. **Search-engine/index coverage is imperfect.** Queries covered theorem language, mechanism language, multiple platforms, mixed oligopoly, local governments, R&D/investment-before-price, infrastructure/toll pricing, and current 2020–2026 work, but unpublished manuscripts or poorly indexed appendices can be missed.
7. **Bibliographic precision varies by source.** Where a DOI or exact proposition/page was not independently verified, the ledger says “not re-verified” rather than inventing one.
8. **Component inference is separated from theorem observation.** The audit does not infer P2-R4 from a paper merely because it contains public objectives, network effects, investment, or endogenous pricing.

## Evidentiary consequence

These limitations justify a **narrow GO**, not a priority claim. They do not meet the workflow's CONDITIONAL-GO standard because no single inaccessible source is presently known to contain a theorem that is clearly equivalent to P2-R4; the strongest inaccessible threat is structurally close but observably differs in mechanism and benchmark construction.
