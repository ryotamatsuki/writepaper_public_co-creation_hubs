# B3 — Two Public Hubs + Fixed Metro Offer

Fix `p_T=bar p` and write `q=m-bar p`. In the public–metro–public regime (`q>kappa`, `K<q<min{x_1d,x_2d}`), each public Hub competes at the margin with metro rather than directly with the rival public Hub.

The baseline government FOC is

`F_1^{B3}=[3d^2 x_1^2+q^2-2 gamma u x_1^3]/(2u x_1^2)`.

Crucially,

`partial F_1^{B3}/partial x_2 = W_{1,12}^{B3}=0`.

The SOC is

`-gamma-q^2/(u x_1^3)<0`.

Thus B3 public best responses are independent in the interior P–M–P regime. The fixed metro middle interval **insulates** the two public tails from one another.

The symmetric interior investment solves

`2 gamma u x^3=3d^2 x^2+q^2`.

This zero-cross-effect benchmark is the key comparator for the endogenous metro price in G.
