# Damiano–Li Result-Level Kill

Damiano and Li (2008), “Competing Matchmaking,” Journal of the European Economic Association 6(4), 789–818, DOI `10.1162/JEEA.2008.6.4.789`, studies price competition among matchmakers and sorting of heterogeneous participants across matching markets.

The current model does **not** reproduce their matching-market architecture: local Hubs do not set prices, partners do not strategically join markets, and the CSDS project sorting is driven by deterministic expected payoff profiles.

Accordingly, Damiano–Li is not an exact absorption of the full game.

But it removes any contribution claim based merely on heterogeneous sorting across matching intermediaries or on the fact that prices help determine intermediary selection. Those elements are already established in matching theory.

After Stage 4's characteristic-space transformation, the remaining sorting result is actually weaker as a matching contribution than Damiano–Li because no matching-market composition is endogenous.

**Verdict: sorting and intermediary-choice claims KILL; not the primary full-game kill.**
