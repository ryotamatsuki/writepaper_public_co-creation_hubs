# Scalar-Quality Collapse Retest

There are two different collapse tests and they give different answers.

## Narrow provider-only scalar test

For `u>0`, `M_i(theta)=x_i[c+u(1-theta)]` or `x_i[c+u theta]` cannot be replaced by a provider-only number `q_i(x_i)` without losing project sorting. Two ecosystems with the same mean compatibility `c+u/2` can generate different allocations from a homogeneous scalar environment.

**Narrow verdict: PASS.** Composition matters beyond the mean.

## Broader characteristic-space test

Define

`Q_i=x_i(c+u)`, `t_i=x_i u`.

Then the entire project-choice system becomes

`U_1=Q_1-t_1 theta-kappa`,
`U_2=Q_2-t_2(1-theta)-kappa`,
`U_T=m-p_T-kappa`.

This removes all matching terminology without changing any choice boundary, metro demand, price response, public FOC, or strategic cross-effect.

**Broad verdict: FAIL.** The selected model does not collapse to one provider-only scalar, but it does collapse exactly to a standard one-dimensional characteristic/facility differentiation structure. Under the Stage 4 contract this is fatal.
