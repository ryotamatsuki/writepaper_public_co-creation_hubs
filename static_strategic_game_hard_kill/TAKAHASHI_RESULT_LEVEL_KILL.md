# Takahashi Result-Level Kill

Takahashi (2004), “Spatial competition of governments in the investment on public facilities,” Regional Science and Urban Economics 34(4), 455–488, DOI `10.1016/S0166-0462(03)00070-X`, studies governments strategically choosing investment in excludable public facilities in a linear economy and explicitly analyzes strategic interaction and welfare.

Stage 4 does not claim that Takahashi alone reproduces the complete two-public-plus-private follower game. It does establish that, after the CSDS label-stripping transformation, the public side of the candidate is in the same spatial public-facility investment family.

The Stage 4 formulas for public allocation, strategic substitution/complementarity, and decentralized-versus-planner investment therefore cannot be defended as matching-specific merely because `x_i` is called screening capability.

The metro follower adds a strategic price-response channel, but the resulting expression is obtained by adding a flat priced outside supplier to the same characteristic-space facility game.

**Result-level verdict: very strong absorption threat; no exact single-paper full-game identity required for the Gate 2 failure.**
