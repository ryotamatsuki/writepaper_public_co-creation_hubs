# B2 — Two Regional Public Hubs, No Metro

When the public crossing is interior and `kappa<=K`, all projects participate and split at `theta_12`.

Regional welfare is `W_1=3A_1+A_2-kappa-gamma x_1^2/2`, with the analogous expression for region 2.

The exact government cross-partial in the full-public regime is

`W_{1,12}^{B2} = x_1 x_2 a^2(x_1-5x_2)/[u(x_1+x_2)^4]`.

At symmetry this becomes

`-a^2/(4ux)<0`.

Hence the symmetric interaction is strategic substitution. Off symmetry, region 1's local interaction becomes complementary only when `x_1>5x_2`.

The symmetric interior Nash investment is

`x_N^{B2}=(8c^2+20cu+11u^2)/(8 gamma u)`

before `[0,1]` KKT clipping.

With national welfare `4(A_1+A_2)-2kappa-gamma(x_1^2+x_2^2)/2`, the symmetric interior planner choice is

`x_SP^{B2}=(4c+3u)/(2 gamma)`.

Therefore

`x_N^{B2}-x_SP^{B2}=(8c^2+4cu-u^2)/(8 gamma u)`.

The B2 decentralization wedge is underinvestment iff

`c/u < (sqrt(3)-1)/4`,

and overinvestment above that threshold, subject to the stated interior/KKT conditions. Thus a sign-changing welfare wedge already exists without the metro player.
