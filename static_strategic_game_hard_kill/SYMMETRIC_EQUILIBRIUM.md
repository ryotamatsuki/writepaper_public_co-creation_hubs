# Symmetric Equilibrium

Set `x_1=x_2=x`, `a=2c+u`, and `B=12c^2+28cu+15u^2`.

Then

`K=ax/2`,
`p_T*=m/2-ax/4`,
`q*=m/2+ax/4`.

The full-game symmetric public FOC is

`F_N^G(x)=[3B x^2+4amx+4m^2-32 gamma u x^3]/(32u x^2)`.

An interior symmetric Nash investment solves

`32 gamma u x^3=3B x^2+4amx+4m^2`.

The own SOC at symmetry can be written

`[(ax-2m)(ax+m)-8 gamma u x^3]/(8u x^3)<0`

because the active metro regime requires `m>ax/2`.

The cubic has one positive root: its left-minus-right polynomial starts negative at zero, has a single positive turning point before increasing without bound, and therefore crosses once. The constrained equilibrium is obtained with `[0,1]` KKT conditions.

The central-regime validity conditions at the root are

`ax/2 < m < x(a/2+u)` and `kappa<(m+ax/2)/2`.

At symmetry the public cross-effects are

- B2: `-a^2/(4ux)`,
- B3: `0`,
- G: `-a^2/(8ux)`.

Thus G restores exactly half the symmetric direct B2 substitution magnitude relative to the fixed-metro insulation benchmark. This identity is diagnostic, not a contribution after the characteristic-space kill.
