# G — Full Strategic Game

In the central public–metro–public regime,

`K=a x_1x_2/(x_1+x_2)`,
`q*=(m+K)/2`,
`p_T*=(m-K)/2`.

Substitute `q*` into the regional welfare functions before the governments choose investment.

A useful exact decomposition is

`F_1^G = F_1^{B3}(x_1;q*) + W_{1q} q_{x_1}*`,

where

`W_{1q}=[q(x_1-x_2)-a x_1x_2]/(u x_1x_2)`,

`q_{x_1}*=a x_2^2/[2(x_1+x_2)^2]`.

At symmetry, the indirect private-price-response term is

`W_{1q} q_{x_1}* = -a^2/(8u)`.

Thus the private follower's price cut disciplines local investment when local-partner incidence is counted.

The exact public cross-partial is

`W_{1,12}^{G} = -a[a x_1x_2(x_1+7x_2)+2m(x_2^2-x_1^2)]/[4u(x_1+x_2)^4]`.

At symmetry:

`W_{1,12}^{G}=-a^2/(8ux)<0`.

Therefore endogenous private pricing re-couples governments that are independent in B3. This is a genuine three-player feedback, but the Hotelling/characteristic-space reparameterization shows it is not matching-specific.
