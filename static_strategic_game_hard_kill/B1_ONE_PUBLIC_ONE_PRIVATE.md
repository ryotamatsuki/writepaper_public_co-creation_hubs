# B1 — One Regional Public Hub + Metro Private

Remove `H_2`. In the active interior regime, Hub 1 serves low `theta` and metro serves the remaining types.

Cutoff:

`theta_1T=(x_1 d-q)/(x_1u)`.

Metro share per home population:

`1-theta_1T=(q-x_1 c)/(x_1u)`.

Private best response:

`q_B1*=(m+x_1 c)/2`,
`p_B1*=(m-x_1 c)/2`,
`dp_B1*/dx_1=-c/2`.

Validity requires `m>x_1c`, `q_B1*>kappa`, and `q_B1*<x_1d`.

With the baseline regional objective, the public FOC after the private best response is

`F_B1=[(7c^2+24cu+12u^2)x_1^2+m^2-8 gamma u x_1^3]/(8u x_1^2)`.

The SOC is

`-(4 gamma u x_1^3+m^2)/(4u x_1^3)<0`.

Thus private price discipline of public investment already exists with a single public Hub. Any headline claim based only on `dp_T*/dx_i<0` or the induced own-investment effect is not full-game-only.
