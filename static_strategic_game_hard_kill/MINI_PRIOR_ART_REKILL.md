# Targeted Prior-Art Re-Kill

Search date: 2026-09-02. This is a proposition-targeted update, not a repeat of Stage 2.

## Spatial public investment

Takaaki Takahashi (2004), “Spatial competition of governments in the investment on public facilities,” Regional Science and Urban Economics 34(4), 455–488. DOI: `10.1016/S0166-0462(03)00070-X`.

This directly confirms that strategic local-government facility investment, spatial relationships, third-region effects, and welfare are established theory objects.

## Local public vs private spatial competition

Tomohiro Inoue, Yoshio Kamijo, and Yoshihiro Tomaru (2009), “Interregional mixed duopoly,” Regional Science and Urban Economics 39(2), 233–242. DOI: `10.1016/j.regsciurbeco.2008.10.001`.

This uses a Hotelling-type linear-city model with a local-welfare public firm competing with a private firm and analyzes sequential moves.

## Public/private platform competition

Jeong-Yoo Kim (2024), “Mixed duopoly in two-sided markets,” The Manchester School 92(3), 281–295. DOI: `10.1111/manc.12467`. The model uses a linear-city public/private platform competition framework and investment.

Youming Liu, Edona Reshidi, and Francisco Rivadeneyra (2026), “Public vs. Private Payment Platforms: Market Impacts and Optimal Policy,” Bank of Canada Staff Working Paper 2026-10. DOI: `10.34989/swp-2026-10`. It studies a welfare-maximizing public platform versus a profit-maximizing private platform, including private fee responses and user switching/participation.

## Matching-market sorting

Ettore Damiano and Hao Li (2008), “Competing Matchmaking,” Journal of the European Economic Association 6(4), 789–818. DOI: `10.1162/JEEA.2008.6.4.789`.
