# Matching and Referral Subgame

Let `q=1-p`.

## One hub open

A project whose preferred specialist is in the open hub has:

`{h:p, l:qp, 0:q^2}`.

The other type has:

`{m:p, l:qp, 0:q^2}`.

A representative beneficiary firm's unconditional single-hub distribution `S` is therefore

- `h`: `p/2`
- `m`: `p/2`
- `l`: `pq`
- `0`: `q^2`.

## Both hubs open with referral

Endogenous choice sends each type to its preferred hub. The first hub searches its specialist and generalist. On complete direct failure it refers to the rival specialist:

- `h`: `p`
- `l`: `pq`
- `m`: `pq^2`
- `0`: `q^3`.

Call this distribution `B`.

## Both hubs open without referral

`B0={h:p,l:pq,0:q^2}`.

Referral therefore adds the genuine fallback probability mass `pq^2` at productivity `m`; it is not an additive utility term.
