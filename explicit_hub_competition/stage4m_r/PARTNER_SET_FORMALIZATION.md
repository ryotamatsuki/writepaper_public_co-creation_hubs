# Partner-Set Formalization

Two hubs have finite partner pools:

`S_1={g_1,s_1}`, `S_2={g_2,s_2}`.

`g_1,g_2` are distinct resident generalists with overlapping capability coverage. `s_1,s_2` are region-specific specialists.

Projects are equiprobable types `theta_1,theta_2`. Productive capability gain is:

| project | g1 | s1 | g2 | s2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| theta1 | l | h | l | m |
| theta2 | l | m | l | h |

where `0<l<m<h`.

Thus:

- overlapping generalist coverage creates alternative intermediaries;
- specialist asymmetry creates matching-content differentiation;
- the rival specialist remains a positive fallback, so referral has real value.

Every feasible partner is independently compatible with probability `p in (0,1)`.
