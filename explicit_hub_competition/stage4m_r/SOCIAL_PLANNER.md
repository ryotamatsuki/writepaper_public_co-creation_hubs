# Social Planner

The planner uses the identical matching technology and information structure and maximizes `W_1+W_2`.

Gross social marginal benefits are

`G_0=2(K_S-K_0)+2 sigma mu_S`

for the first hub and

`G_1=2(K_B-K_S)+2 sigma(mu_B-mu_S)`

for the second.

Decentralized gross incentives are

`D_0=K_S-K_0+2 sigma mu_S`

`D_1=K_B-K_S+sigma mu_B`.

Thus externalities are

`X_0=G_0-D_0=K_S-K_0>0`

and

`X_1=G_1-D_1=(K_B-K_S)+sigma(mu_B-2mu_S)`.

`X_1` is sign-ambiguous. In 500,000 admissible draws it is positive in 222,325 and negative in 277,675.

An exact identity is

`G_1-G_0 = 2 Gamma < 0`.

Therefore the public-decentralization layer has genuine welfare content, but it does not rescue the central competition/cooperation theorem.
