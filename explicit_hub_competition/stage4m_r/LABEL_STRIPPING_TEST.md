# Label-Stripping Test

Remove the words government, region, public, hub, innovation, co-creation, local and prefecture. The model becomes:

> Two principals decide whether to activate two matching intermediaries. Each beneficiary project has a known type and chooses its first intermediary according to the distribution of productive match quality generated by that intermediary's partner set. Compatibility is learned after entry. The first intermediary searches a generalist and a specialist; after complete failure it may refer the project to the rival intermediary's non-overlapping specialist. A successful match changes the beneficiary's downstream competitive capability and creates surplus for the matched partner. Principals internalize different subsets of beneficiary and partner surplus.

## Absorption assessment

- Endogenous choice among heterogeneous matchmakers: close to Damiano & Li and the broader competing-intermediary literature.
- Competitor referral: already a formal strategic object in platform-referral work.
- Binary principal provision and user competition: close to public-facility/public-input competition.
- Split incidence without beneficiary relocation: not located as an exact one-paper specialization.

Verdict: **NEAR — no exact single-paper one-page isomorphism established.**

This does not rescue Stage 4M-R because the surviving proposition is only global strategic substitution; referral never generates the targeted sign reversal.
