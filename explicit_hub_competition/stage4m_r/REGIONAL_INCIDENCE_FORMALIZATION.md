# Regional Incidence Formalization

A successful match with productivity gain `x` creates two grounded components:

1. beneficiary capability/profit effect through marginal-cost reduction `x`, accruing to the beneficiary firm's home region;
2. resident complementary-partner collaboration surplus `sigma*x`, accruing to the partner's home region.

No hub receives a fee, attraction rent or prestige payoff.

For a Region-1 beneficiary:

| route | beneficiary component | partner component |
|---|---|---|
| Hub1 direct -> Region1 partner | Region1 | Region1 |
| Hub1 fail -> referral -> Region2 specialist | Region1 | Region2 |
| Hub2 direct -> Region2 partner | Region1 | Region2 |
| Hub2 fail -> referral -> Region1 specialist | Region1 | Region1 |

The Region-2 table is symmetric.

The `sigma=0` counterfactual is explicitly solved. Competition remains payoff-relevant and `Gamma<0`; partner-side incidence is therefore **not** an ad hoc rent required to manufacture the result.
