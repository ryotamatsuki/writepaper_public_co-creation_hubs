# Government Incidence Map

## Selected incidence concept: I3 — both regions can receive grounded components of collaboration value

A productive collaboration connects a beneficiary firm in one region to a complementary partner located in a regional partner ecosystem. Regional incidence follows agent residence, not which hub gets symbolic credit.

## Local firm uses local Hub

Example: Region 1 firm uses Hub 1 and matches with a Region 1 partner.

- Region 1: beneficiary-firm capability/profit gain; resident partner collaboration surplus; local consumer share where applicable; Hub 1 fixed cost.
- Region 2: only downstream/cross-market effects.

## Local firm uses foreign Hub

Example: Region 1 firm uses Hub 2 and matches with a Region 2 partner.

- Region 1: beneficiary-firm capability/profit gain remains in Region 1 because the firm does not relocate.
- Region 2: resident complementary partner receives collaboration surplus; Region 2 pays Hub 2 cost if provided.
- Both regions may experience downstream market effects.

This split incidence is what makes foreign-hub project allocation potentially relevant to both governments without inventing Hub revenue.

## Foreign firm uses local Hub

Example: Region 2 firm uses Hub 1 and matches with a Region 1 partner.

- Region 2: beneficiary-firm gain.
- Region 1: resident complementary-partner surplus.
- Region 1 does **not** receive an arbitrary project-attraction bonus or fee.

## Cross-hub referral

Example: Region 1 firm's project first enters Hub 1, direct matching fails, and Hub 1 refers it to Hub 2 where a Region 2 partner is matched.

- Region 1 gains from improved capability/profit of its beneficiary firm.
- Region 2 gains partner-side collaboration surplus.
- The referring Hub receives no assumed fee or local-credit payoff.

## Why governments care

A regional government maximizes welfare of resident economic agents, not Hub market share per se. Hub competition matters only because allocation changes which resident complementary partner participates and which beneficiary firm obtains capability, plus downstream consumer/competition effects.

## Stage 4M-R discipline

The next stage must derive or normalize partner-side match surplus transparently. It may not insert a free project-attraction rent solely to make market share valuable. No bargaining stage is authorized.
