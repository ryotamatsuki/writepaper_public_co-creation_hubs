# Canonical Hub Cooperation Definition

> **Hub cooperation is cross-intermediary referral of an unsuccessfully matched project to complementary partner capabilities available in the rival hub's non-overlapping regional pool.**

Cooperation therefore expands the feasible productive matching set from one hub's direct pool toward the union of the two pools without assuming generic network utility, common ownership, pricing agreements, or full multihoming.

The cooperation mechanism is linked to the same partner-set heterogeneity that creates competition: overlap creates alternative mediators; non-overlap creates gains from referral.
