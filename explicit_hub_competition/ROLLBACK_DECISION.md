# Stage 3M-R Rollback Decision

## Verdict

**ROLLBACK TO STAGE 3M ONLY — EXPLICIT HUB COMPETITION WAS AN ESSENTIAL MISSING STRATEGIC MARGIN.**

## Why Stage 3M is the earliest invalidated stage

Stage 0M–2M remains valid: co-creation is productive collaboration among heterogeneous complementary agents, and the direct hub primitive remains improvement of productive matching technology.

The defect enters at Stage 3M. The previous architecture let governments choose hub provision and let hub profiles change firms' match probabilities, but firms/projects never chose between two simultaneously available intermediaries. Hence it captured government strategic interaction but not literal intermediary competition for matching demand.

## Governance classification

- Stage 0M–2M: **FROZEN AND VALID**.
- Existing Stage 3M–4M / PR #17: **USEFUL DIAGNOSTIC — NOT CANONICAL**.
- No previous result is deleted.
- Existing results on overlap, referral, downstream rivalry and decentralization remain diagnostic results conditional on the absence of endogenous hub choice.

## Authorized change

Exactly one new strategic margin is authorized:

**ENDOGENOUS HUB CHOICE / PROJECT ALLOCATION.**

No new hub technology, network utility, R&D spillover, pricing, Hotelling distance, congestion, capacity or quality choice is authorized.
