# Candidate Propositions for Stage 4 Hard Kill

These are hypotheses, not results.

**P1 — Private price response.** Characterize `dp_T*/dx_i`; interior pre-check gives a negative response. Determine whether corners create additional regimes.

**P2 — Public strategic interaction.** Characterize the sign of `d BR_i/dx_j` and whether commonness/specialization changes it.

**P3 — Full-game price-feedback effect.** Compare B3 and G. Test whether endogenous `p_T` changes the public BR slope, equilibrium investment ranking, allocation threshold, or welfare qualitatively.

**P4 — Additionality composition.** Derive project-type regions for creation, quality improvement and displacement after a marginal/finite increase in `x_i`.

**P5 — Decentralization wedge.** Compare Nash and planner investment; seek primitive conditions for over- versus underinvestment, without requiring both signs for survival.

**P6 — Geometry beyond scalar mean.** Hold `c+u/2` fixed and vary ecosystem composition. Test whether an equilibrium/welfare object changes after endogenous optimization.

At least one proposition must be full-model-only relative to B1-B3 for Stage 4 `GO`.
