# Mini Prior-Art Update for Selected Architecture

Search date: 2026-09-02.

The selected compatibility-density architecture prompted a targeted check of matching-platform design and public innovation intermediaries.

- Halaburda, Piskorski & Yildirim, “Competing by Restricting Choice: The Case of Matching Platforms,” *Management Science* 64(8), DOI `10.1287/mnsc.2017.2797`: competing matching platforms, heterogeneous outside options, choice-set effects and platform pricing. Important component overlap; no two regional welfare-maximizing public screening investors plus metro follower price.
- Condorelli (2025/2026), “Buyer-Optimal Platform Design,” *RAND Journal of Economics*, DOI `10.1111/1756-2171.70014`: heterogeneous match quality and platform matching design; explicitly distinct from the present public-public-private investment/pricing game.
- Duan & Jin (2022), DOI `10.1080/09537325.2021.1915473`: public innovation intermediaries across two Japanese regions; institutional evidence, not an exact strategic game.
- Kohsetsushi empirical work confirms local-government innovation intermediaries and heterogeneous effects but does not provide the selected three-provider game.

No new exact whole-game prior art was located. This is not a novelty proof. Strongest remaining theoretical threats remain Liu et al. (2026) on public-private strategic fee response, Takahashi (2004) on public-facility investment, and Damiano & Li (2008) on heterogeneous competing-matchmaker sorting.
