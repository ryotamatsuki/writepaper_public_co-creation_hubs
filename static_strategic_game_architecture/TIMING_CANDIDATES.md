# Timing Candidates

| Timing | Institutional reading | Strategic content | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `(x_1,x_2)->p_T->h` | public capability is long-lived; private fee flexible | directly exposes `p_T*(x_1,x_2)` feedback | **SELECT** |
| simultaneous `(x_1,x_2,p_T)` | equal commitment speed | removes follower price response channel | robustness candidate |
| `p_T->(x_1,x_2)->h` | private fee commits before public capacity | weak institutional plausibility for durable public capacity | reject baseline |

The selected timing is not chosen to force a sign. It is the minimal timing consistent with durable capability investment and flexible platform pricing.
