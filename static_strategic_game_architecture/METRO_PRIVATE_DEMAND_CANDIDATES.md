# Metro Private Demand Candidates

The metro intermediary has fixed broad matching technology `M_T(theta)=m`, `0<m<=1`, zero baseline marginal monetary cost, and chooses access price `p_T>=0`.

Firm net payoffs are

`U_i=x_i w_i(theta)-kappa`,
`U_T=m-p_T-kappa`,
`U_0=0`.

In the interior region where metro beats H1 on the left boundary and H2 on the right boundary, metro serves

`theta in (L_T,R_T)`,

where

`L_T=[x_1(c+u)-(m-p_T)]/(x_1 u)`,
`R_T=[(m-p_T)-x_2 c]/(x_2 u)`.

With unit project mass in each of two regions,

`D_T=2[(m-p_T)/u(1/x_1+1/x_2)-(1+2c/u)]`,

subject to clipping and participation conditions.

Thus private profit `Pi_T=p_T D_T` has a standard concave interior pricing problem. Corners are a Stage 4 obligation.
