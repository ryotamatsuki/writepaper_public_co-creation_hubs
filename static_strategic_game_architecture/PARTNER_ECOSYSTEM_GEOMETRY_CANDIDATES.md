# Partner Ecosystem Geometry Candidates

## G1 — common/specialist compatibility profile — SELECTED

Let `c>0` be generalist compatibility intensity and `u>0` be specialist intensity:

`w_1(theta)=c+u(1-theta)`, `w_2(theta)=c+u theta`.

The two ecosystems have equal mean compatibility `c+u/2` but different technological composition. `c/u` governs commonness versus specialization; it is not a reduced-form competition coefficient.

## G2 — unrestricted regional distributions `F_i`
More realistic but no closed-form provider thresholds without strong restrictions. Rejected baseline.

## G3 — correlated latent match draws
Captures overlap in the joint distribution but correlation is irrelevant to exclusive ex-ante provider choice unless it affects marginal distributions or another forbidden mechanism (multihoming/referral) is added. Rejected baseline.
