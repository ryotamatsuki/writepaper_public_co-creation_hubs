# Top Candidate Deep Dive — CSDS

## Primitive intuition
Each region contains generalist partner capability `c` plus a specialist component tilted toward one end of technological need space. A public hub's capability investment raises the probability of identifying a compatible partner; the metro intermediary has broad fixed coverage and charges a fee.

## Why it beats C
Generic `q_i(theta,x_i)` is equally tractable but can hide the result in a chosen reduced form. CSDS derives the type slopes from explicit common/specialist ecosystem composition.

## Why it beats A
A finite capability model preserves set structure but creates step demand for the metro intermediary. CSDS yields continuous provider boundaries without adding taste shocks.

## Why it beats B/E/F
Order statistics, correlated maxima, and vector capabilities are richer but impose algebraic complexity before the full-game contribution is established.

## Main vulnerability
The linear compatibility schedules may admit a characteristic-space/spatial analogy. Stage 4 must demonstrate a full-game result that depends on ecosystem composition and strategic metro pricing, not rely on institutional labels.
