# Scalar-Quality Collapse Test

Selected local match schedules are

`M_1=x_1[c+u(1-theta)]`,
`M_2=x_2[c+u theta]`.

## MS1
They cannot be represented by provider-only scalars `q_i(x_i)` for all `theta` unless `u=0`. Thus the model does not collapse to scalar quality in the selected `u>0` region.

## MS2
Both ecosystems have the same mean compatibility `c+u/2`, yet different project-level match schedules. Equal mean quality therefore does not identify allocation.

## MS3
Holding mean compatibility fixed while changing commonness/specialization changes the type-specific slopes and metro/public allocation boundaries. Stage 4 must verify that this also changes an equilibrium or welfare object after optimization.

## MS4 — remaining threat
The model is still a one-dimensional characteristic model and may be mathematically analogous to some spatial/facility models. Productive interpretation alone is not novelty. Stage 4 must compare actual full-game results to Takahashi-type public-facility competition rather than claiming safety from labels.
