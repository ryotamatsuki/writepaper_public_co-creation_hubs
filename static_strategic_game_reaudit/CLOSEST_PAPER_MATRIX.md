## Reading rule

`STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE` means a serious theorem-level threat, not that the candidate is safe. None is classified `EXACT PRIOR ART` for the whole game. The candidate earns no novelty credit merely because exact rows do not match; Stage 4 must produce a full-game-only result.
