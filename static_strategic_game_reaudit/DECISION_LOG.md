No standalone novelty may be claimed for:

- competing matchmakers;
- heterogeneous sorting;
- public/private objective asymmetry;
- strategic public investment;
- matching-quality/search investment;
- private price response;
- creation versus switching/displacement;
- multiple public plus private providers.

## D6 — What survives only as a candidate

The joint public-public-private best-response network with productive opportunity-set matching remains a candidate generalization/unification. It becomes a contribution only if Stage 4 derives a full-game-only result relative to B1-B3 and prior theorems.

## D7 — Partner overlap
