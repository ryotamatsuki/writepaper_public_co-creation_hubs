## Distance result

No close paper has `Y` across the candidate's economically material columns. In particular, the candidate's three-node strategic network combines:

1. two decentralized public investment best responses;
2. a private price best response to both investments;
3. endogenous allocation of immobile projects by productive matching fit.

This establishes **distance**, not contribution. Contribution requires a result that depends on those simultaneous differences.
