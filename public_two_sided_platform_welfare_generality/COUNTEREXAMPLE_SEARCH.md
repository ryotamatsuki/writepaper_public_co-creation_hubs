# Counterexample Search

The hostile search records three boundaries rather than a robustness percentage.

- `beta=0`: fixed-price cross effect disappears; strong complements-to-substitutes comparison fails.
- Coordinated-public welfare optimization: the central smooth regime does not contain a clean interior optimum at the witness; the search runs into a regime boundary.
- Large functional/distribution changes: no global sign theorem was derived, so P2-R4 is not advertised as universal.

The verified claim is an open-set/local theorem, not a global comparative-static law.
