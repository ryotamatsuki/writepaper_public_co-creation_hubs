# Coordination Externality

Define the reduced cross-region externality

`E_ij = d Wtilde_j / dx_i`

with the private fee already optimized.

At an interior decentralized G equilibrium,

`dW^N/dx_i = dWtilde_i/dx_i + dWtilde_j/dx_i + dPi_T/dx_i`

and the first term is zero by government `i`'s FOC. Therefore

`dW^N/dx_i = E_ij + dPi_T/dx_i`.

At the frozen witness this is approximately

`0.44471 - 0.02194 = 0.42277 > 0`.

This is a non-transfer coordination wedge. Its sign is not implied by the BR slope.
