| Prediction | Model object | Observable counterpart | Identification challenge | Falsification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| public expansion lowers private fee | `p^*_{T,x_i}<0` | fee/discount change after exogenous public capacity increase | public investment endogeneity | stable/increasing private fee under clean shocks |
| fixed/rigid private fees support positive cross-region response | `BR_B3'>0` | rival public facilitation rises after region-i expansion | common shocks | robust negative response under rigid pricing |
| flexible private pricing weakens/reverses public reaction | `BR_G'<0` | rival response smaller where private prices adjust | price flexibility endogenous | no interaction with price flexibility |
| stronger cross-side feedback strengthens fixed-price complementarity | `M_B3` network term | response larger where partner participation reacts more to projects | measuring network elasticity | opposite sign with credible elasticity measure |
| price flexibility repairs private-network contraction | price-response channel | private participation falls less where fees adjust | simultaneous demand/price | larger contraction despite comparable fee response |

These are model-implied restrictions, not claims that existing data already verify them.
