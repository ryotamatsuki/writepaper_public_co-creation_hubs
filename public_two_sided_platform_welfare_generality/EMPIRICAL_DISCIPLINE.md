# Empirical Discipline

The model yields falsifiable conditional restrictions, not causal estimates. A clean empirical design would need exogenous or predetermined variation in regional facilitation investment, separately measured private fee flexibility, and project/partner participation on both sides. The central falsifier is failure of the predicted interaction: if private prices are demonstrably flexible yet regional public-investment responses remain as positively linked as under rigid pricing, the P2-R4 interpretation is rejected for that setting.
