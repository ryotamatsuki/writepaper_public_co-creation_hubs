# Asymmetry Test

The frozen canonical model is parameter-symmetric, so introducing new region-specific primitives would violate the model freeze. Stage 7 therefore separates two concepts.

1. **Asymmetric states (`x_1 != x_2`)**: continuity of the smooth regime implies the strict sign-reversal result survives sufficiently small off-diagonal perturbations around the symmetric witness.
2. **Structural regional asymmetry**: not certified because the canonical parameterization does not contain separately indexed `rho_i`, `kappa_i`, or `gamma_i`.

Verdict: **local state asymmetry survives; a theorem on structurally asymmetric regions is outside the frozen model and remains unproven.**
