# Remaining Fatal / Major Concerns

## Single Stage-7 blocker
**Institutional private-price identification.** A primary-source-backed national private innovation intermediary must be shown to be a relevant substitute for focal projects, to charge the relevant side, and to have a credible repricing margin.

## Major but non-blocking limitations
- project single-homing is a focal-project approximation, not literal firm exclusivity;
- local, not global, distribution/network generality;
- no clean interior coordinated-public optimum in the central regime;
- welfare-externality sign does not reverse with the BR sign at the witness.

Only the first item blocks Stage 7.5.
