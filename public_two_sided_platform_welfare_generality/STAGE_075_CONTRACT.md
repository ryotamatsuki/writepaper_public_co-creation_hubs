# Stage 7.5 Contract

Stage 7.5 is **not yet authorized**.

The only required precondition is to resolve the institutional private-price blocker:

- identify a credible shared national private innovation/co-creation intermediary;
- document primary-source evidence that it charges the relevant focal-project/user side;
- establish that it is a meaningful substitute for regional public intermediation;
- document a credible competitive repricing margin.

If that blocker is resolved without changing the frozen model or P2-R4, Stage 7 may be upgraded to `GO TO STAGE 7.5`. Stage 7.5 itself may only make a freeze decision; no new mechanism/extensions.
