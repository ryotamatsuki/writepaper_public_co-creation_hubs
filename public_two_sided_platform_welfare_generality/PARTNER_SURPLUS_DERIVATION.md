# Partner Surplus Derivation

The Stage-3/4 architecture has partner cost `c~U[0,1]` and per-platform participation value `b_h`. A partner can multi-home, so surplus is additive across platforms.

For interior `0<b_h<1`,

`PS_h^P = ∫_0^{b_h}(b_h-c)dc=b_h^2/2`.

With two symmetric residence groups, each region owns half of the national partner population and receives `b_h^2/4` from platform `h`.

This derivation validates the Stage-4 shortcut and introduces no new partner utility.
