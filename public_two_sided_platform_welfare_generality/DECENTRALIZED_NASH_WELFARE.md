# Decentralized Nash Welfare

In G, each government maximizes `Wtilde_i(x_i,x_j)=W_i(x_i,x_j,p_T^*(x_i,x_j))`; the private platform chooses `p_T^*`.

Frozen witness re-evaluation:

- `x_1=x_2=0.684028`
- `p_T=0.0227467`
- `W_1=W_2=0.223019`
- `Pi_T=0.012603`
- `W^N=0.458642`

At the decentralized equilibrium, own reduced marginal welfare is zero by the government's FOC, but the national marginal value of an unilateral increase in `x_i` is about `+0.42277`. This is the cross-region/private-profit externality omitted by the decentralized government.

This is a benchmark/welfare implication, not a revived P1-R novelty claim.
