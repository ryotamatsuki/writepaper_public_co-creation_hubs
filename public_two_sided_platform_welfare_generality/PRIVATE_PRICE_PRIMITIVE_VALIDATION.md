# Private Price Primitive Validation

E:N BASE registration and facility use are free. Tokyo Innovation Base also states that facility use is free, apart from some fabrication-area charges. Thus the public-hub side does not validate a project-side fee.

The missing object is a **national private innovation intermediary** that (i) is a meaningful substitute for focal projects, (ii) charges the relevant side, and (iii) adjusts that fee competitively when regional public intermediation expands. No primary evidence establishing all three links was located in this Stage-7 pass.

Adjacent markets show the economic object is realistic: the private taxi app GO charges app/service fees and states that some charges vary by area, season, and supply-demand conditions.
