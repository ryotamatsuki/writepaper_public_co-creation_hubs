# Hostile Referee Attack

The paper's mathematical sign reversal may be valid, but the motivating institution has not been shown to contain the actor that makes the theorem economically distinctive. E:N BASE and Tokyo Innovation Base are free public facilities; the analysis requires a shared national private intermediary that charges the focal project side and reprices in response to local public expansion. Without primary evidence for that competitive object, the paper risks presenting a generic sequential-price-response theorem in co-creation-hub clothing. Moreover, the decentralized welfare wedge remains positive in the witness regardless of the BR sign, so the welfare contribution is narrower than the strategic result.

**Audit result:** the welfare-triviality attack fails, but the institutional-private-price attack survives and is the single Stage-7 blocker.
