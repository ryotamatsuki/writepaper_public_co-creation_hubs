# Evidence Limitations

1. No primary source located showing a shared national private innovation/co-creation intermediary reprices because regional public hubs expand.
2. Public hubs inspected (E:N BASE, TIB) are free-use institutions, so their own pricing does not validate `p_T`.
3. Cross-hub partner multihoming is plausible but not directly proven by the E:N BASE partner list.
4. Project single-homing is an interpretation at the focal project/program level.
5. Mobility/matching settings are generality analogues, not exact structural matches.
6. Generic-distribution/network results are local continuity results, not global universality claims.
