# Policy Scope and Limits

Supported policy interpretation:
- regional governments should not infer rival-investment incentives from network effects alone when a shared private intermediary can reprice;
- coordination value depends on the **externality**, not on whether best responses are complements or substitutes;
- private price flexibility can materially alter decentralized public investment incentives.

Not supported:
- regulate/fix `p_T`;
- subsidize partners;
- mandate interoperability;
- merge hubs;
- ban private platforms.

B3 is an identification benchmark, not an implemented policy instrument.
