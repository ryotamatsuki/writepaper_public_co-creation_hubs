# National Welfare Derivation

Summing the two regional project-surplus expressions subtracts the private fee exactly `p_T n_T^F` times. Adding `Pi_T=p_T n_T^F` cancels that term.

Therefore national welfare can be written without the transfer:

`W^N = real project surplus net of kappa_L/kappa_T + national partner surplus - gamma(x_1^2+x_2^2)/2`.

Equivalently,

`W^N=W_1+W_2+Pi_T`.

This identity is exact in the frozen model. A fall in `p_T` is not itself a national welfare gain. Only induced sorting, participation, network benefits, investment changes, and real costs matter.
