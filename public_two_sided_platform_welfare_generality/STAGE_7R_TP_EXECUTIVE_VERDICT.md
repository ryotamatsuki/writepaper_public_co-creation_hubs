## Welfare finding

The fee `p_T` is a regional outflow but a national transfer. With zero private real operating cost,

`W^N = W_1 + W_2 + Pi_T`

and the total private fee payment cancels exactly. Hence any national-welfare effect of endogenous pricing must operate through project sorting, network participation, investment responses, and real participation/investment costs.

At the frozen Stage-4 witness, deterministic re-evaluation gives:

| object | B3 (fixed p at G price) | G |
|---|---:|---:|
| x_1=x_2 | 0.656016 | 0.684028 |
| p_T | 0.0227467 | 0.0227467 |
| private project mass n_T^F | 0.610686 | 0.554063 |
| W_1=W_2 | 0.210910 | 0.223019 |
| Pi_T | 0.013891 | 0.012603 |
| W^N | 0.435711 | 0.458642 |

The B3 price is deliberately fixed at the G equilibrium fee; therefore the table identifies equilibrium investment-response effects, not a policy comparison between two fee levels.

At the decentralized G equilibrium, the reduced national marginal value of increasing one region's investment is approximately `+0.42277`: rival-region welfare effect `+0.44471` plus private-profit effect `-0.02194`. This is a real coordination wedge after transfer cancellation. It does **not** follow from `BR_G'<0`; strategic slope and welfare sign remain distinct.

## Generality

The result is not a knife-edge artifact of the uniform/linear specification in the local mathematical sense. For a generic `C^1` project CDF `F` with positive density at the relevant cutoffs, and for `C^1` partner-response functions with the same derivative signs, the equilibrium/cross-derivative map is continuous wherever the cutoff Jacobian, private-price SOC, and public SOCs remain nonsingular. Because the Stage-4 sign inequalities are strict, P2-R4 survives sufficiently small `C^1` perturbations. This establishes **local structural generality**, not a global theorem for arbitrary distributions/functions.

## Institutional finding

E:N BASE strongly supports public facilitation and broad partner participation, but its registration and facility use are free. Current primary-source evidence does not identify a shared national private innovation intermediary whose project-side fee both competes with E:N BASE and responds to E:N BASE/other regional-hub investment. Project single-homing is also only defensible as a focal-project/program approximation, not literal organizational exclusivity.

Adjacent mobility and platform-market settings demonstrate that fee-setting private intermediaries with variable service charges are institutionally plausible, but they do not cure the missing literal co-creation-hub mapping.

## Field-journal assessment

**Viable but needs disciplined framing.** The mathematical result has welfare bite through a real cross-regional coordination externality and local structural generality. The remaining blocker is singular and institutional: document a credible national private price-setting substitute in the motivating innovation-intermediation environment, or explicitly reframe the motivating application before Stage 7.5.

Stage 7.5 is **not yet authorized**.
