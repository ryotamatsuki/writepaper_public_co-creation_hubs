# Generality Setting B — Regional Public Matching Services vs National Private Matching Platform

A labor/business matching interpretation maps a vacancy/project engagement to the single-homing side and employers/advisers/support providers to the multi-homing side. Regional public agencies can invest in matching/facilitation capacity while a national private platform charges access/recruitment fees.

The architecture can reproduce the same strategic mechanism without adding congestion, dynamics, bargaining, compatibility, or another strategic instrument.

However Stage 7 did not locate primary evidence for the exact pair of two regional public investors plus one shared private price setter with observed price response. Therefore this setting establishes **theoretical portability**, not exact institutional identification.

Verdict: **PLAUSIBLE BUT EVIDENTIARILY WEAKER THAN SETTING A**.
