# Welfare Definitions

In region `i`, resident project surplus in the central `0 -> H_T -> H_i` regime is

`PS_i = ∫_s^{t_i}(z q_T-kappa_T-p_T) dz + ∫_{t_i}^1(z q_i-kappa_L) dz`.

Partner type is `c~U[0,1]`; participation in platform `h` occurs for `c <= b_h=n_h^P`. National partner surplus on platform `h` is therefore

`∫_0^{b_h}(b_h-c)dc = b_h^2/2`.

With symmetric residence ownership, each region receives one half: `b_h^2/4`.

Thus

`W_i = PS_i + (b_1^2+b_2^2+b_T^2)/4 - gamma x_i^2/2`.

Private profit is `Pi_T=p_T n_T^F`.

National welfare is

`W^N=W_1+W_2+Pi_T`

under the frozen assumption of zero private real operating cost.
