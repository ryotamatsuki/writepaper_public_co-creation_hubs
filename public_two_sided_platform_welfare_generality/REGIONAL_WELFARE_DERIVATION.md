# Regional Welfare Derivation

Let `a=kappa_T+p_T`, `s=a/q_T`, and `t_i` solve `t_i(q_i-q_T)=kappa_L-a`. Then

`PS_i = q_T(t_i^2-s^2)/2 - a(t_i-s) + q_i(1-t_i^2)/2 - kappa_L(1-t_i)`.

Regional welfare is

`W_i=PS_i + (b_1^2+b_2^2+b_T^2)/4 - gamma x_i^2/2`.

The private fee is correctly treated as a regional outflow because it enters `a`; it is not added back to `W_i`. Partner surplus is real surplus generated by the frozen partner utility, not revenue.

The region's reduced full-game objective is `Wtilde_i(x_i,x_j)=W_i(x_i,x_j,p_T^*(x_i,x_j))`.
