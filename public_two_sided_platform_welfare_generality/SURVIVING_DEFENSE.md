# Surviving Defense

Endogenous private pricing changes more than a label on reaction functions. In the frozen full game it changes the cross derivative of decentralized public marginal incentives enough to reverse strategic complementarity, while exact welfare accounting shows a separate, real cross-regional coordination externality remains after all private-fee transfers cancel. The strict sign result is locally stable to smooth perturbations of the project distribution and network-response functions.

The defense is deliberately narrow: it does not assert that the welfare externality reverses sign, that strategic substitutes are harmful, or that E:N BASE already supplies a verified private-price counterpart.
