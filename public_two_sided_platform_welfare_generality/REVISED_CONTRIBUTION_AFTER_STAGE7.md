# Revised Contribution after Stage 7

A defensible contribution statement is:

> We show that cross-side network feedback can make decentralized regional public investments strategic complements when a shared private intermediary's access fee is fixed, yet strategic substitutes when that profit-maximizing intermediary reprices. Exact welfare accounting separates this incentive reversal from welfare rankings: the fee is a national transfer, while public investment creates a real cross-regional coordination externality through allocation and network participation. The sign-reversal result is locally stable to smooth departures from the benchmark distribution and network functions.

Institutional application to public co-creation hubs remains conditional on identifying the shared private fee-setting substitute.
