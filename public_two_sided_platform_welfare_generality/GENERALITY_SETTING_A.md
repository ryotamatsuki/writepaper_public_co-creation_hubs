# Generality Setting A — Local Public Mobility Intermediation vs National Private Ride Platform

A mobility interpretation can map a focal trip to the single-homing project side and transport operators/drivers to the multihoming partner side. Local governments can invest in regional dispatch/MaaS coordination while a national private ride intermediary sets a user-side service/arrangement fee.

GO's official pricing materials show a project/user-side app service fee and explicitly state that service charges can vary by area, season and supply-demand conditions. This validates the economic plausibility of an endogenous private intermediary price.

What is not established here is an exact Japanese pair of two decentralized public mobility platforms competing with GO under the frozen timing. Hence this is a **structural analogue**, not direct empirical validation.

Verdict: **PLAUSIBLE GENERALITY SETTING**.
