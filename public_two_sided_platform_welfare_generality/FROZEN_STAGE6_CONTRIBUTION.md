# Frozen Stage-6 Contribution

The sole headline contribution is P2-R4:

`BR_i^{B3′} > 0 > BR_i^{G′}`

on a nonempty open parameter set.

B3 holds the national private platform fee fixed. G lets the profit-maximizing national private platform choose the fee endogenously. The positive B3 cross effect is generated by cross-side participation; endogenous pricing adds a negative reduced-form contribution that can dominate it.

The following remain supporting components only: two-sidedness, multiple platforms, regional welfare objectives, partner multihoming, the private fee response itself, and the negative follower-price term. P1-R and P3-P5 remain dead.
