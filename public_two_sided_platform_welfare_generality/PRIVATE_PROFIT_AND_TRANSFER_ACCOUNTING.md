# Private Profit and Transfer Accounting

`Pi_T=p_T n_T^F`.

Regional incidence: project users paying `p_T` transfer resources out of their region to the national private platform, so the payment lowers `W_i`.

National incidence: the same amount enters `Pi_T`. Under zero private operating cost, it is a pure internal transfer and cancels from `W^N`.

Consequently a policy statement based only on a lower private fee is invalid. National welfare changes only if the price response changes real allocation, network participation, public investment, or real costs.
