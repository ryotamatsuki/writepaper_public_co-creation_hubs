# Public Co-Creation Hub Mapping

E:N BASE is a strong match for:
- public regional operator;
- open membership/partner ecosystem;
- partner-side facilitation and matching;
- public investment in community-management/support capacity.

It is a weak or unverified match for:
- literal project single-homing;
- a shared national private substitute;
- endogenous private project-side pricing;
- observed private-price response to local public expansion.

Therefore the model should be described as a theory **motivated by** regional public co-creation intermediation, not as a literal structural model of E:N BASE until the private-alternative evidence is supplied.
