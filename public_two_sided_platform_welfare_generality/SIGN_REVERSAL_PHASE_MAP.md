# Sign-Reversal Phase Map

Define `M_B3` as the fixed-price public-public FOC cross derivative and `M_G=M_B3+P_price`.

Conceptual regions:
1. `M_B3<0`: fixed-price substitutes.
2. `M_B3=0`: no local fixed-price strategic interaction.
3. `M_B3>0, M_G>0`: complements survive endogenous pricing.
4. `M_B3>0>M_G`: **P2-R4 price-dominated substitutes**.

At `beta=0`, Stage 4 shows the relevant B3 cross effect collapses to zero. At `beta=.05`, the witness lies strictly in region 4. Continuity implies a nonempty neighborhood of region 4 but Stage 7 does not claim a closed-form global `beta*`.
