# Sign-Reversal Welfare Decomposition

Let the reduced government FOC be

`R_i(x_i,x_j)=d W_i(x_i,x_j,p^*(x_i,x_j))/dx_i`.

Its cross derivative in G can be written schematically as

`M_G = M_B3 + P_price`

where `M_B3` is the cross derivative holding `p_T` fixed and `P_price` collects chain-rule terms involving `p^*_{x_j}`, `p^*_{x_ix_j}`, and welfare derivatives with respect to price.

Stage 4 establishes `M_B3>0` and `M_G<0` at the witness. Stage 7 adds a welfare warning: this cross-FOC sign is not the cross-region welfare externality `dWtilde_j/dx_i`. At the same witness the latter is positive. Thus the strategic sign reversal changes marginal incentives without mechanically determining social desirability.
