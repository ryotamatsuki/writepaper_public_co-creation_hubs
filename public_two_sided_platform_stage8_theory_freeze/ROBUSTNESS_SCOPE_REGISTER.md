# Robustness Scope Register

Approved robustness is local only.

1. **Parameter open set:** all defining witness inequalities are strict; 20/20 deterministic ±0.5% perturbations preserve `BR_B3'>0>BR_G'`.
2. **Project distribution:** sufficiently small `C^1` perturbations of uniformity, with positive density at cutoffs and regular Jacobians/SOCs, preserve the strict reversal by IFT/continuity.
3. **Network functions:** sufficiently small `C^1` perturbations preserving relevant derivative signs and regularity preserve the reversal.
4. **Asymmetry:** small asymmetric action-state perturbations around the symmetric witness survive by continuity.

Not approved: arbitrary distributions, arbitrary nonlinear network functions, a global parameter theorem, arbitrary asymmetric regional primitives, or a heterogeneous-region global theorem.
