# Canonical Research Question

## Notation-free
When two regional governments invest in public innovation intermediation while sharing a private metropolitan innovation hub as an outside option, can cross-side network feedback make their investments strategic complements when the private hub's access price is fixed, but strategic substitutes when that private hub optimally reprices?
