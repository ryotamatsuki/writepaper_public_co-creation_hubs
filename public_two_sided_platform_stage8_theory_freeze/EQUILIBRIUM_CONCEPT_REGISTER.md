# Equilibrium Concept Register

| Regime | Private price | Public choices | Equilibrium object | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B3 | fixed `p_T=bar p` | `x_1,x_2` noncooperative | public Nash equilibrium conditional on fixed fee and endogenous participation | supporting benchmark |
| G | `p_T^*(x_1,x_2)` maximizes `Pi_T` after public choices | `x_1,x_2` anticipate private response | SPNE; reduced public welfare `W~_i` | headline full game |
| B1/B2/B4 | as defined in Stage 4 | nested restrictions | supporting/essentiality only | not headline |

In G: `W~_{i,x_i}=W_{i,x_i}+W_{i,p}p^*_{x_i}=0` at an interior public optimum.

Do not conflate: a welfare cross-partial, a reduced cross derivative, a best-response slope, a rival-welfare externality, or a planner/national-welfare derivative.
