# Utility, Participation, and Costs

Projects: unit mass in each region, `z~U[0,1]`, outside payoff zero. A focal project chooses one primary route among `0,H_1,H_2,H_T`.

`U_rh(z)=z[v+alpha n_h^P]-kappa_rh-1{h=T}p_T`.

Access/coordination costs: `kappa_rr=kappa_L`, `kappa_rj=kappa_L+tau`, `kappa_1T=kappa_2T=kappa_T`.

Partners: platform-specific participation cost `c~U[0,1]`; they may multihome. Interior equations:
`n_i^P=rho+x_i+beta n_i^F`,
`n_T^P=rho_T+beta n_T^F`.

Public control: `x_i in [0,1]`; cost `C_i=gamma x_i^2/2`.
Private control: `p_T>=0`; profit `Pi_T=p_T n_T^F`.

Uniform distributions, quadratic public cost and zero private real operating cost are baseline tractability/accounting assumptions, not independent novelty claims.
