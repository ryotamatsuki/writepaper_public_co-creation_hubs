# Closest-Paper Distinction Freeze

López and Vives (2019) already establish that endogenous later-stage price responses enter first-stage investment incentives and can contribute negatively, so the negative follower-price channel is not new. Zogheib and Bourreau (2021), Mun (2019), Hahn and Kim (2026) and related work also narrow claims based on mixed public/private objectives, multiple regional governments, network/platform competition and endogenous downstream pricing.

The frozen distinction is narrower: in the same two-public/one-private strategic architecture, cross-side participation generates a positive public-public strategic relation when the third actor's price is fixed, while restoring that actor's optimizing price response can overturn the positive term and make the same public investments strategic substitutes. Stage 6 found no Distance-0/1 theorem or direct relabeling delivering this nested-benchmark reversal.

Editorial status: distinct enough for a focused paper, not a broad new theory of price responses. **Combination novelty remains a MAJOR RISK.**
