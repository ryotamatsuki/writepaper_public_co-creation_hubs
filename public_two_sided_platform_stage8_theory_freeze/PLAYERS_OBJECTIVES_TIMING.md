# Players, Objectives, and Timing

## Strategic decision-makers
1. `G_1`: chooses `x_1` to maximize regional welfare `W_1`.
2. `G_2`: chooses `x_2` to maximize regional welfare `W_2`.
3. Private operator `H_T`: chooses `p_T>=0` to maximize `Pi_T=p_T n_T^F`.

`H_1` and `H_2` are public platform/routes governed by `G_1,G_2`; they are not additional strategic decision-makers. This clarifies Stage-4 shorthand without changing the game.

## Timing
Exact verified Stage-4 timing:
`(x_1,x_2) -> p_T -> participation fixed point/project sorting -> surplus`.

The two public investments are first-stage choices. The private fee is a follower choice conditional on both investments. Participation/sorting then solves the two-sided fixed point. Payoffs realize last.

## Equilibrium concept
Subgame-perfect Nash equilibrium (SPNE). In G, public incentives use reduced welfare `W~_i(x_1,x_2)=W_i(x_1,x_2,p_T^*(x_1,x_2))`.
