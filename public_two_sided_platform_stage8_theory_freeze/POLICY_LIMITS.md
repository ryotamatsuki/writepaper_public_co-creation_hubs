# Policy Limits

The frozen model supports incentive and coordination interpretation, not direct policy prescriptions.

It does **not** justify, without new analysis: fixing/regulating `p_T`; subsidizing private or partner participation; mandating interoperability; merging regional hubs; banning private hubs; subsidizing metropolitan access; imposing national coordination; or mandating project exclusivity.

B3 is a controlled identification benchmark that removes private repricing. It is not a literal policy proposal.

The positive national coordination wedge at the witness establishes that decentralized incentives can differ from aggregate welfare incentives; it does not identify an optimal corrective instrument.
