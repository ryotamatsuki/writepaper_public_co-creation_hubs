# Institutional Interpretation Register

Canonical mapping:
- regional governments operate/fund public innovation intermediation `H_i`;
- `x_i` represents facilitation capacity such as coordinators, matching, events, partner recruitment and programme quality;
- `H_T` is a shared metropolitan private/commercial innovation hub accessible from both regions;
- `p_T` is an effective paid membership/access price for a workspace+network/intermediation bundle;
- project-side mass counts focal projects using a primary route;
- partner-side mass represents investors, experts, universities, large firms and support organizations.

Supporting analogue class from Stage 7: CIC Tokyo, SHIBUYA QWS, SENQ; Level39 and international CIC sites; STATION F; public-side Helsinki and Barcelona. QUINTBRIDGE and Xport remain boundary/counterexamples.

Do not claim direct observed competition between any named pair, observed fee cuts caused by regional investment, firm-level exclusivity, universal partner multihoming, or literal profit maximization by every real hub.
