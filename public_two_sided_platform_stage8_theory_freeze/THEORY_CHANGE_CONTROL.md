# Theory Change Control

After Stage 8, no substantive theory change may occur silently.

Any proposal must record:
1. exact change;
2. economic reason;
3. affected primitive/equation;
4. affected proposition;
5. affected welfare claim;
6. affected verification;
7. affected literature positioning;
8. affected institutional interpretation;
9. stages that must be reopened;
10. approval/rejection decision.

Changes requiring re-gating include project multihoming; distance/travel costs; a different private objective or paying side; a new price instrument; structural regional asymmetry; a new planner objective; new distribution/network theorems; another hub; a changed project utility; or treating B3 as a policy regime.

Notation cleanup and unambiguous restatement are allowed only if payoff/equilibrium content is unchanged. Any change to P2-R4, welfare scope, timing, player objectives, novelty position, or institutional primitives requires reopening the relevant Stage 4–7.5 gate.
