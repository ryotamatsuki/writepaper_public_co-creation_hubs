# Working Title Decision

Maximum three candidates were considered.

1. **Strategic Interaction among Public Innovation Hubs under Private Repricing**
2. Public Innovation Hubs with Endogenous Private-Hub Pricing
3. Regional Public Innovation Hubs and Metropolitan Private Competition

## Recommended title

**Strategic Interaction among Public Innovation Hubs under Private Repricing**

Reasons:

- it puts the economic object — strategic interaction — before the application label;
- “private repricing” points directly to what changes between B3 and G without claiming that price responses themselves are novel;
- it does not overstate the paper as generic platform theory;
- it avoids making “metropolitan” a theoretical primitive;
- it remains compatible with the documented regional-public / commercial-metropolitan institutional interpretation.

Stage 8 may clean notation or punctuation but should not materially reframe the contribution without reopening Stage 7.5.
