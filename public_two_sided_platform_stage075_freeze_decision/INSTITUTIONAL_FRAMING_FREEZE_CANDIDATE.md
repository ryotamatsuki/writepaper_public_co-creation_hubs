# Institutional Framing Freeze Candidate

## Recommended canonical language

**regional public innovation hubs and a shared metropolitan private innovation hub**

This is preferable to “national private platform.” The frozen game requires the private route to be a common outside option accessible from both public regions, to possess network/intermediation value, and to have an operator-chosen positive access price. National territorial coverage is not required.

## Approved mapping

- `G_i`: regional/local government;
- `H_i`: regional public innovation hub or public innovation intermediary;
- `x_i`: facilitation capacity such as coordinators, matching, events, partner recruitment, and program quality;
- `H_T`: shared commercial metropolitan innovation hub;
- `p_T`: effective paid membership/access price for the innovation-intermediation bundle;
- `n_h^F`: focal innovation projects using route `h`;
- `n_h^P`: partners, investors, experts, or supporting organizations connected to route `h`.

## Scope discipline

Public and private institutions may cooperate at the ecosystem level while substituting for the focal project's primary intermediation route. Project single-homing means one primary route for one focal project, not firm-level exclusivity. Partner multihoming remains an institutional abstraction supported by non-exclusive ecosystem participation, not a claim that every partner simultaneously belongs to every hub.

## Direct analogues

CIC Tokyo is the primary Japanese direct analogue; SHIBUYA QWS is supporting. Level39 and international CIC campuses support international portability. Helsinki and Barcelona support the public-intermediation side.

## Freeze verdict

**APPROVE this language for Stage 8, with all caveats preserved.**
