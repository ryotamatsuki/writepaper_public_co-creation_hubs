# Combination-Novelty Hard Kill

## Hostile question

Is P2-R4 merely “known network complementarity + known follower-price response”?

## Evidence against a mechanical-combination verdict

1. The headline object is a **comparison of the same public-public best-response relation across nested games**, not the existence of either component.
2. Two-sided feedback is essential to the positive B3 side: at `beta=0`, the relevant fixed-price cross effect collapses to zero.
3. Endogenous private pricing is essential to the G side: holding the price fixed removes the price-response term that creates the reversal.
4. The result is a dominance statement, `M_B3>0` but `M_B3+P_price<0`; the mechanism requires the known components to interact strongly enough to change the qualitative strategic type of the public game.
5. Stage 6 found no Distance-0/1 theorem, no direct relabeling, and no mechanical second-public extension that produces P2-R4.

## Evidence for the hostile view

1. Neither fixed-price network complementarity nor the negative price-response contribution is independently novel.
2. The current paper does not derive a global closed-form parameter threshold for the reversal.
3. The novelty margin is therefore narrow and can be perceived as a comparative-static synthesis rather than a foundational new mechanism.

## Verdict

**SURVIVES HARD KILL — GENUINE BUT NARROW QUALITATIVE BENCHMARK REVERSAL.**

Referee classification: **MAJOR RISK, NOT FATAL**.

The manuscript must present the reversal itself as the contribution and explicitly concede that both underlying channels have predecessors.
