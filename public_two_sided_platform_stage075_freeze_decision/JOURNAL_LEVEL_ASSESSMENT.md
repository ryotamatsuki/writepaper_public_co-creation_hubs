# Journal-Level Assessment

No new journal search is performed in Stage 7.5, and no single outlet is declared canonical here.

## Primary audience

**Regional/urban economics** is the most natural primary audience because the strategic players are decentralized regional governments, the key object is interregional public-investment interaction, and the welfare result is a cross-regional coordination externality.

Secondary audiences are **industrial organization / platform economics** through private repricing and two-sided participation, **public economics** through decentralized welfare objectives, and **innovation economics** through the institutional application.

## Level assessment

### A. Ambitious field-journal level

A Journal of Regional Science / Regional Science and Urban Economics type target is defensible as an ambitious benchmark if the final manuscript makes the interregional strategic mechanism central, proves every stated proposition at its exact approved status, and keeps the institutional section disciplined.

### B. Realistic field-journal level

A solid specialist field outlet in regional economics, innovation economics, or industrial organization is the most realistic current positioning. The project has enough theory and welfare content for a full article, but the novelty margin is narrow and the main sign-reversal existence result is local rather than global.

### C. Fallback

A compact specialist theory/innovation/regional note remains a credible fallback if referees treat the combination novelty as insufficient for a full field-journal article.

## Verdict

**Serious field-journal path: credible. Top-general-theory positioning: not supported.**
