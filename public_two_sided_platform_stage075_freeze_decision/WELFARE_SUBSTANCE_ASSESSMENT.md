# Welfare Substance Assessment

## Question

Does P2-R4 matter economically if the strategic-sign reversal does not generate a welfare-sign reversal?

## Answer

Yes, but the welfare contribution is secondary and must be framed carefully.

Exact Stage-7 accounting shows that the private access fee is a regional outflow but an aggregate transfer under the frozen zero-private-real-cost model. Therefore welfare cannot be manufactured by counting a fee cut as a social gain. After this cancellation, the decentralized G equilibrium still has a real marginal coordination wedge: at the frozen witness, the rival-region welfare effect is about `+0.44471`, the private-profit effect is about `-0.02194`, and the national marginal effect of an additional unit of one region's investment is about `+0.42277` after the investing government's own reduced FOC is zero.

This establishes a substantive distinction between **strategic interaction** and **welfare externality**. Endogenous repricing can turn public investments into strategic substitutes even while the cross-regional welfare externality remains positive.

## What is not established

- no welfare-externality sign reversal;
- no theorem that strategic substitutes are socially harmful;
- no global G-over-B3 welfare ranking;
- no clean interior coordinated-public threshold;
- no revival of P1-R as a novelty claim.

## Verdict

**SUBSTANTIVE, NON-MECHANICAL SECONDARY VALUE.**

The welfare section can support a full paper because it disciplines interpretation and identifies a real coordination problem, but it must not be advertised as a second headline theorem.
