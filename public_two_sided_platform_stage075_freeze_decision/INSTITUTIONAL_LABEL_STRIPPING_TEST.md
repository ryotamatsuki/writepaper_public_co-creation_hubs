# Institutional Label-Stripping Test

## Test

Remove the words “Ehime,” “co-creation hub,” “CIC,” “Tokyo,” and “metropolitan.” Does the economic result survive as a meaningful object?

## Stripped structure

- two decentralized public decision makers choose costly investments;
- each maximizes a distinct local/regional welfare objective;
- focal users/projects choose among the public routes, a shared private route, and nonparticipation;
- cross-side participation makes public investment affect network value;
- the shared private intermediary sets an access price;
- the fixed-price and optimizing-price versions of the same game generate opposite signs for the public-public strategic relation on a nonempty local region.

Nothing in P2-R4 requires the labels “Ehime” or “CIC.” “Metropolitan” is an institutional interpretation of the shared outside option, not a mathematical primitive.

## Why retain the innovation-hub application

The application supplies a disciplined economic environment in which the player objectives, facilitation investment, cross-side participation, paid commercial outside option, and focal-route substitution are interpretable. It should motivate and discipline the model, not carry the novelty claim.

## Verdict

**PASS. Novelty does not disappear under label stripping.**
