# Full-Paper Value Assessment

## Reader payoff — maximum four contributions

1. **Qualitative strategic-interaction result.** The public-public relation can switch from strategic complements under a fixed private price to strategic substitutes under optimizing private repricing.
2. **Mechanism decomposition.** The paper separates the positive cross-side network term from the negative endogenous-price contribution and shows how the latter can dominate the former.
3. **Welfare/coordination discipline.** Strategic substitution is not equated with social harm; exact transfer accounting leaves a real cross-regional coordination externality whose sign is a separate object.
4. **Institutional and empirical interpretation.** The model maps to a documented class of regional public innovation intermediaries facing priced commercial metropolitan hubs and generates falsifiable restrictions on how private price flexibility changes public strategic responses.

## Why a full paper rather than a note

The project now has a coherent Introduction question, a minimal model, nested benchmarks, one sharply delimited proposition, essentiality results, a hostile prior-art distinction, a nontrivial welfare section, local generality results, an international institutional interpretation, and empirical predictions. These are distinct sections with nonduplicative economic content.

## Why not oversell

The headline theorem is narrow, the main existence certificate is local/computer-assisted rather than a global closed-form parameter theorem, and the two constituent channels are known. The manuscript must therefore be compact and disciplined.

## Verdict

**FULL PAPER VALUE: YES, AS A FOCUSED FIELD-THEORY PAPER.**
