# Essential versus Tractability Assumptions

| Assumption / feature | Stage-7.5 classification | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| two decentralized public investors | **ESSENTIAL** | P2-R4 is a public-public best-response theorem; one public actor cannot generate the target cross-response. |
| regional welfare objectives / noncooperative public choice | **ESSENTIAL** | Separate public best responses disappear under a coordinated objective; Stage 4 verifies a material decentralized/coordinated incentive difference. |
| shared private outside option | **ESSENTIAL** | It is the common third route through which public investment changes private demand and feeds back across regions. |
| endogenous private price in G | **ESSENTIAL** | Fixing the price removes the price-response contribution and therefore the benchmark-to-full-game reversal. |
| cross-side network feedback (`beta>0`) | **ESSENTIAL for P2-R4** | At `beta=0`, the relevant B3 cross effect collapses to zero; pricing may still re-couple governments but not from complements to substitutes. |
| three-player / two-public-plus-private structure | **ESSENTIAL to the headline object** | It combines a public-public strategic relation with a distinct private price setter. |
| project single-homing | **SUPPORTING ARCHITECTURE** | It makes the focal-route allocation and cutoffs sharp; Stage 7.5 does not establish that the reversal would fail under project multihoming. |
| partner multi-homing | **SUPPORTING ARCHITECTURE** | It gives the selected two-sided participation structure institutional meaning; theorem-level necessity beyond the frozen architecture is not established. |
| central smooth allocation regime | **SUPPORTING / PROOF DOMAIN** | The certified result is established on this regular interior regime; no claim is made over all regime boundaries. |
| uniform project heterogeneity | **TRACTABILITY / NORMALIZATION** | Stage 7 shows the result survives sufficiently small smooth non-uniform perturbations. |
| linear network participation function | **TRACTABILITY / NORMALIZATION** | Stage 7 shows local survival under small smooth nonlinear perturbations with the same derivative signs. |
| symmetric primitive regions | **TRACTABILITY / NORMALIZATION** | Small asymmetric action states survive; structural region-specific primitive asymmetry is not proved. |
| quadratic public investment cost | **TRACTABILITY / NORMALIZATION** | It supplies a regular convex cost and clean SOCs; no evidence makes the quadratic form itself the mechanism. |
| zero private operating cost | **TRACTABILITY / WELFARE ACCOUNTING NORMALIZATION** | It makes fee revenue an exact aggregate transfer; it is not the source of P2-R4. |
| “metropolitan” label for the private hub | **INSTITUTIONAL INTERPRETATION ONLY** | The mathematics requires a common accessible private outside option, not national or metropolitan territorial coverage. |

No Stage-7.5 claim upgrades the supporting assumptions into theorem-level essentiality.
