# Hostile Field-Editor Attacks

| Attack | Verdict | Editorial reason |
|---|---|---|
| A. “The negative price-response term is already known from sequential games.” | **SURVIVES BUT MANAGEABLE** | Correct for P2-R3; the paper must claim novelty only for the benchmark-to-full-game public-public reversal. |
| B. “Fixed-price complementarity is standard network economics.” | **SURVIVES BUT MANAGEABLE** | Correct for P2-R1; it is benchmark structure, not a contribution by itself. |
| C. “Combining two known components does not create a paper.” | **MAJOR RISK** | The defense is the qualitative change in the same public-public strategic relation, plus essentiality and no Distance-0/1 predecessor. This remains the paper's main editorial vulnerability. |
| D. “The reversal exists only around one calibration.” | **FAILS as stated** | Strict inequalities, 20/20 local perturbations, IFT continuity, and smooth distribution/network perturbations establish a nonempty local neighborhood. Globality remains unclaimed. |
| E. “There is no welfare reversal, so the strategic reversal is economically unimportant.” | **SURVIVES BUT MANAGEABLE** | No welfare reversal is claimed; a real coordination wedge remains after transfer cancellation and shows the strategic and welfare objects differ. |
| F. “The planner result is incomplete.” | **SURVIVES BUT MANAGEABLE** | Correct; no clean interior planner theorem is claimed. The welfare section is deliberately limited to verified local coordination implications. |
| G. “The institutional mapping is opportunistic.” | **FAILS in fatal form** | The blocker-resolution audit documents priced commercial hubs and public intermediation internationally and explicitly retains counterexamples and caveats. |
| H. “Project single-homing is too strong.” | **SURVIVES BUT MANAGEABLE** | It is a focal-project primary-route abstraction, not firm-level exclusivity; multi-route projects remain outside scope. |
| I. “The model is really about generic platforms, not public innovation hubs.” | **SURVIVES BUT MANAGEABLE** | The mechanism is abstract enough to survive label stripping, but regional welfare objectives and facilitation investment give the innovation-hub application real content. |
| J. “The model is too specific to innovation hubs to be general theory.” | **SURVIVES BUT MANAGEABLE** | Mathematical novelty does not depend on the labels, and the constituent institutional class appears internationally. |
| K. “The private hub's profit-maximization assumption is too literal.” | **SURVIVES BUT MANAGEABLE** | Treat `max Pi_T` as a reduced-form approximation for selected commercial operators, strongest for CIC/Level39, not for all hubs. |
| L. “Without observed private fee responses, the mechanism is empirically unmotivated.” | **SURVIVES BUT MANAGEABLE** | Price existence and discretion are documented; `p^*_{T,x_i}<0` is a falsifiable model prediction, not a claimed historical fact. |

## Overall verdict

No attack is **FATAL**. Attack C is the strongest **MAJOR RISK** and must remain explicit in the manuscript and Stage-8 freeze.
