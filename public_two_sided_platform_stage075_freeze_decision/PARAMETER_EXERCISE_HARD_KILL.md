# Parameter-Exercise Hard Kill

## Attack

“`BR_i^{B3′}>0>BR_i^{G′}` is only a parameterized sign flip around one calibration.”

## Audit

The literal “one calibration” attack fails. The Stage-4 witness is a regular interior point with strict participation inequalities, negative private/public SOCs, and best-response slopes bounded away from zero. A deterministic 20-draw ±0.5% perturbation exercise preserved the reversal in every draw. Because the equilibrium system is smooth and locally nonsingular, continuity implies a nonempty neighborhood around the witness. Stage 7 further shows that sufficiently small `C^1` departures from the uniform project distribution and the linear network functions preserve the strict sign inequalities.

The stronger attack, however, survives as a scope limitation. The project does not have a global theorem over arbitrary parameter values, distributions, network functions, or regime boundaries. The certified proposition is an **open-set/local existence result**.

## Verdict

**SURVIVES HARD KILL — MORE THAN A SINGLE CALIBRATION, LESS THAN A GLOBAL SIGN THEOREM.**

This is sufficient for a focused field-theory paper if proof status is stated exactly. It is not sufficient for claims of universal strategic substitution under endogenous private pricing.
