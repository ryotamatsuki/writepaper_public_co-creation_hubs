# Generality Assessment

P2-R4 is not a theorem for arbitrary environments. The approved generality is deliberately local.

## Mathematical generality already established

- sufficiently small smooth perturbations of the uniform project distribution preserve the reversal when density is positive at the cutoffs and the relevant Jacobians/SOCs remain nonsingular;
- sufficiently small smooth perturbations of the linear network functions preserve the reversal when the relevant derivative signs and regularity remain intact;
- small asymmetric action states survive by continuity.

These results are enough to reject the claim that P2-R4 is an exact uniform-linear knife edge. They do not establish global robustness to large nonlinearities, arbitrary distributions, structural region-specific primitive asymmetry, or regime switching.

## Institutional generality already established

The relevant constituent class is not limited to Ehime or Japan: local/municipal public innovation intermediation and priced commercial metropolitan innovation hubs have documented analogues in multiple countries. No claim is made that any foreign city reproduces the full three-player game literally.

## Verdict

**LOCAL STRUCTURAL GENERALITY + CREDIBLE INSTITUTIONAL-CLASS GENERALITY.**

This is sufficient for the intended focused field-paper framing. It is not sufficient for a universal platform-theory claim.
