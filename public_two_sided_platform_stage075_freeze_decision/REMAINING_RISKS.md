# Remaining Risks after Stage 7.5

No fatal risk remains under the current record, but the following must be visible in the manuscript.

## 1. Combination novelty — MAJOR RISK

The positive network channel and negative follower-price channel are individually known. The manuscript succeeds only if referees value the nested-benchmark reversal of the public-public strategic relation as a distinct economic result. This is the largest risk.

## 2. Local rather than global result — MATERIAL BUT MANAGEABLE

P2-R4 is certified on a nonempty regular local region and under small smooth perturbations. It is not a global parameter theorem and should never be written as one.

## 3. Focal-project single-homing — MANAGEABLE

The empirical institution permits firms to interact with multiple hubs. The model must be interpreted at the focal project's primary intermediation-route level.

## 4. Partner multihoming — MANAGEABLE

Institutional non-exclusivity supports the abstraction, but simultaneous multihoming is not directly documented partner-by-partner.

## 5. Profit-maximization approximation — MANAGEABLE

`max Pi_T` fits selected commercial hub operators as a reduced-form objective but should not be generalized to public, nonprofit, mission-driven, or zero-price hubs.

## 6. No observed public-investment-induced fee response — MANAGEABLE

The existence of positive prices and price discretion is documented; the negative optimal price response to public expansion remains a model prediction and potential empirical test.

## 7. Welfare scope — MANAGEABLE

The paper has a real coordination wedge but no welfare-sign reversal, no clean planner threshold, and no global G-versus-B3 welfare dominance.

## Overall

These risks lower the appropriate journal ambition and require disciplined exposition. They do not justify returning to model development at Stage 7.5.
