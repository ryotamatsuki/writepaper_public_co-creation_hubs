This is a narrow GO, not a claim that the project now contains a global theorem or a new generic theory of two-sided platforms. The full-paper case rests on one qualitative strategic result, P2-R4: the same two decentralized regional public investments can be strategic complements when the shared private hub's access price is held fixed and strategic substitutes when the private hub optimally reprices.

The result survives the Stage-7.5 hard kill for four reasons. First, it is not a single calibrated point: the Stage-4 witness is regular, all relevant inequalities are strict, deterministic local perturbations preserve the reversal, and Stage 7 establishes local continuity to smooth changes in the project distribution and network functions. Second, Stage 6 found no Distance-0/1 theorem or direct corollary for the fixed-price-to-endogenous-price public-public sign reversal, even though the positive network channel and the negative price-response channel are individually known. Third, exact welfare accounting leaves a real cross-regional coordination externality after transfer cancellation. Fourth, the institutional class survives label stripping and has documented Japanese and international analogues.

## Editorial qualification

The strongest remaining referee risk is **combination novelty**: a skeptical referee may argue that the paper combines standard network complementarity with a known follower-price response and obtains a sign switch. That attack is **MAJOR but not FATAL** because the nested benchmark comparison changes the public-public strategic relation itself, the required two-sided feedback is essential to the positive benchmark, and no inspected predecessor delivers the reversal as a direct relabeling or one-line corollary.

The paper should therefore be positioned as a focused field-theory contribution, not as a broad platform-theory breakthrough. A serious regional/urban or innovation/IO field-journal path is credible if the manuscript keeps P2-R4 central, treats welfare as a substantive implication rather than a second novelty theorem, and preserves all killed-claim boundaries.
