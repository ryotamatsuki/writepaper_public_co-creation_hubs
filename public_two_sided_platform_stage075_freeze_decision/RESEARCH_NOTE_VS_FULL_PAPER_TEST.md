# Research Note versus Full Paper Test

## Case for a research note

- only one primary novelty proposition survives;
- both component mechanisms have close predecessors;
- no global welfare-ranking theorem or planner threshold survives;
- the main reversal is an open-set/local existence result rather than a universal closed-form theorem.

## Case for a full paper

- P2-R4 changes the strategic type of the public game across a clean nested benchmark and full game;
- no Distance-0/1 predecessor was located after a 35-paper/model proposition-level audit;
- essentiality is economically interpretable: cross-side feedback is required for the positive benchmark, and endogenous pricing is required for the reversal;
- the result is not a single numerical point or an exact functional-form knife edge;
- welfare accounting produces a real coordination implication rather than a transfer artifact;
- the motivating institutional class is credible in Japan and abroad;
- the model generates empirical restrictions without requiring a new extension.

## Decision

**FULL PAPER dominates RESEARCH NOTE at the present stage.**

Fallback to a compact specialist note remains sensible only if external referees reject the combination-novelty margin or if manuscript construction reveals that the benchmark/full-game and welfare material cannot sustain a coherent full-length exposition. Stage 7.5 itself does not identify such a fatal deficiency.
