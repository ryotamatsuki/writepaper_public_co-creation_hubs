# Direct Hub Function vs Intermediate Behavior vs Outcome

## Layer 1 — Direct hub action

**Canonical direct object: productive matching technology.**

The hub changes how effectively heterogeneous agents with complementary resources/capabilities are converted into productive collaborations.

Institutional microfoundations that may sit behind this object:

- search/partner discovery;
- screening and information provision;
- brokerage across organizational/network boundaries;
- translation across knowledge domains;
- coordination support;
- relationship/trust formation;
- external-ecosystem access.

These are not separately frozen in the baseline model.

## Layer 2 — Intermediate behavior

**Formation of a productive collaboration / match.**

This is the economic behavior called “co-creation” in the model ontology. It is not equivalent to attending an event, registering at a facility, or being a member of a network. A match must combine complementary resources/capabilities and create an economically productive relationship.

## Layer 3 — Immediate collaboration value

The match creates joint economic value because the matched resources are complementary. This property is conceptual and must be present, but the next stage should choose the smallest representation of that value.

Potential interpretations (not yet frozen):

- productivity/cost reduction;
- innovation capability;
- new-project value;
- access to a missing input/resource.

## Layer 4 — Secondary systemic effect

**Shared partner-pool / cross-hub referral externality** is the only retained secondary candidate. It says one intermediary may make another more valuable by expanding the effective set/quality of potential matches available across hubs.

This is distinguished from a generic utility-side network effect.

## Layer 5 — Downstream market interaction

Beneficiary firms whose capabilities change after collaboration compete strategically in a downstream market. Profits must therefore be endogenous to that competition in the eventual formal model.

No product-market functional form is frozen here.

## Explicitly not direct hub functions

- pure R&D spillover;
- absorptive capacity;
- generic network utility;
- productivity gain itself;
- Cournot output;
- consumer surplus.

These are outcomes, downstream mechanisms, or possible extensions rather than what the intermediary directly does.
