## Why M2 wins

### Institutional fit

Explicit matching or functionally equivalent connection appears repeatedly in the institutional sample: E:N BASE community-manager matching; NEXs cross-region connections; Setouchi-i-Base business matching and partner discovery; Osaka Innovation Hub matching/meetups; FGN human-resource matching; Aichi/STATION Ai partner-hub co-creation formation; TIB fit-based support matching and TEAMUP; Interface Scotland's explicit search for the best academic match.

### Economic fit

The top-level ontology says co-creation is a **productive collaboration among complementary agents**. A matching technology directly maps the intermediary's action into the probability/quality of forming that collaboration. This avoids assuming that the hub directly creates R&D, knowledge spillovers, or productivity.

### Minimality

A single matching technology can take search, screening, brokerage and some coordination as microfoundations. A generic scalar “collaboration friction” is even smaller but too opaque: it erases heterogeneity and complementarity, both central to the institutional definition of co-creation.

### Competition compatibility

The same primitive naturally separates two levels of strategic interaction:

1. **hub competition:** overlapping matching opportunities/users make services substitute one another;
2. **beneficiary-firm competition:** successful matches alter firm capability before downstream market competition.

No particular product-market conduct assumption is needed at this stage.

## Why M6 is the only secondary candidate

M2 alone naturally creates cannibalization when hubs match overlapping users to overlapping partner sets. It does **not automatically create a positive cross-hub effect**. The institutional evidence for inter-hub cooperation instead points to cross-hub referral, shared ecosystem access and pool expansion: STATION Ai/partner hubs explicitly cooperate to build a broader ecosystem; NEXs links regional actors to a Tokyo/national network; Feser's Berlin labs cooperate for specialization, learning and access to missing knowledge/resources.

Therefore the single secondary candidate is a **shared matching-pool/cross-hub referral externality**. This is narrower than a generic network effect and has a direct matching interpretation.

## Anti-result-driven test

Even if M2 + M6 eventually fails to generate an interior strategic complement/substitute switch, the institutional evidence would still support M2 as the correct direct representation and M6 as a real cross-hub cooperation channel. Therefore neither is retained solely to manufacture the desired theorem.
