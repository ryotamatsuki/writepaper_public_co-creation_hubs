# Next-Stage Routing

## Stage 0M verdict

**PASS — INSTITUTIONAL FUNCTION IDENTIFIED.**

The most general direct economic function across the sampled public/regional co-creation and innovation-intermediary cases is the formation of connections/matches among heterogeneous actors, not a direct R&D spillover or generic network-utility effect.

## Stage 1M verdict

**PASS — CANONICAL PRIMITIVE FREEZE.**

Direct primitive:

> **Hub provision improves productive matching technology among complementary agents.**

Single secondary candidate:

> **Shared matching-pool / cross-hub referral externality.**

Negative cross-hub mechanism:

> **Overlap/cannibalization of productive matching opportunities.**

## Stage 2M verdict

**B2 — STRUCTURAL FORMAL-THEORY GAP.**

Matching, search intermediation, platform competition, innovation intermediation and downstream-intermediary models are all mature individually. The documented search did not find the full decentralized-public-hub + productive-match + cross-hub duality + downstream-firm-competition architecture.

## Routing decision

**GO TO MINIMAL FORMAL MODEL CONSTRUCTION.**

The next stage should be a clean Stage 3M–4M rather than a continuation of the old Gateway/Cournot equations.

### Stage 3M — mechanism specification

Freeze only:

1. two decentralized regional/public principals;
2. one productive-matching intermediary option per region;
3. heterogeneous/complementary agents whose collaboration has positive value;
4. overlapping intermediation opportunities;
5. shared-pool/referral effect as the only candidate positive cross-hub channel;
6. downstream strategic firm competition after collaboration;
7. regional welfare and hub provision cost.

Do not yet add R&D, absorptive capacity, generic network utility, differentiated products, dynamics or more than two regions.

### Stage 4M — minimal-model hard kill

Required tests:

1. Can the direct matching primitive alone create only substitution? If yes, document it rather than rescuing the model.
2. Is the shared-pool/referral channel genuinely necessary for complementarity?
3. Can the model produce both strategic signs on nonempty interior parameter regions without tuning an unrelated product-market parameter?
4. Does downstream firm competition materially affect the public-hub strategic relationship?
5. Is any result just a specialization of Caillaud–Jullien/Damiano–Li plus a standard public-input game?
6. Does removing downstream competition eliminate a proposition or only change welfare magnitudes?
7. Are firm profits fully endogenous?

## Next novelty discipline

Do **not** run a final proposition-level novelty claim before Stage 4M yields a precise theorem. Once the minimal theorem exists, return to Caillaud–Jullien, Damiano–Li, Calcagnini et al., public-input competition and downstream-intermediary theory with an exact specialization test.
