## Evidence highlights

- **E:N BASE:** official FAQ defines co-creation as actors bringing knowledge, technology and networks together; official member page explicitly provides community-manager matching, workshops, pitches and meetups.
- **NEXs Tokyo:** officially connects startups nationwide with large companies, local governments and support organizations and operates programs to create business collaboration.
- **Setouchi-i-Base:** resident coordinators explicitly provide business creation/matching and build organic networks inside and outside the region.
- **STATION Ai/Aichi:** the prefecture explicitly describes mutual cooperation between STATION Ai and partner hubs and supports formation/implementation of co-creation projects.
- **TIB:** matches ventures to support providers according to business domain and stated needs and uses TIB's network to create matching opportunities.
- **Interface Scotland:** particularly clean evidence for the matching interpretation: staff identify organizational needs and search all Scottish universities/research institutions to find the best match.
- **Forum Virium, Catapult, Vinnova and Copenhagen Solutions Lab:** demonstrate that experimentation/testing is central for some intermediary subtypes, but not universal across the sample.

## Cross-case conclusion

No single operational activity appears in every case, but **connecting/matching/brokering heterogeneous actors into collaboration** is the most recurrent common denominator. Experimentation, direct facilities, R&D support and knowledge transfer are important subtype-specific functions. This is why the institutional audit favors a productive-matching primitive rather than a pure network-effect, R&D-spillover, or experimentation primitive.
