# Rejected / Deferred Primitive Audit

## Pure network effect

**REJECT AS DIRECT PRIMITIVE.**

Reason: institutional evidence does not say the general hub's direct action is to increase utility mechanically with participant mass. When larger networks help, the more defensible mechanism is usually improved access to potential partners/resources. Treat that as a matching-pool externality with explicit matching interpretation.

Prior-art risk is also severe because indirect network effects are foundational in competing platform/matchmaker models.

## Pure R&D spillover

**REJECT AS DIRECT PRIMITIVE; DEFER AS POSSIBLE OUTCOME CHANNEL.**

Reason: many co-creation facilities support business creation, market access, human resources, public-problem solving and cross-sector projects, not only technological R&D. Automatic knowledge spillover is narrower than the institutional object and close to mature R&D-spillover theory.

## Absorptive capacity

**DEFER.**

Economically meaningful for knowledge-intensive collaboration but it describes a firm's ability to exploit external knowledge, not the direct general function of a hub. Use only if the minimal productive-matching model later requires heterogeneous ability to use matches and institutional evidence makes it essential.

## Joint synergy term

**REJECT AS DIRECT PRIMITIVE.**

The conceptual ontology requires complementarity of resources, but inserting an explicit `synergy` parameter/interaction term at the outset risks assuming the desired benefit. The minimal model should encode productive match value without adding an independent synergy mechanism unless necessary.

## Generic gateway access

**REJECT AS PRIMARY; EMBED IN MATCHING.**

External ecosystem access is strongly documented (NEXs, Setouchi-i-Base, STATION Ai, Catapult), but “gateway” is best interpreted as expanding the feasible partner/matching set. It is an important operational function, not the most general primitive.

## Search-friction reduction

**RETAIN AS MICROFOUNDATION, NOT PRIMARY.**

Search is direct and formalizable but too narrow: hubs also screen, broker and coordinate; quality/complementarity of the match matters. Standard search-intermediation theory is mature.

## Matching

**RETAIN — PRIMARY DIRECT PRIMITIVE.**

Institutionally recurrent, aligned with heterogeneous complementary resources, capable of incorporating search/screening/brokerage without separately modeling every friction, and compatible with explicit downstream competition.

Novelty is not claimed for matching itself.

## Coordination

**RETAIN AS MICROFOUNDATION / DEFER.**

Coordination and relationship formation are strongly documented, particularly after actors meet. But a general coordination-cost primitive is less observable and less directly linked to partner complementarity than productive matching. It may be needed only if post-match implementation failure becomes central.

## Experimentation / living-lab learning

**REJECT AS GENERAL PRIMITIVE; RETAIN FOR SUBTYPE EXTENSION.**

Forum Virium, Vinnova testbeds, Catapults and parts of TIB strongly support experimentation/testing. E:N BASE, NEXs, Setouchi and other broader co-creation hubs do not make experimentation their universal defining feature.

## Summary

| Candidate | Status |
|---|---|
| Pure network effect | REJECT direct; matching-pool interpretation only |
| R&D spillover | REJECT direct / DEFER outcome |
| Absorptive capacity | DEFER |
| Joint synergy | REJECT direct; complementarity stays conceptual |
| Generic gateway access | EMBED IN MATCHING |
| Search | MICROFOUNDATION |
| **Matching** | **RETAIN PRIMARY** |
| Coordination | MICROFOUNDATION/DEFER |
| Experimentation | SUBTYPE ONLY |
| Shared partner-pool/referral | RETAIN single SECONDARY candidate |
