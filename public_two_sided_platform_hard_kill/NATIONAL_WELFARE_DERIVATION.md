# National Welfare Derivation

`SW=W_1+W_2+Pi_T` under zero private real operating cost.

Resident project utilities subtract private fees, while `Pi_T=p_T n_T^F` adds them back. Hence fee transfers cancel exactly at the national level.

National welfare therefore consists of project real benefits net of real access costs, partner real surplus net of participation costs, and minus `gamma(x_1^2+x_2^2)/2`.
