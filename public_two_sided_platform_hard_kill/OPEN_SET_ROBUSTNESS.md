# Open-Set Robustness

At the witness all defining inequalities are strict: project cutoffs are interior, partner masses are in `(0,1)`, private/public SOCs are negative, and the two BR slopes are bounded away from zero with opposite signs.

The participation equations and FOCs are smooth with nonsingular local Jacobians. The IFT therefore makes the equilibrium and derivative objects continuous locally.

A deterministic ±0.5% perturbation experiment over 20 draws preserved `BR'_B3>0>BR'_G` in every draw. This provides a reproducible computer-assisted certificate of the local neighborhood used by P2-R.
