# Private Demand Derivation

In the central regime,
`D_T=t_1+t_2-2s`, with `s=(kappa_T+p_T)/q_T`.

The cutoffs and `q_T` are determined by the participation fixed point, hence `D_T=D_T(p_T;x_1,x_2)`.

At `beta=0`, the expression collapses exactly. Define `Delta_i=q_i-q_T`, `A=1/Delta_1+1/Delta_2`, `B=2/q_T`. Then
`D_T=[(kappa_L-kappa_T)A-kappa_T B] - p_T(A+B)`.

This beta-zero formula is used for the two-sidedness essentiality audit.
