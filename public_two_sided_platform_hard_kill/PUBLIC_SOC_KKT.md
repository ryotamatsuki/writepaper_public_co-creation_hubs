# Public SOC / KKT

The headline witness is interior: `0<x_B3,x_G<1`. Own second derivatives are strictly negative in both B3 and G, so the local FOCs satisfy the SOC.

Boundary regimes `x_i=0,1`, partner clipping, platform inactivity, and project full participation remain KKT/corner cases. They are not used to establish P2-R.

The national planner in the witness region moves toward a higher-investment regime boundary; this is one reason P1-R is not retained as a headline theorem.
