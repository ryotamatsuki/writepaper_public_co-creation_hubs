# P2-R Hard Kill

## Verdict

**SURVIVES STAGE 4 — CONDITIONAL SIGN-REVERSAL RESULT.**

There exists a smooth interior parameter region in which fixed-price B3 has `BR_i'>0` while G has `BR_i'<0`.

Economic decomposition:
1. fixed-price private-network fragmentation creates a positive cross-region network channel in B3;
2. endogenous private pricing responds to public investment by cutting `p_T`;
3. the price cut makes the private platform more attractive in the rival region;
4. this negative private-price channel can dominate the positive B3 network channel.

Sufficient sign-reversal condition in derivative form is `M_B3>0` and `M_B3+P_price<0`, where `M_B3` is the fixed-price cross effect and `P_price` is the total endogenous-price contribution to the reduced cross derivative.

The deterministic witness and strict SOCs provide a regular point; continuity plus 20/20 local perturbation successes establish a numerically certified open neighborhood. Stage 6 must determine whether this proposition-level result is actually novel.
