# Market Creation / Displacement Accounting

The central regime has positive outside mass, so public investment can move the lowest participating cutoff and create participation (`0 -> H_i`) as regimes/cutoffs change.

Within the headline local regime, marginal reallocation also includes `H_T -> H_i`. Off symmetry, rival-public entry allows `H_j -> H_i`.

These margins answer the motivating policy question but remain accounting objects: generic creation-versus-fragmentation is already known and is not the contribution.
