# Limiting Cases

- `beta -> 0`: B3 cross effect tends to zero; private price response remains.
- `alpha -> 0`: platform partner thickness ceases to matter for project choice.
- `tau -> 0+`: headline result has a well-defined limit; exact zero creates a public-platform tie.
- `gamma -> infinity`: public investment collapses toward zero/corners.
- `p_T -> 0`: private pricing channel vanishes at the boundary.
- weak `rhoT`: private partner baseline is thin; fee response can be strong.
- strong `rho,rhoT` can trigger partner clipping, outside the smooth theorem region.
