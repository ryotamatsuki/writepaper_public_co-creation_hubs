# tau=0 Robustness

In the symmetric central regime, the local equations do not contain `tau` because the rival public platform is inactive for any `tau>0` when public qualities are equal.

Therefore the P2-R formulas and sign reversal persist as `tau -> 0+`.

At exactly `tau=0`, H1 and H2 are identical for a resident project at symmetric investment, so assignment between the two public platforms is set-valued. This is a tie singularity, not the source of the sign reversal. No geography term appears in the headline cross-channel decomposition.
