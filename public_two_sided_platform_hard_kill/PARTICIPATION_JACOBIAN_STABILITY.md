# Participation Jacobian and Stability

For `y=(t_1,t_2,q_T)`, the smooth-regime Jacobian is

`J11=q_1-q_T-delta t_1`, `J13=-t_1`,
`J22=q_2-q_T-delta t_2`, `J23=-t_2`,
`J31=J32=-delta`,
`J33=1-2 delta a/q_T^2`.

All omitted entries are zero.

The witness has a nonsingular Jacobian and strict interior inequalities, so the IFT gives a locally unique smooth participation branch. The sign-reversal certificate is restricted to this branch; no high-participation equilibrium is selected by fiat.
