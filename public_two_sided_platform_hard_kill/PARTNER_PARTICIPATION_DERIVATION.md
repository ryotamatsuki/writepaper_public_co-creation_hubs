# Partner Participation Derivation

A partner with per-platform real participation cost `c~U[0,1]` joins public platform i iff `c <= rho+x_i+beta n_i^F` and joins the private platform iff `c <= rhoT+beta n_T^F`.

Hence, before clipping,
`n_i^P=rho+x_i+beta n_i^F`,
`n_T^P=rhoT+beta n_T^F`.

Because participation is a separate activity on each platform, multihoming does not require a partner-side discrete-choice allocation equation.
