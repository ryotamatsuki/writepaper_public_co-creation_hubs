# Social Planner

National welfare is `SW=W_1+W_2+Pi_T` with fee transfers cancelled.

At the P2-R witness, `dSW/dx_i` evaluated at the decentralized symmetric G equilibrium is approximately `+0.423`. Hence the national planner prefers higher public facilitation at that point.

The planner optimum exits the central interior regime toward a higher-investment boundary in the witness calibration; no interior planner threshold is used as a headline result.
