# Regime Map

Headline smooth regime by home region: `0 -> H_T -> local H_i`.

Strict conditions: `0<s<t_i<1`, `q_i>q_T`, `kappa_T+p_T<kappa_L`, interior partner masses, nonsingular participation Jacobian, private SOC<0, public SOC<0.

Adjacent regimes include: private inactive, local public inactive, full participation, partner clipping, rival-public entry, and public-platform tie as tau approaches zero.

P2-R is an existence/open-set theorem in the headline regime, not a global-all-parameters theorem.
