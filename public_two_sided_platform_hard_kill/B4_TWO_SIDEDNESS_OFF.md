# B4 — Two-Sidedness Off

Set `beta=0` and change nothing else.

Then `n_i^P=rho+x_i`, `n_T^P=rhoT`; private network quality no longer responds to project mass.

At fixed private price, each region's local project problem is independent of the other region in the central regime, so `W_{i,x_i x_j}^{B3,beta=0}=0` exactly.

Endogenous private pricing still depends on both public investments: `p^*_{x_i}<0` by the closed-form beta-zero price solution. Thus private pricing can re-couple the governments even without two-sided feedback.

The *strong* P2-R result selected for the paper—complements in B3 versus substitutes in G—does not survive: the complementarity side collapses to independence at beta=0. Two-sidedness is therefore essential for the sign reversal, though not for mere re-coupling.
