# Asymmetric Equilibria

Network effects permit regime changes and asymmetric participation branches. Stage 4 checked that the symmetric headline witness lies on a locally unique smooth fixed-point branch and does not require a tipping selection.

Potential asymmetric cases include rival-public entry when a quality advantage exceeds `tau`, one public platform becoming inactive, and partner clipping. These are standard network/regime effects and are not used as contribution claims.

A global catalogue of every asymmetric corner is unnecessary for the existence form of P2-R, but Stage 6/manuscript work must state that the theorem is local to the specified interior regime.
