# Two-Sided Essentiality

The headline is **complements in B3 -> substitutes in G**.

Set `beta=0`. Then at fixed price B3 is locally decoupled: `W_{i,x_i x_j}=0` exactly. Endogenous private pricing still yields `p^*_{x_i}<0` and can re-couple the governments.

Therefore two-sidedness is not essential for the existence of a private-price transmission channel, but it **is essential for the stronger sign reversal** because the positive B3 complementarity is generated by project-to-partner feedback through the shared private network.

Stage 4 essentiality verdict: **PASS for the sign-reversal theorem; fail for any weaker 're-coupling' claim.**
