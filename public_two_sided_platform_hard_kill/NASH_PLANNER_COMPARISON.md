# Nash vs Planner Comparison

At the P2-R witness, decentralized regional Nash investment is below the direction preferred by national welfare: the regional FOC is zero while the planner's marginal derivative is positive.

This demonstrates a real decentralization wedge but does not establish P1-R novelty. The wedge is retained as welfare interpretation only because the planner optimum hits a regime boundary and generic network under/overinvestment results are already known.
