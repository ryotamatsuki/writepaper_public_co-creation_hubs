# Private Price Solution

Private profit is `Pi_T=p_T D_T`.

Interior FOC: `D_T+p_T D_{T,p}=0`; SOC: `2D_{T,p}+p_T D_{T,pp}<0`.

For `beta>0`, the admissible solution is characterized jointly with the three participation equations and verified by the implicit solver.

For `beta=0`, demand is linear. With `A=1/Delta_1+1/Delta_2`, `B=2/q_T`,

`p_T^*=[(kappa_L-kappa_T)A-kappa_T B]/[2(A+B)]`.

This exact benchmark confirms that endogenous private pricing is not itself a two-sided phenomenon.
