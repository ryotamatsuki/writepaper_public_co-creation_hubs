# G Public Cross Effect

In G, differentiate `W~_2=W_2(x_1,x_2,p^*(x_1,x_2))`.

The reduced cross derivative contains the B3/direct network terms plus private-price terms generated by `p^*_{x_1}`, `p^*_{x_2}`, and second derivatives of the price policy. In particular `W_{2,x_2p}p^*_{x_1}` is absent from B3.

In the verified region `p^*_{x_1}<0`: a stronger H1 induces the private platform to cut its fee. That price cut makes `H_T` more attractive to region-2 projects and lowers the marginal return to x2.

At the witness the full reduced cross derivative is `-0.00480885<0`.
