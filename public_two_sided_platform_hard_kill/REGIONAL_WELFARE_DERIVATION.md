# Regional Welfare Derivation

Region i counts its resident project surplus plus its resident share of partner surplus across platforms minus own public investment cost.

In the central regime resident project surplus is
`PS_i^F = int_s^{t_i}(z q_T-a)dz + int_{t_i}^1(z q_i-kappa_L)dz`.

With symmetric residence shares, each region owns one-half of the national partner population. For an interior platform with partner threshold `b_h=n_h^P`, national partner surplus is `b_h^2/2`, so region i receives `b_h^2/4`.

Therefore
`W_i=PS_i^F + ( (n_1^P)^2+(n_2^P)^2+(n_T^P)^2 )/4 - gamma x_i^2/2`.

Private fees are already subtracted in resident project utility and are regional outflows.
