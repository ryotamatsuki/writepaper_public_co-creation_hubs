# B2 — Two Public, No Private

Remove `H_T`.

Near a symmetric point with `tau>0`, each resident uses only its local public platform or the outside option; the rival public platform is strictly more costly at equal quality.

Each local platform then solves its own platform-specific feedback `n_i^F -> n_i^P -> n_i^F`. The two regional investment problems are locally decoupled, so the public-public cross effect is zero in this symmetric local-use regime.

This benchmark shows that the sign reversal is not generated by a pure two-government network game.
