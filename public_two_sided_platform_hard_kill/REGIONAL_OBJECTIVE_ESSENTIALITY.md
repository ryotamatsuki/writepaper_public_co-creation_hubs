# Regional Objective Essentiality

P2-R is a noncooperative public-public best-response theorem. Under a coordinated public objective, separate `BR_1,BR_2` cease to be equilibrium objects; the planner internalizes cross-region project and partner effects and private-profit transfers.

At the witness, the national-welfare marginal derivative at the decentralized G equilibrium is positive, while each regional government satisfies its own FOC. Hence coordinated and decentralized incentives are materially different, not merely relabeled welfare levels.

Verdict: **PASS for the decentralized strategic-relation result.**
