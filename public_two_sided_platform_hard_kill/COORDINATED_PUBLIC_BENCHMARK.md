# Coordinated Public Benchmark

Define the coordinated objective as national welfare `SW=W_1+W_2+Pi_T`, with the same private best-response function `p_T^*(x_1,x_2)`.

At the regional G witness, the coordinated marginal derivative is positive (`approximately 0.423`) rather than zero. The coordinated authority therefore does not choose the decentralized symmetric investment locally.

The coordinated problem has no public-public BR slope; it internalizes the external effects that create the decentralized strategic game.
