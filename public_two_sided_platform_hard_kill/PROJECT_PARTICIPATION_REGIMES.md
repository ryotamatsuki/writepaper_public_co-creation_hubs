# Project Participation Regimes

The upper envelope of four affine utilities is piecewise. The analytically useful central regime is `0 -> private -> local public` in each region.

Aggregate masses in that regime are
`n_i^F=1-t_i`,
`n_T^F=t_1+t_2-2s`,
`D_0^F=2s`.

At symmetric public investments and `tau>0`, each resident's rival public platform is dominated by its local public platform because the two public qualities coincide but rival access costs more. This does not remove the rival option from the model; off symmetry, rival demand is positive when a quality advantage offsets `tau`.

Other upper-envelope orderings are recorded as corner/regime cases and are not used to manufacture P2-R.
