# B3 — Two Public + Fixed Private Price

Retain `H_1,H_2,H_T` and two-sided participation, but fix `p_T=bar p`.

With `beta>0`, stronger `x_1` draws region-1 projects out of the private platform, lowers private project mass, lowers private partner mass and hence private project-side network value. That shared private-network contraction changes region-2 sorting and raises the marginal return to `x_2` in the verified region.

At the witness, fixing `bar p` at the G equilibrium price gives symmetric B3 equilibrium `x_B3=0.656020`, own second derivative `-0.118358`, cross derivative `+0.0135412`, and `BR'_B3=+0.114409`.

Hence the regional public investments are strategic complements in B3.
