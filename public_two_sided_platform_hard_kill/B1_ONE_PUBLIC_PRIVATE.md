# B1 — One Public + Private

Remove `H_2` without changing utility or welfare definitions.

The one-public/private game retains endogenous two-sided participation and a nonzero private fee response to `x_1`. Thus own-public price discipline is already available in B1 and cannot be the contribution.

B1 has no public-public reaction slope by construction, so it cannot reproduce P2-R's strategic-relation reversal.
