# Partner Surplus Accounting

For platform h, a partner of cost c obtains `b_h-c` when participating, with `c~U[0,1]`.

For `0<b_h<1`, national partner surplus is `int_0^{b_h}(b_h-c)dc=b_h^2/2`.

Symmetric residence partitions this equally, so each regional government counts `b_h^2/4` for every platform. This preserves the Stage 3 W-A accounting and does not attach partner surplus mechanically to platform location.
