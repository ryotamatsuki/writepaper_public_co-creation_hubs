# Ehime / E:N BASE Deep Audit

## Bottom line

**E:N BASE does not currently provide primary-source evidence of residual-core depletion.** Its design contains functional overlap with metropolitan innovation-support institutions, but the prefecture's explicit metropolitan policy is to strengthen, not weaken, E:N BASE members' connections with metropolitan firms.

## Functions supplied locally

Official Ehime materials show that E:N BASE provides:

- a membership-based physical co-creation space;
- community management and participant networking;
- seminars, workshops and meetups;
- consultation and accompaniment support;
- open-innovation understanding and opportunity creation;
- municipality-to-company/startup matching and project formation.

These functions overlap materially with community, matching, event, consultation and acceleration functions available in metropolitan/national startup ecosystems.

## Evidence against a headline diversion interpretation

Ehime Prefecture's 2026 procurement for metropolitan co-creation events states that the purpose is to use issues identified through E:N BASE activities to hold metropolitan workshops, strengthen networks between metropolitan firms and E:N BASE members, and ultimately create new examples of mutual collaboration between the metropolitan area and Ehime.

This is direct institutional evidence of a gateway/complementarity design. It is not evidence that regional participation reduces direct metropolitan participation.

The recurring E:N BASE Innovation Meetups likewise seek to build relationships and networks among participants; they do not document displacement of Tokyo-based networks.

## Six blocker questions

1. Does E:N BASE provide services also obtainable through Tokyo/national ecosystems? **YES, partly.** Networking, matching, events, open-innovation support and consultation overlap.
2. Is it explicitly intended to reduce the need for direct metropolitan search? **NO EVIDENCE FOUND.**
3. Does it instead explicitly expand metropolitan access? **YES.** The metropolitan co-creation procurement explicitly seeks stronger networks and mutual collaboration.
4. Are firms realistically choosing between local and metropolitan networking activities? **POSSIBLE, but not evidenced for E:N BASE.** Generic startup-support programs impose meaningful time demands, but E:N BASE-specific displacement data were not found.
5. Is there indication of limited participation bandwidth? **GENERICALLY YES; E:N BASE-SPECIFICALLY NO DIRECT MEASURE.**
6. Does regional provision replace, duplicate, or complement metropolitan functions? **MIXED FUNCTIONAL OVERLAP, BUT OFFICIAL DESIGN IS PRIMARILY COMPLEMENT/GATEWAY.**

## Verdict

E:N BASE application fit for I3: **WEAK**.

The appropriate counterfactual is not established. It cannot currently be claimed that, absent E:N BASE, marginal users would participate more directly in NEXs Tokyo or an equivalent metropolitan core.
