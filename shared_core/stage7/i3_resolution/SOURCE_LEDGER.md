## International / peer-reviewed sources

19. Aarstad, Kvitastein & Jakobsen (2016), Geoforum, "Local buzz, global pipelines, or simply too much buzz?"
    - DOI: 10.1016/j.geoforum.2016.07.009
    - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718516301919
    - Use: regional and international collaboration can substitute for medium-sized firms and be subtractive for small firms; supports non-universal complementarity.

20. "No islands of entrepreneurship—mapping the trans-local dimension of entrepreneurial ecosystems through networks of accelerator participation" (Small Business Economics, 2025)
    - https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11187-025-01026-1
    - Use: trans-local exchange through accelerator participation is common; strong contrary evidence against assuming local ecosystems isolate users from external networks.

21. Techstars — Inside a Techstars Accelerator (2026)
    - https://www.techstars.com/blog/founder-advice/inside-a-techstars-accelerator-what-to-expect-from-the-three-months
    - Use: intensive three-month structured participation; generic capacity evidence.

22. Y Combinator FAQ
    - https://www.ycombinator.com/faq/
    - Use: founders expected to work full-time on company during batch; additional generic attention/time discipline.

## Evidentiary conclusion

The source set supports scarce participation capacity and the possibility of local/external substitution in some environments, but does not directly demonstrate E:N BASE-driven thinning of a common Tokyo/national network. Primary Japanese policy evidence is predominantly complementary/gateway-oriented.
