# Stage 3M — Mechanism Specification

## Players and timing

Two regional governments `i in {1,2}` choose binary public-hub provision `E_i in {0,1}` and pay fixed cost `F>0` if open. Each region has one beneficiary firm. After hub choices, productive matching occurs; a successful match improves the beneficiary firm's productive capability; firms then compete in one homogeneous-product Cournot market; regional welfare is realized.

## Productive matching environment

Each firm requires a complementary capability that it does not possess internally. A mere contact has no value unless the partner is compatible with that missing capability.

For each firm there are three logically distinct opportunity classes:

- **overlap opportunity**: discoverable through either open hub; productive with probability `p`;
- **home-exclusive opportunity**: discoverable only through the firm's own regional hub; productive with probability `p`;
- **foreign-specialist referral opportunity**: located in the other hub's distinct pool and usable only if both hubs are open, because one hub identifies the partner and the other receives/routes the referral; productive with probability `r`.

Candidate-productivity events are independent. A firm uses at most one successful complementary match because one match is sufficient to generate the normalized capability improvement.

## Match probabilities

Let

`h = 1-(1-p)^2 = 2p-p^2`

and

`k = (1-p)^2 r`.

Then the focal firm's success probability is:

- `00`: `0`;
- own hub only: `h`;
- other hub only: `p`;
- both hubs: `t = h+k = 1-(1-p)^2(1-r)`.

The same structure applies symmetrically to the other region.

## M0 and M1 nesting

- **M0**: set `r=0`, hence `k=0`; productive matching plus overlap only.
- **M1**: allow `r>0`; the only additional channel is cross-hub referral/shared-pool access.

Nothing else changes between M0 and M1.

## Capability mapping

A productive match lowers the beneficiary firm's marginal cost by `Delta>0`:

`c_i = cbar - Delta m_i`, where `m_i in {0,1}` is the realized productive-match indicator.

This is a normalization of general productive-capability improvement, not an R&D primitive. No firm chooses R&D.

## Downstream market

Inverse demand is `P = a-q_1-q_2`. Define `A=a-cbar`. The admissible interior domain imposes `A>Delta>0`, ensuring positive Cournot quantities in every matching state.

For realized `(m_i,m_j)`,

`q_i = [A+2 Delta m_i-Delta m_j]/3`.

Firm operating profit is `pi_i=q_i^2`.

## Government objective

Canonical baseline:

`W_i = E[pi_i] + (1/2) E[CS] - F E_i`,

where `CS=(q_1+q_2)^2/2`. The one-half consumer-surplus share is justified by a symmetric national market with half of consumers in each region. Producer-only welfare is retained only as a diagnostic.

## Stage 3M verdict

**PASS.** Overlap and referral are generated by access to finite partner opportunities, rather than arbitrary payoff cross-terms. Complementarity has formal content because only capability-compatible partners generate productive matches.
