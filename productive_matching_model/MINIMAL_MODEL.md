# Minimal Productive-Matching Model

## Parameters

- `A=a-cbar>0`: baseline market size / demand-cost margin;
- `Delta>0`: productive-capability gain from one successful complementary match;
- `p in (0,1)`: compatibility probability for the overlapping and home-exclusive opportunities;
- `r in [0,1]`: probability that the bilateral referral pool yields a productive foreign-specialist match;
- `F>0`: fixed public hub cost.

Interior Cournot condition:

`A>Delta>0`.

## Matching probabilities

Define

`h = 2p-p^2`,

`k = (1-p)^2 r`,

`t = h+k = 1-(1-p)^2(1-r)`.

The profile-specific productive-match probabilities `(mu_1,mu_2)` are:

- `00`: `(0,0)`;
- `10`: `(h,p)`;
- `01`: `(p,h)`;
- `11`: `(t,t)`.

Conditional matching outcomes across beneficiary firms are independent. This is a minimal independence normalization; the cross-hub linkage operates through accessible opportunity sets, not correlated match shocks.

## Downstream Cournot equilibrium

For realized match indicators `(m_i,m_j)`:

`c_i = cbar-Delta m_i`.

With inverse demand `P=a-q_1-q_2`, the interior equilibrium is

`q_i(m_i,m_j) = [A+2 Delta m_i-Delta m_j]/3`.

The Cournot first-order condition implies

`P-c_i=q_i`,

so operating profit is

`pi_i(m_i,m_j)=q_i(m_i,m_j)^2`.

Total output is

`Q=[2A+Delta(m_i+m_j)]/3`,

and consumer surplus is `CS=Q^2/2`.

## Expected regional gross welfare

Let `u=Pr(m_i=1)` and `v=Pr(m_j=1)`. Under independent Bernoulli match realizations,

`R_i(u,v) = E[pi_i] + (1/2)E[CS]`

has the exact form

`R_i(u,v) = [8A^2 + 20A Delta u - 4A Delta v - 14 Delta^2 u v + 17 Delta^2 u + 5 Delta^2 v]/36`.

Net regional welfare is

`W_i(E_i,E_j)=R_i(mu_i,mu_j)-F E_i`.

Because `F` cancels from the cross-entry comparison, define

`D_0 = R_i(h,p)-R_i(0,0)`,

`D_1 = R_i(t,t)-R_i(p,h)`,

and

`Gamma^M = D_1-D_0`.

`Gamma^M<0` means strategic substitutes; `Gamma^M>0` means strategic complements.

## Exact gross-entry benefit without rival hub

`D_0 = Delta p[-20A p+36A+14 Delta p^2-45 Delta p+39 Delta]/36`.

## Compact cross-effect representation

The exact formulas become transparent by keeping `h` and the referral-induced incremental match probability `k`:

`Gamma^M = Gamma_0 + [Delta k(8A+Delta(11-14h-7k))]/18`,

where

`Gamma_0 = -[Delta p(8A+Delta(11-14p^2+7p^3))]/18`.

This decomposition is not imposed as an economic three-force decomposition. It is a nested-model identity: `Gamma_0` is M0 and the second term is the exact change generated by switching on the single referral channel.
