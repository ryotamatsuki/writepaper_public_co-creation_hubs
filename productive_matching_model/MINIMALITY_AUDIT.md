# Minimality Audit

## Does every primitive do distinct economic work?

### `p` — baseline compatibility / overlap intensity

Needed to distinguish a contact from a productive complementary match and to generate duplicated opportunity access. Removing `p` removes the baseline matching technology.

### `r` — bilateral referral success

Needed only for the one authorized positive cross-hub channel. Setting `r=0` nests M0 exactly and kills complementarity globally.

### `Delta` — value of productive capability in the downstream market

Maps a productive collaboration into a market-relevant capability change. It is not R&D and is not chosen by firms. Setting `Delta=0` removes all economic value of matching.

### `A` — baseline demand-cost margin

Needed for a nondegenerate homogeneous Cournot market and interior quantities. It does not determine matching probabilities.

### `F` — public hub fixed cost

Needed for endogenous provision levels, but intentionally cancels from the strategic cross-effect `Gamma`.

## Rejected redundancies

The model does not include separate parameters for:

- search cost;
- information precision;
- coordination cost;
- generic network size;
- R&D spillover;
- absorptive capacity;
- product differentiation;
- hub quality;
- separate referral utility;
- arbitrary cannibalization payoff.

## Why the referral term is not a hidden generic network effect

The positive effect is generated only by an additional foreign-specialist opportunity that becomes usable when both infrastructures required for referral are present. Its effect on match probability is

`k=(1-p)^2 r`,

which is bounded by the probability that the baseline own/shared opportunities have both failed. This saturation restriction is generated by the finite opportunity set and prevents referral from acting as an unconstrained additive `+beta E_iE_j` payoff.

## Why the overlap term is not arbitrary

The neighbor hub gives the focal firm access to the same overlap opportunity that the focal hub would otherwise discover. Hence the marginal value of own entry is mechanically reduced when the neighbor already exists. No `-delta E_iE_j` term is used.

## Product-market minimality

Homogeneous Cournot is selected because it is the smallest smooth strategic market game in which a capability improvement changes both firms' endogenous quantities and profits. Homogeneous Bertrand with identical goods would create discontinuous/knife-edge cost ranking outcomes and would not be a cleaner baseline. Product differentiation is therefore unnecessary.

## Verdict

**VALID MINIMAL MODEL.** No parameter or stage is retained solely to obtain a desired sign reversal.
