## Frozen architecture

1. two regions, immobile projects;
2. local partner sets `S_1,S_2` with a primitive common/unique decomposition;
3. `x_i` = local screening/evaluation capacity;
4. one private metropolitan intermediary with fixed matching technology and endogenous price `p_T`;
5. project choice among `{H_1,H_2,H_T,0}` by expected productive surplus;
6. Timing A: public investments -> private price -> project choice/matching;
7. no referral, no downstream Cournot, no firm relocation, no taste shocks.

## Exactly one allowed unresolved issue

**Formalize and hard-kill the overlap-additionality mechanism.**

Stage 1 must choose the smallest tractable set/distribution structure that lets overlap be derived from common partner opportunities and derive the marginal decomposition of `x_i` into creation versus duplication/displacement.

At least one proposition must be nontrivial. Candidate target:

> under explicit primitive conditions, greater overlap in local partner opportunity sets lowers the creation share of marginal local matching investment and changes the strategic response `BR_i(x_j)` through duplication, while the metropolitan price response changes the uncovered-project threshold.

The sign need not match this candidate. A negative proof is acceptable.

## Kill conditions

Stop immediately if:

- overlap can be replaced by a generic facility cross-elasticity without loss;
- the result is equivalent to scalar quality competition;
- the only theorem is 'local hub is used when its expected value is high enough';
- the only surviving margin is Liu-style new participation versus switching without an additional overlap-driven investment wedge;
- a desired sign requires referral, arbitrary complementarity, downstream Cournot, or endogenous metro quality.

## Verification

Use symbolic derivation where possible, then numerical counterexample search. Do not expand the model until this one gate is resolved.
