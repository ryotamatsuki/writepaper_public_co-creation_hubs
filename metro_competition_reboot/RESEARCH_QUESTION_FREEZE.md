# Research Question Freeze

## Exact frozen RQ

> **When does a locally financed public co-creation hub generate positive additional value for local firms and regional welfare when firms can also access competing public hubs in other regions and a metropolitan private matching intermediary?**

Japanese interpretation:

> 地方企業が他地域の公共拠点や大都市圏の民間マッチング拠点を利用できるにもかかわらず、地方政府が自地域の官民共創拠点の matching capability に投資することが、どの企業・どの地域・どの競争環境で追加的な productive match と地域厚生を生むのか。

## What the paper must explain

The paper is not a theory of hub prestige, manager behavior, referral failure, or municipal competition per se. It must explain:

`who benefits × where × under what market environment`.

The theory must separately identify:

- projects that become matched only because the local hub exists or improves;
- projects that would already match through a rival local hub or metropolitan private intermediary but obtain a better match locally;
- pure reallocation/business stealing;
- regional incidence changes without productive additionality.

## Frozen exclusions

No Hotelling taste distance, no exogenous 50–50 allocation, no firm relocation, no Cournot baseline, no automatic referral, and no generic scalar facility quality. PR #21 remains a diagnostic negative benchmark only.
