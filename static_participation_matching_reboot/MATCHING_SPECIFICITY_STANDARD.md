# Matching Specificity Standard

A candidate needs an equilibrium-relevant state `Xi` satisfying

`strategy/participation -> Xi -> matching -> allocation -> payoff`.

Provider-specific expected quality alone is insufficient.

A candidate fails if, after solving its matching subproblem, all strategic behavior can be written as

`h*(omega)=argmax_h {V_h(omega,x_h)-P_h}`

with `V_h` independent of endogenous masses/composition on the other side.

Even an endogenous `Xi` is insufficient if label stripping turns it into an ordinary network-size, queue-length, or generic facility-congestion state with no additional matching restriction or result.
