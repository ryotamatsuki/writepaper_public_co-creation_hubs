# Four-Way Participation Requirement

For project type `(k,z)`, a generic provider payoff can be written for diagnostic purposes as

`U_h(k,z)=z mu_h(k;Xi_h)-c_h`, `U_0=0`.

Conditional on an equilibrium matching state `Xi`, the non-use threshold is

`z_0(k;Xi)=min_h c_h/mu_h(k;Xi)`

for positive matching probabilities. Thus low-`z` projects choose zero while higher-`z` projects choose the provider with highest net return.

Four-way coexistence is robust whenever there are open sets of `k` on which H1, H2, and HT are respectively optimal for some positive interval of `z`, while `z<z_0(k)` has positive measure.

A diagnostic deterministic example gives shares approximately

- `D_0=0.293`
- `D_1=0.335`
- `D_2=0.335`
- `D_T=0.038`.

Nine small perturbations of metro productivity and public access cost retained strictly positive shares for all four options. Hence positive-mass non-use is not the fundamental obstacle.
