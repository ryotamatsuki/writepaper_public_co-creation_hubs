# Candidate B — Endogenous Market Thickness / Matching Function

## Skeleton

Partner mass `P_h` is fixed within period. Project demand `D_h` is endogenous and

`Y_h=M(x_h D_h,P_h)`, `mu_h=Y_h/D_h`.

Project payoff is `z mu_h-c_h`.

## Participation

PASS. Continuous productive value `z` supports positive non-use together with three active providers.

## Matching state

`Xi_h=D_h/P_h` (market tightness) is endogenous and feeds back into match probabilities.

## Hard kill

This is genuinely a search/matching-function object in origin, but under strong label stripping it becomes a service facility with demand-dependent success/congestion technology. The exact state `D/P`, matching-function elasticities, thick-market and congestion effects are standard objects in Hosios/Moen/Pissarides-style search theory and directed search. Burdett–Shi–Wright explicitly derive endogenous matching frictions from buyers choosing capacity-constrained sellers.

No architecture-level restriction here prevents a generic congestion-service interpretation. Public ownership has not yet generated an independent matching theorem.

**Verdict: KILL — K4 strong congestion/platform reduction; K11 prior mechanism saturation.**
