# Stage 4 Failure Diagnosis

Stage 4 exposed two separable problems.

## F1 — characteristic-space collapse

CSDS became exactly

`U_1=Q_1-t_1 theta-kappa`,
`U_2=Q_2-t_2(1-theta)-kappa`,
`U_T=m-p_T-kappa`.

The matching story disappeared after payoff transformation.

## F2 — degenerate non-use

When the type-independent metro offer was active, every project not selecting a public Hub strictly preferred metro to `0`. Hence active metro competition and positive-mass non-use could not coexist.

## New diagnosis

F2 is easy to repair with a productive value margin `z`: low-value projects rationally choose `0`. F1 is harder. A new static architecture must contain an endogenous matching state that cannot be represented as a deterministic provider index or ordinary platform/congestion state.
