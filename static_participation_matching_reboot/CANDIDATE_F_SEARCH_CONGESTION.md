# Candidate F — Search Congestion / One-Shot Screening Rivalry

## Skeleton

Finite screening resource `x_h` is divided over endogenous demand:

`e_h=x_h/D_h`,
`mu_h(k)=phi_h(k,e_h)`.

Project payoff is `z mu_h-c_h`.

## Participation

PASS and highly tractable. Low `z` projects choose zero and congestion can support interior provider shares.

## Matching state

`Xi_h=D_h/x_h` or `e_h=x_h/D_h`.

## Hard kill

After label stripping this is a facility with finite service capacity per user: more users reduce service intensity/quality. Directed-search and queueing models already make posted terms trade off against endogenous queue length and probability of trade. Burdett–Shi–Wright derive exactly such pricing/matching frictions from buyers selecting capacity-constrained sellers.

No bilateral partner composition survives. `partner` can be replaced by `service resource` without changing any constraint.

**Verdict: KILL — K4/K11 generic congestion/directed-search absorption.**
