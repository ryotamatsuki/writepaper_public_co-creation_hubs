# Sign-Reversal Characterization

## Exact Level-3C equivalence

On `Theta^{RI,SOC}` the headline sign pattern is exactly

`BR_i^{B3′}>0>BR_i^{G′}`

iff

`M_B3(x^{B3},p^G)>0`

and

`M_G(x^G,p^G)<0`,

where

`M_G = M_fixed + P_price`

and

`P_price=W_{i,x_ip}p_j+W_{i,x_jp}p_i+W_{i,pp}p_ip_j+W_{i,p}p_{ij}`.

This is necessary and sufficient **conditional on the selected regular interior equilibrium branches and their SOCs**.

## Same-state mechanism threshold

At any one regular state at which the fixed-price cross derivative is positive,

`M_fixed>0` and `M_G<0`

iff

`-P_price>M_fixed>0`.

This is an exact analytic dominance condition. It cleanly identifies the repricing force that must dominate the fixed-price network force.

## Why this is not yet the requested primitive Level 3

The production headline compares two distinct roots:

- `G`: solve the public game jointly with `p^*(x_1,x_2)` to obtain `x^G,p^G`;
- `B3`: hold `p=p^G` fixed and re-solve the public game to obtain `x^{B3}`.

Therefore a primitive necessary-and-sufficient characterization must eliminate or transparently parameterize **both** equilibrium roots as well as the private policy derivatives. The exact block inequality above is Level-3C: mathematically complete conditional on equilibrium objects, economically interpretable, but not reduced far enough to primitives for the user-defined Level-3 GO standard.
