# Nonbinding Journal-Upgrade Implications

This memo does not change Stage-12 PR #38 or its selected target.

## If the Level-2 theorem is fully proved and integrated later

The proof architecture would become:

1. an exact analytic participation reduction;
2. exact private-price IFT identities;
3. an exact full-game cross-effect decomposition;
4. an analytic small-`beta` sufficient theorem establishing complements-to-substitutes reversal on a class of regular interior branches;
5. the existing canonical numerical witness at `beta=0.05` plus its local/open-set certificate.

This would reduce the current **proof-ceiling** risk because the economic phenomenon would no longer rest solely on one certified numerical neighborhood. It would also make the mechanism more transparent: two-sided feedback creates the B3 positive cross effect to first order in `beta`, while the endogenous-price game already has a strictly negative cross effect at `beta=0`.

## What it would not solve

- combination novelty remains the principal scholarly risk;
- the closest follower-price literature remains relevant;
- there is still no primitive necessary-and-sufficient global/interior-region threshold;
- no global welfare, planner, arbitrary-distribution, arbitrary-network, or equilibrium-uniqueness theorem follows;
- JRS-style spatial-content fit is not improved by stronger proofs;
- an IO-heavy referee pool may still view the component mechanisms as crowded.

## Nonbinding positioning consequence

The Stage-12 Primary, Journal of Public Economic Theory, would remain a natural target and would become better defended mathematically. A stronger theorem would make a theory-heavier stretch attempt more credible, but this L3-0 result alone does not justify retargeting to IJIO or another higher theory outlet. Any retargeting requires a fresh journal-positioning stage after the Level-2 theorem is fully formalized and referee-attacked.
