# Private Price Response Derivation

Let `D(x_1,x_2,p)` denote private project demand after reducing the participation block on a selected regular branch. Private profit is

`Pi=p D`.

Define the private first-order condition

`R(x_1,x_2,p)=D+pD_p=0`.

The private SOC and IFT denominator coincide:

`R_p=2D_p+pD_pp<0`.

## First response

For either public investment,

`p_i := p^*_{x_i} = -R_i/R_p = -(D_{x_i}+pD_{px_i})/(2D_p+pD_pp)`.

Thus `p_i<0` is equivalent, under the private SOC, to `R_i<0`. This is an exact reduced-form sign condition, but for `beta>0` the sign of `R_i` is itself generated by the participation branch and is not globally signed by the baseline primitive restrictions.

At `beta=0` the production proof already obtains the exact negative sign from the closed-form price solution. The L3 track does not promote that special-case sign to the full two-sided regime.

## Second response

The full public cross derivative also needs the mixed second derivative of the price policy. Differentiating `R(x,p^*(x))=0` twice gives

`p_{ij}= -[R_{ij}+R_{ip}p_j+R_{jp}p_i+R_{pp}p_ip_j]/R_p`.

This term is required for an exact full-game chain rule whenever the regional payoff has `W_p != 0`.

## Analytic status

- private FOC/SOC: exact;
- `p_i` and `p_{ij}` IFT formulas: exact;
- sign `p_i<0` at the canonical witness: independently reproducible numerically and exact at `beta=0`;
- global primitive sign for `beta>0`: **not established**.

The private-price block therefore supports an analytic Level-2 decomposition but does not by itself deliver a primitive Level-3 threshold.
