## Verdict

**Institutional-class generality is established.** The claim is not that one city outside Japan reproduces the entire three-player game literally. Rather, both constituent institutional primitives—local public innovation intermediation and priced metropolitan private innovation campuses with network value—are independently observed across multiple countries without changing the frozen mechanism.
