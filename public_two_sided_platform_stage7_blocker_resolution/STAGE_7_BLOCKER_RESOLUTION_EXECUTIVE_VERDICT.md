# Stage 7R-TP Institutional Blocker Resolution — Executive Verdict

Date: 2026-09-03

## Verdict

**BLOCKER RESOLVED — UPGRADE STAGE 7R-TP TO GO**

**STAGE 7.5 AUTHORIZED**

The Stage-7 blocker was whether the frozen `H_T,p_T` objects can be mapped to a real innovation-intermediation institution without changing the model, and whether that institutional class is broader than one Japanese case. The hard validation supports a narrower and more accurate interpretation:

`H_T = shared metropolitan private innovation hub`

`p_T = effective paid membership/access price for the metropolitan innovation-intermediation bundle`.

The strongest Japanese direct analogue is **CIC Tokyo**. Official CIC materials establish a privately held commercial innovation-community model, positive coworking membership pricing, explicit suitability as a Tokyo base for people normally working in local areas, access to an innovation community and global CIC network, and managerial ability to alter the effective access price through promotions. **SHIBUYA QWS** is a second strong analogue: it has positive project/member fees, explicit co-creation functions, a private seller, and an unusually clear contractual right to revise plans and charges. SENQ is a credible supporting case.

Internationally, **Level39 London** and multiple **CIC campuses** (Rotterdam, Warsaw, Cambridge/Boston) independently show the same priced metropolitan innovation-campus form. STATION F is a supporting but weaker profit-objective analogue. On the public side, the **City of Helsinki Campus Incubators Programme** and **Barcelona Activa Startup Lab** establish that local/municipal public investment in incubation, networking, mentoring and innovation-intermediation capacity is not Japan-specific.

The change from “national private platform” to “shared metropolitan private innovation hub” is **INTERPRETATION REFINEMENT ONLY**, not a model change. The mathematics requires a common outside option accessible from both regions, a private price setter, and network participation; it does not require national territorial coverage.

## Discipline retained

The evidence does **not** establish that a regional public-hub expansion has historically caused CIC, QWS, Level39 or another hub to cut its fee. `p^*_{T,x_i}<0` remains a testable model prediction, not an institutional fact.

Membership prices often bundle workspace and innovation-community access. Project single-homing is therefore defended only as a focal project's **primary intermediation route**, not firm-level legal exclusivity. Partner multi-homing is institutionally plausible/non-exclusive but not literally documented for every partner across every ecosystem. Private and public hubs can also cooperate ecosystem-wide while remaining substitutes for a focal project's primary route.

No frozen equation, strategic player, control, welfare identity, Stage-6 novelty status, or Stage-7 numerical result is changed.
