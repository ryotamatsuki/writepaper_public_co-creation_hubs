# QUINTBRIDGE Boundary Case

**Overall: C — strong innovation-network boundary case, not the canonical positive-price `H_T`.**

Official fee-change notice:
https://www.quintbridge.jp/news/detail/202303310000.html

Accessed 2026-09-03. QUINTBRIDGE announced that, from 2023-04-01, the annual fee for its then Co-creation Partner A category changed from JPY 220,000/year to **free**, while membership categories were redesigned.

This is useful for two reasons.

1. It demonstrates that innovation-hub operators can redesign membership pricing.
2. It demonstrates that a dense innovation network need not have a positive access price. Network-building objectives can support `p_T=0` as an observed boundary.

Therefore QUINTBRIDGE must not be used as the primary empirical counterpart to the frozen positive-price mechanism. Its current zero membership price contradicts C4 for the canonical application, even though C3/C6 are strong and historical C5 is established.

The boundary case disciplines the paper: the theory is about a subset of private metropolitan hubs with positive discretionary access prices, not all co-creation hubs.
