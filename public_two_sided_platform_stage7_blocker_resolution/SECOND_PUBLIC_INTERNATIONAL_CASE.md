# Second Public International Case — Barcelona Activa

Primary sources, accessed 2026-09-03:

- https://www.barcelonactiva.cat/en/-/incubadora-glories
- https://emprenedoria.barcelonactiva.cat/en/startup-lab
- https://www.barcelonactiva.cat/en/-/startup-lab-day-brings-together-the-entrepreneurial-community-to-foster-new-connections-and-share-the-impact-of-startups/1.0

Barcelona Activa's Startup Lab is a city innovation/incubation environment in the 22@ district for newly created technology/deep-tech startups. Official materials describe specialised support, mentoring, cooperation networks, ecosystem connections and a startup community. A July 2026 municipal-company article describes an annual Startup Lab Day intended to strengthen ties, create synergies and consolidate a collaborative network among projects in Barcelona's innovation ecosystem.

## Mapping

This validates the existence, in a second country and institutional system, of municipal investment in a local innovation-intermediation environment that combines physical space with advice, networks and project support. It is therefore a valid analogue for `G_i,H_i,x_i` at the institutional-class level.

## Counterexample value

Barcelona Activa is a municipal/public-service actor, not the intended profit-maximizing `H_T`. Its existence reinforces the need to distinguish the public side from commercial metropolitan private hubs rather than inferring objective functions from the word “hub” alone.
