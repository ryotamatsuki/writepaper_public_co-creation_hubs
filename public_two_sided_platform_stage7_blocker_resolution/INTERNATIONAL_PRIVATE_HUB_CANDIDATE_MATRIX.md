## Conclusion

The relevant institutional class is not Japan-specific. Independent operators/campuses in London, Paris, Rotterdam, Warsaw and the United States charge positive access prices for bundles that explicitly include entrepreneurial community/network/intermediation value.

No claim is made that all campuses share the frozen timing or that observed prices respond to public investment exactly as predicted.
