# Institutional Counterexamples

## 1. QUINTBRIDGE — strong network, current zero membership price

Official 2023 notice changed a JPY 220,000/year membership category to free. This rejects any claim that innovation-network value necessarily implies `p_T>0`.

## 2. Xport — paid and two-sided-like, but not profit-private

Xport has differentiated fees, matching, mentors and advisers, yet its chamber/university institutional structure makes the frozen private-profit objective unsuitable.

## 3. Municipal/public incubators

Helsinki and Barcelona provide strong innovation intermediation, but their public objectives make them analogues for `H_i,x_i`, not `H_T`.

## 4. FINOLAB / Inspired.Lab — intermediation without enough public pricing evidence

Both have plausible innovation-community functions, but this audit could not verify a current public fee and explicit price-setting margin strongly enough to promote them to direct analogues.

## Lesson

The theory applies to a **selected institutional class**: paid, commercially operated metropolitan innovation hubs with network/intermediation value. The word “innovation hub” alone is insufficient.
