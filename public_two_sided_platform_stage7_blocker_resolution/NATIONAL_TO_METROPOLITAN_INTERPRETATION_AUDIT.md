# National → Metropolitan Interpretation Audit

## Question

Does changing the institutional label from `national private platform` to `shared metropolitan private innovation hub` modify the frozen model?

## Verdict

**INTERPRETATION REFINEMENT ONLY — NOT A MODEL CHANGE.**

## Reason

The frozen game uses `H_T` as one common alternative to `H_1` and `H_2`. Its relevant mathematical properties are:

1. projects in both regions can select `H_T`;
2. `H_T` has partner/network participation;
3. the private operator chooses `p_T` to maximize `Pi_T`;
4. project allocation responds to the relative utility of `H_i` and `H_T`.

No equation requires the private operator to cover the entire nation, to operate branches nationwide, or to serve a national market in a geographic sense. The `T` label therefore need not encode territorial universality.

Official CIC Tokyo material is especially useful because its coworking product explicitly identifies users who normally work in local areas but want a Tokyo base. That directly supports a metropolitan shared-outside-option interpretation without adding a new travel-cost primitive.

## Prohibited inference

This audit does not add geography. No distance cost, metropolitan preference, migration decision, transportation cost, or location-specific type is introduced. If any future proposition required such objects, that would be a model extension and would require rollback to an earlier workflow stage.

## Preferred wording

Use:

> regional public innovation hubs and a shared metropolitan private innovation hub

Avoid presenting `national` as a formal primitive.
