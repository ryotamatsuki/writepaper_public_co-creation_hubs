# Evidence Limitations

1. No primary source was located documenting a causal historical episode in which a regional public innovation-hub expansion caused CIC, QWS, Level39 or another metropolitan private hub to cut its fee. This is a model prediction, not a validated fact.
2. Observed `p_T` analogues usually bundle workspace and innovation-community/intermediation access. A pure separately billed matching fee is not required or claimed.
3. Focal-project single-homing is a modeling abstraction. Firms and even projects can interact with multiple ecosystems in reality.
4. Partner multi-homing is institutionally plausible and non-exclusive but was not proven partner-by-partner across every hub pair.
5. Profit maximization is a reduced-form approximation for the selected commercial private class. It is not a literal documented corporate objective for every private-led facility.
6. QWS and other private facilities can have social/innovation ecosystem objectives in addition to commercial objectives.
7. Public and private hubs can collaborate ecosystem-wide. The substitution margin is limited to the focal project's primary intermediation route.
8. CIC live pages can display location/locale-specific price variants; all price claims should preserve page, currency and access date rather than be treated as immutable constants.
9. Overseas public and private examples do not establish an observed one-to-one three-player market identical to the frozen game. They establish that the constituent institutional primitives recur internationally.
10. QUINTBRIDGE shows that a strong innovation network can rationally/strategically operate with zero membership fees, so the paper must limit its application to positive-price private hubs.
