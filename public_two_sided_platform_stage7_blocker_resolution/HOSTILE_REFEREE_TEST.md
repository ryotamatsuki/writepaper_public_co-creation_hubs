# Hostile Referee Test

Date: 2026-09-03

| Attack | Verdict | Defense / remaining exposure |
|---|---|---|
| 1. “This is just an Ehime-specific institutional story.” | **FAILS** | Local/municipal public innovation intermediation is independently documented in Helsinki and Barcelona; paid private metropolitan innovation hubs are documented across Japan, UK, Netherlands, Poland, France and US. |
| 2. “The private metropolitan hub is merely a coworking space, not an innovation intermediary.” | **FAILS** | CIC, QWS, SENQ and Level39 explicitly bundle innovation-community, connections, mentors, investors, partner/business-creation functions with access. |
| 3. “The model requires a price that does not exist in real innovation ecosystems.” | **FAILS** | Positive current member/access prices are published by CIC Tokyo, QWS, SENQ, Level39, CIC overseas and STATION F. QUINTBRIDGE is retained as a zero-price counterexample rather than forced into the model. |
| 4. “Private hubs do not have price-setting discretion.” | **FAILS** | QWS agreement explicitly permits plan/charge revisions; CIC demonstrates effective-price discretion through current promotions; historical QUINTBRIDGE reform and other plan redesigns corroborate operator pricing margins. |
| 5. “Projects can use multiple hubs simultaneously, so single-homing is fiction.” | **SURVIVES BUT NONFATAL** | The paper must define single-homing at the focal project's primary intermediation-route level, not firm-level contractual exclusivity. Real multi-route use remains outside the minimal model. |
| 6. “Partners are not actually multi-homing.” | **SURVIVES BUT NONFATAL** | Broad non-exclusive partner categories make multi-homing plausible, but this audit does not prove simultaneous membership partner-by-partner. Claim only institutional plausibility. |
| 7. “Regional public hubs and metropolitan private hubs are complements, not substitutes.” | **SURVIVES BUT NONFATAL** | They may cooperate ecosystem-wide while still substituting for the primary route used by one focal project. The paper must make only the narrow substitution claim. |
| 8. “The operator is not a profit maximizer.” | **SURVIVES BUT NONFATAL** | CIC is privately held and commercial; Level39 is commercially operated. `max Pi_T` is a reduced-form approximation for this selected class, not a claim about QWS/Station F/public hubs universally. |
| 9. “You have no evidence that public expansion causes private fee cuts.” | **SURVIVES BUT NONFATAL** | Correct. `p^*_{T,x_i}<0` is a testable equilibrium prediction, not an institutional primitive. The audit validates existence of the actors, price, network value and price-setting margin. |
| 10. “Replacing ‘national’ with ‘metropolitan’ changes the model after the fact.” | **FAILS** | National territorial coverage never appears as a mathematical primitive. The frozen game only requires a common `H_T` accessible to both regions with network participation and a chosen `p_T`. No geography/travel-cost primitive is added. |

## Overall hostile-referee result

No surviving attack is fatal to institutional applicability. The surviving objections are explicitly scope/abstraction limitations and must remain visible in the paper. Therefore the institutional blocker is resolved without expanding the model or novelty claim.
