# Other Japan Candidates

## Blooming Camp — Osaka

Official membership: https://bloomingcamp.sakura.ad.jp/membership/

Sakura Internet's open-innovation facility has a paid `Seeds Member` tier (published at JPY 2,200/month) and presents the membership system as a mechanism through which co-creation propagates. Membership information indicates the amount may change. Community tools/events and partner recruitment support C3/C5/C6. **Overall B**; weaker than CIC on direct evidence that it is a common outside option for distant regional projects.

## The DECK — Osaka

Official pricing: https://thedeck.jp/coworking

Official corporate/open-innovation use: https://thedeck.jp/corporate-use

Published paid drop-in/monthly plans and an explicit statement that prices may be revised support C4/C5. Open-innovation/co-creation functions support C3/C6, but curated intermediation is less directly documented than CIC/QWS. **Overall B-/C**.

## JAM BASE / Syn-SALON — Osaka

Official: https://umekita.com/jambase/

Syn-SALON: https://umekita.com/jambase/facilities/syn-salon/

Syn-SALON publishes a JPY 11,000/month membership and is designed for exchange/co-creation among startups, corporate new-business actors, universities, VC and others. **Overall B-/C** because the profit-objective/operator mapping is less clean than CIC.

## FINOLAB — Tokyo

Official: https://finolab.co.jp/

FINOLAB is a member-only fintech community/space with strong intermediation/network fit. Publicly verifiable current member pricing and price discretion were not located in this audit. **Overall C**; C4/C5 unverified.

## Inspired.Lab — Tokyo

Official: https://inspiredlab.jp/

It explicitly supports new-business development and innovation-community formation. Publicly verifiable current pricing/price-discretion evidence was not strong enough for a direct analogue. **Overall C**.

## Xport — Osaka

Official member page: https://xport.osaka.jp/member/

Xport has differentiated annual fees and strong matching/mentoring functions, but its Osaka Chamber of Commerce + Osaka Institute of Technology institutional structure makes `max Pi_T` inappropriate. **Overall C/D counterexample**, not `H_T`.
