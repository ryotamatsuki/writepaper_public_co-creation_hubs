# Price Primitive Validation

## Question

Does a positive project/member-side access price corresponding to `p_T` exist in real metropolitan innovation ecosystems?

## Verdict

**ESTABLISHED for a nontrivial institutional class.**

Examples current on official pages at the 2026-09-03 audit:

- CIC Tokyo: JPY 90,000/seat/month coworking.
- SHIBUYA QWS: Project Member JPY 16,500/month plus registration fee; other paid member categories.
- SENQ: paid coworking/corporate memberships.
- Level39 London: Community from GBP 300 + VAT/month; Coworking from GBP 440 + VAT/month.
- CIC Rotterdam: current published paid coworking membership.
- CIC Warsaw: current published paid coworking membership.
- STATION F: in-house programme from EUR 259/desk/month and Landing Zone EUR 269 HT/desk/month.

These prices are often bundles of workspace plus innovation-community/network/intermediation access. The frozen price is therefore interpreted as an **effective access price to the metropolitan private innovation-hub bundle**, not a pure separately itemized matching fee.

## Boundary

Current QUINTBRIDGE membership is free after a 2023 fee reform. This contradicts the positive-price mapping for that institution and demonstrates that the model is not intended to cover every innovation hub.
