# Empirical Prediction vs Institutional Primitive

## Institutional primitives validated here

- a metropolitan private innovation hub exists;
- the strongest direct analogues charge a positive membership/access price;
- the operator has a meaningful margin to set/change plans, charges or effective prices;
- the paid bundle contains innovation-community/network/intermediation value;
- local/municipal public innovation intermediation exists in multiple countries;
- projects outside the metropolis can access at least some metropolitan hubs (directly documented for CIC Tokyo's local-area users);
- support-side actors span broad partner categories.

## Model predictions — not treated as observed facts

- `p^*_{T,x_i}<0`;
- `BR_i^{B3′}>0>BR_i^{G′}`;
- public expansion contracts private demand/network participation absent sufficient repricing;
- price flexibility can weaken/reverse public strategic interaction.

## Critical discipline

No primary evidence was located that CIC/QWS/Level39 actually reduced fees because a regional public innovation hub expanded. This is not hidden. The causal sign is the equilibrium prediction to be tested empirically.

Institutional validity would be circular if Stage 7 required the paper's own novel comparative-static result to be observed before theory construction. What must be observed are the actors, margins and feasible choices on which the prediction operates.
