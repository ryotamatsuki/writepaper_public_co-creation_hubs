Accessed 2026-09-03. STATION F publishes in-house programme prices starting around EUR 259/desk/month; its Landing Zone is published around EUR 269 HT/desk/month. The bundle includes campus resources, community, mentorship, experts and access to investor/VC channels.

https://stationf.co/about

STATION F is a major Paris startup campus created by Xavier Niel and hosts a large startup/partner ecosystem.

## Fit

C2-C4 and C6 are strong. Project-level single-homing and partner multi-homing are plausible abstractions. The main weakness is C9: the audit does not treat the campus's mission or founder ownership as proof of a pure profit-maximization objective. Price-setting exists, but the commercial objective mapping is less clean than CIC or Level39.

Use STATION F to establish breadth of the priced metropolitan innovation-campus form, not as the sole empirical anchor for `max Pi_T`.
