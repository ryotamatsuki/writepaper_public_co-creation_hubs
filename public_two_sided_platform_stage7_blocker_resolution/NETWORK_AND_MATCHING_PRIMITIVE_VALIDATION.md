# Network and Matching Primitive Validation

## Verdict

**ESTABLISHED as an institutional feature of the strongest direct analogues.**

CIC describes its product as an innovation community and connects startups with investors, corporations, professional supporters and global innovation centres. CIC Tokyo membership includes community/network access, not only physical space.

QWS explicitly describes itself as a co-creation facility rather than a normal rental office; its membership system and community functions are designed around encounters and collaboration.

SENQ identifies itself as an Open Innovation Office and explicitly offers cross-location/industry matching, alliance partners and staff support for cooperation and business creation.

Level39 says it is more than a place to work and includes events, connections, mentors, investors and specialist business support in membership.

STATION F bundles community, mentorship, experts and VC access with its paid programme/campus access.

## Economic interpretation

These facts validate the plausibility of cross-side/network value: projects value access partly because partners/supporters/investors are present, while support-side participation is more valuable in an active project ecosystem. The audit does not claim that the exact linear equation `n_h^P=rho+x_h+beta n_h^F` has been empirically estimated for any facility.
