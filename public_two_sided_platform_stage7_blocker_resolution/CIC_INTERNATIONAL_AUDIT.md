# CIC International Audit

CIC provides the cleanest evidence that the Tokyo case belongs to a recurring international institutional class rather than a Japan-specific arrangement.

Official global/coworking source: https://cic.com/office-space/coworking/

## Rotterdam

https://cic.com/rotterdam/

The current page presents coworking around EUR 395/month and describes an actively curated ecosystem of entrepreneurs, investors and corporate innovators, potential partners, and access through CIC's Global Passport.

## Warsaw

https://cic.com/warsaw/

The current page presents coworking around PLN 1,700/person/month and a community of 200+ companies with events and relationships to mentors, investors and talent.

## Cambridge / Boston

https://cic.com/cambridge/
https://cic.com/boston/

Current pages publish positive coworking prices and emphasize that the product is not merely a desk: community, events, capital and partner connections are part of membership. CIC originated in Cambridge and describes the company as privately held in its official corporate history/launch materials.

## Verdict

C1, C3, C4 and C6 are strongly established across the CIC institutional form. C2 is established/plausible because the campuses operate as large metropolitan bases and Global Passport connects the network. C5 is supported by operator-controlled plans/promotions; no claim of public-investment-triggered repricing. C9 is plausible/strong as a reduced-form commercial objective.

This cross-country recurrence materially supports institutional-class generality.
