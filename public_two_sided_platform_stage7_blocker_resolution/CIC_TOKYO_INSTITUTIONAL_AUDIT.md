# CIC Tokyo Institutional Audit

**Overall: A — strong direct analogue.**

## Evidence

Official coworking page (accessed 2026-09-03):
https://jp.cic.com/office-space/co-working/

- current coworking price: JPY 90,000 per seat per month, tax excluded;
- explicitly recommends the product to people who normally work in local areas but want a comfortable Tokyo base;
- membership is presented as more than a desk, including community access.

Official launch/company description:
https://jp.cic.com/en/news/launch-on-1-oct-2020/

- CIC describes itself as a global builder/operator of innovation communities and as privately held;
- CIC Tokyo connects startups with venture capital, government agencies and professional supporters and provides access to an innovation community/global centres.

Current price-discretion evidence:
https://jp.cic.com/news/cic-tokyo-amex-pods-room-2026/

- a 2026 promotion offered qualifying customers an effective one-month CIC Tokyo membership benefit, demonstrating managerial discretion over the effective access price. This does not establish a competitive fee response to public hubs.

## C1-C9

- C1 **ESTABLISHED**: privately held CIC group; commercial Japanese operating entities.
- C2 **ESTABLISHED**: the official product explicitly targets users normally working in local areas who need a Tokyo base.
- C3 **ESTABLISHED**: innovation community, connections to investors/professionals and global network.
- C4 **ESTABLISHED**: positive monthly membership price.
- C5 **ESTABLISHED/PLAUSIBLE**: promotional price discretion is observed; no claim of continuous best-response pricing.
- C6 **ESTABLISHED**: participation/community/network is part of the product proposition.
- C7 **PLAUSIBLE**: one focal project can be modeled as choosing a primary intermediation route; no legal exclusivity is claimed.
- C8 **PLAUSIBLE**: investors, professionals, corporates, universities and government groups can participate across ecosystems; universal literal multihoming is not documented.
- C9 **PLAUSIBLE**: privately held commercial membership business makes `max Pi_T` a defensible reduced-form objective, not a documented literal corporate FOC.

## Caveat

CIC also works with public agencies and can run publicly commissioned programs. Thus regional public and metropolitan private institutions may be complements ecosystem-wide. The model uses substitution only for the focal project's primary route.
