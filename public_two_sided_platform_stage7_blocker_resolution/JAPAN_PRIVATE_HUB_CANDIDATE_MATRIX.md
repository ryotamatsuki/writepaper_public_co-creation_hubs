## Ranking logic

CIC Tokyo is primary because it combines a privately held/commercial operator class, explicit current paid membership, innovation-community/network access, explicit local-area-to-Tokyo customer positioning, and global-hub access. QWS is unusually strong on C5 because its membership agreement explicitly permits plan and charge revisions. SENQ corroborates that paid open-innovation-office services are not unique to one operator.

QUINTBRIDGE is deliberately retained as a counterexample: a strong innovation network can choose a zero membership price. It must not be forced into the canonical positive-`p_T` application.
