# Helsinki Public Intermediation Case

Primary source, accessed 2026-09-03:
https://www.hel.fi/en/business-and-work/campus-incubators-programme

## Institutional facts

The City of Helsinki states that the Campus Incubators Programme aims to increase startups, support RDI-based entrepreneurship and strengthen university campuses as innovation/new-business hubs. Participants receive access to workspaces, tools and events. The city focuses on linking incubators with the broader innovation ecosystem and relevant partners.

For the 2021–2024 phase, the city explicitly describes a market gap: early-stage incubation supporting business creation/renewal would not arise sufficiently without public support. Helsinki therefore took an active role as **builder and funder** of regional pre-incubation and incubation. The programme operated across multiple university campuses and produced substantial participant/startup activity. The 2025–2027 model remains jointly funded by the city and universities.
