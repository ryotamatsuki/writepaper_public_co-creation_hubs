# Original Blocker and Resolution Test

## Original blocker

Stage 7 ended at `CONDITIONAL GO — ONE SPECIFIC BLOCKER`: no exact shared **national** private fee-setting innovation intermediary had been identified for the motivating co-creation-hub environment.

That formulation mixed a mathematical primitive with an institutional label. The frozen game requires one private hub `H_T` that is accessible to projects from both regional public hubs, supplies network/intermediation value, and chooses `p_T`. It does not require `H_T` to have nationwide geographic coverage.

## Resolution test

The blocker is resolved only if all six conditions hold:

1. at least one strong Japanese metropolitan private analogue;
2. private operator, shared metropolitan outside option, innovation intermediation, positive member/project-side price, price discretion, and network value are established or strongly plausible;
3. focal-project single-homing and support-side multi-homing are defensible without firm-level exclusivity;
4. at least two credible overseas private metropolitan analogues;
5. overseas local/municipal public innovation intermediation exists;
6. replacing `national` with `metropolitan` requires no mathematical modification.

## Results

- Japan: **PASS** — CIC Tokyo is the strongest direct analogue; QWS independently validates paid co-creation membership and explicit fee-revision authority; SENQ supports the class.
- Overseas private side: **PASS** — Level39 London plus CIC Rotterdam/Warsaw/Cambridge/Boston; STATION F provides additional supporting evidence.
- Overseas public side: **PASS** — Helsinki and Barcelona.
- Single-homing: **PASS AS ABSTRACTION**, not as exclusivity fact.
- Partner multi-homing: **PASS AS PLAUSIBLE/NON-EXCLUSIVE INSTITUTIONAL INTERPRETATION**, not a universal observed fact.
- National→metropolitan: **PASS — INTERPRETATION REFINEMENT ONLY**.

## Exact remaining non-blockers

Observed `public expansion -> private fee cut` evidence was not located. This is correctly retained as an empirical prediction. Pure profit maximization is a reduced-form approximation for commercial private operators; it is not attributed to public, nonprofit, university-led, or zero-price boundary cases.

Final result: **BLOCKER RESOLVED**.
