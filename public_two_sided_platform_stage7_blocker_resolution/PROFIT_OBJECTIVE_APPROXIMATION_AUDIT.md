# Profit Objective Approximation Audit

The frozen private hub chooses `p_T` to maximize `Pi_T`. No official operator is expected to publish the literal first-order condition used in the theory. The relevant question is whether a profit-oriented reduced-form objective is defensible.

| Institution | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|

| Helsinki / Barcelona public programmes | **CONTRADICTED as `H_T`** | public/municipal objectives |

## Verdict

CIC is sufficient to defend `max Pi_T` as a reduced-form objective for the primary institutional analogue. The paper must not generalize that objective to all co-creation hubs. “Private operator” is not treated as proof of pure profit maximization; the claim is an economically standard abstraction for the selected commercial class.
