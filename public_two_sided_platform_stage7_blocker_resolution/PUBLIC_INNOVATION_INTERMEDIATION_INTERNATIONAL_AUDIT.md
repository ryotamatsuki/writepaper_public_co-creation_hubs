# Public Innovation Intermediation — International Audit

The private-side evidence would not establish generality if the public `G_i,H_i,x_i` objects were Japan-specific. Two independent municipal cases pass the public-side test.

## Helsinki

The City of Helsinki's Campus Incubators Programme is jointly funded by the city and universities, strengthens campus innovation/business hubs, connects incubators to the innovation ecosystem and relevant partners, and provides workspace, events and entrepreneurship support. The city explicitly explains that the earlier programme addressed a market gap and that Helsinki took an active role as builder and funder of regional pre-incubation/incubation operations.

Primary source: https://www.hel.fi/en/business-and-work/campus-incubators-programme

## Barcelona

Barcelona Activa's Startup Lab provides a municipal innovation/incubation environment for technology/deep-tech startups with specialized business support, mentoring, cooperation networks and direct ecosystem connections. 2026 Barcelona Activa materials emphasize strengthening ties and synergies among projects in the city's innovation ecosystem.

Primary sources:
https://www.barcelonactiva.cat/en/-/incubadora-glories
https://emprenedoria.barcelonactiva.cat/en/startup-lab

## Verdict

**ESTABLISHED:** local/municipal governments outside Japan actively resource innovation-intermediation capacity. The precise legal form and programme mix differ across cities, but no new theoretical primitive is needed to map the public investment margin to facilitation/network-building capacity.
