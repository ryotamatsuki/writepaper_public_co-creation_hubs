# Price Discretion Evidence

## Strongest direct evidence — SHIBUYA QWS

Official membership agreement:
https://shibuya-qws.com/membership/agreement/

Article 7(4) permits the facility to revise/abolish membership plans and change charges when operationally necessary or due to changing economic conditions. **C5: ESTABLISHED.**

## CIC Tokyo

Official 2026 promotion:
https://jp.cic.com/news/cic-tokyo-amex-pods-room-2026/

A one-month membership-equivalent benefit for qualifying customers demonstrates that the operator can change the effective access price through commercial promotions. **C5: ESTABLISHED/PLAUSIBLE.** This is not evidence of a public-investment best response.

## QUINTBRIDGE boundary

Official 2023 notice:
https://www.quintbridge.jp/news/detail/202303310000.html

The operator changed a JPY 220,000/year category to free and redesigned membership categories. This establishes institutional price redesign but also removes QUINTBRIDGE from the current positive-`p_T` direct-analogue set.

## Other commercial hubs

The DECK publicly reserves price-revision possibility; multiple private hubs publish and redesign membership plans. These support the existence of price-setting margins at the institutional-class level.

## What is not established

No primary source was located showing `regional public facilitation investment -> private metropolitan fee reduction`. That sign remains the model prediction `p^*_{T,x_i}<0`.
