# Partner Multi-Homing Institutional Defense

## Verdict

**PLAUSIBLE / STRONGLY SUGGESTIVE; NOT UNIVERSALLY ESTABLISHED AS OBSERVED SIMULTANEOUS MULTI-HOMING.**

The partner side in observed innovation ecosystems includes VCs, universities, experts, law/accounting professionals, mentors, corporations, accelerators and public agencies. Official CIC, QWS, SENQ, Level39, Helsinki and Barcelona materials describe broad ecosystems and partner connections without an institutional rule implying that these actors must belong to exactly one hub.

That non-exclusive institutional architecture makes support-side multi-homing a natural reduced-form assumption. However, this audit does not assemble partner-by-partner membership histories proving simultaneous participation across every pair of hubs.

The correct paper language is therefore:

> support-side actors can participate across multiple innovation ecosystems, so multi-homing is institutionally plausible.

Do not write:

> all partners in these facilities are empirically observed to multi-home.

This remaining evidentiary limitation is nonfatal because the model needs the institutional possibility/logic of multi-homing, not a universal empirical frequency claim.
